# The Solar System

## Our Cosmic Neighbourhood!

The **Solar System** is our home in space! It consists of the **Sun** at the centre, **8 planets**, dozens of **moons**, millions of **asteroids**, and thousands of **comets** — all held together by the invisible force of **gravity**! 🌠

---

## What Is the Solar System?

The Solar System formed approximately **4.6 billion years ago**. Everything orbits the Sun:

| Component | Number | Examples |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| ⭐ Stars | 1 | The Sun |
| 🪐 Planets | 8 | Earth, Mars, Jupiter... |
| 🌙 Known moons | 200+ | Moon, Ganymede, Titan |
| 🪨 Asteroids | Millions | Asteroid Belt |
| ☄️ Comets | Thousands | Halley, Hale-Bopp |
| 🔵 Dwarf planets | 5+ | Pluto, Eris, Ceres |

## The Sun — King of the Solar System

The Sun is a **medium-sized star** — an enormous ball of gas undergoing nuclear fusion!

**Mind-blowing facts:**
- 🔥 Core temperature: ~15,000,000 °C
- 🔥 Surface temperature: ~5,500 °C
- 📏 Diameter: 1,392,000 km (109 × the Earth!)
- ⚖️ Mass: 99.8% of all the mass in the Solar System!
- ⏱️ Sunlight takes 8 minutes to reach Earth
- 🌟 Star type: Yellow dwarf (class G)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Without the Sun, Earth would be a frozen, lifeless planet. Solar energy powers almost all life on Earth — through photosynthesis in plants! 🌱

## The 8 Planets

Planets are split into two groups:

**Rocky planets** (close to the Sun, small, solid):
Mercury ⚫ · Venus 🟡 · Earth 🔵 · Mars 🔴

**Gas giants** (far from the Sun, enormous, gaseous):
Jupiter 🟠 · Saturn 💛 · Uranus 🔵 · Neptune 💙

## The Moon and Other Moons

Many planets have moons (natural satellites) orbiting them!

| Planet | Number of moons | Main moon |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| 🔵 Earth | 1 | Moon 🌕 |
| 🔴 Mars | 2 | Phobos, Deimos |
| 🟠 Jupiter | 95 | Ganymede, Europa, Io |
| 💛 Saturn | 146 | Titan (bigger than Mercury!) |
| 🔵 Uranus | 28 | Titania, Oberon |
| 💙 Neptune | 16 | Triton |

**Our Moon 🌕**
- Diameter: 3,474 km (¼ of Earth's)
- Distance from Earth: ~384,400 km
- Time to orbit Earth: 29 days
- Astronauts **Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin** walked on the Moon in **1969**! 👨‍🚀

> [!NOTE]
> **Ganymede** (Jupiter's moon) is actually larger than the planet Mercury! And **Europa** (also Jupiter's) may have a liquid ocean under its icy crust — possibly harbouring life! 🌊

## Asteroids and Comets

**Asteroids 🪨**
- Rocky fragments orbiting the Sun
- Most are in the **Asteroid Belt** (between Mars and Jupiter)
- When they enter the atmosphere and burn → **meteors** (shooting stars! ⭐)
- If they reach the ground → **meteorites**

**Comets ☄️**
- Balls of rock, ice and dust with very long oval orbits
- Near the Sun they develop a bright **tail** visible from Earth
- **Halley's Comet** visits the Sun every 75 years — next time in 2061!

## The Milky Way

The Solar System is part of a galaxy called the **Milky Way**:

- 🌟 Contains approximately **200–400 billion stars**
- 🌌 Spiral shape (like a giant cosmic whirlpool!)
- ☀️ The Sun is about 26,000 light-years from the centre
- ⏱️ The Sun takes 225 million years to orbit the galaxy!

> [!NOTE]
> When you look at the sky on a very dark night, the faint whitish band you see is the **Milky Way** — millions of stars so far away they look like a misty glow! What a wonder! 🌠
