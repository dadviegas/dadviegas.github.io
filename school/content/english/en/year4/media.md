# Media and Technology

## Introduction

Media and technology shape modern life. Being able to discuss devices, platforms, and digital habits in English is increasingly important — and you probably already know many of these words!

> Many technology words are used in Portuguese too, often unchanged: *internet, email, app, selfie, podcast, tweet, blog*. These are called **loanwords**.

### Exercise 2 — Fill in the gap

1. You type words using a ________.
2. You use a ________ to go to websites.
3. A ________ is a person's identity online.
4. ________ is when you read someone's private data.

### Exercise 3 — My digital life

Answer in full sentences:

1. How many hours a day do you spend online?
2. What apps do you use most?
3. What do you use your device for at school?
4. What are the risks of spending too much time on screens?

### Exercise 4 — For and against

Write 3 advantages and 3 disadvantages of social media for young people.

> [!TIP]
> Technology vocabulary changes fast! New words like *selfie*, *hashtag*, *influencer*, and *vlog* were not in dictionaries 20 years ago. Keep up with new words!
