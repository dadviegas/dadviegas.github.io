# Vocabulary — Year 1

## Everyday Words

Learning vocabulary in Portuguese is like collecting treasures — the more words you know, the better you can communicate!

> [!TIP]
> When you learn a new Portuguese word, try to use it in a sentence. That way you won't forget it!

---

## Lexical Field: School (A Escola)

| Portuguese | English |
|------------|---------|
| *a escola* | the school |
| *a sala de aula* | the classroom |
| *o quadro* | the board |
| *o caderno* | the notebook |
| *o lápis* | the pencil |
| *a mochila* | the school bag |
| *a professora* | the teacher (f.) |
| *o aluno / a aluna* | the pupil |
| *o recreio* | the playground |

> [!NOTE]
> Notice that every noun has **o** (masculine) or **a** (feminine) before it — this is the word for "the" in Portuguese!

## Lexical Field: The Body (O Corpo)

| Portuguese | English | Portuguese | English |
|------------|---------|------------|---------|
| *a cabeça* | head | *o braço* | arm |
| *os olhos* | eyes | *a mão* | hand |
| *o nariz* | nose | *o dedo* | finger |
| *a boca* | mouth | *a perna* | leg |
| *a orelha* | ear | *o pé* | foot |

---

## Lexical Field: Colours (As Cores)

| Portuguese | English | Portuguese | English |
|------------|---------|------------|---------|
| *vermelho* | red | *verde* | green |
| *azul* | blue | *cor-de-laranja* | orange |
| *amarelo* | yellow | *cor-de-rosa* | pink |
| *branco* | white | *castanho* | brown |
| *preto* | black | *roxo* | purple |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In Portuguese, colours are **adjectives** and must agree with the noun:
> *o balão vermelho* (the red balloon — masculine) / *a bola vermelha* (the red ball — feminine)

---

## Simple Word Families (Família de Palavras)

Words in the same family share the same root:

| Root | Family |
|------|--------|
| *flor* (flower) | *flor*, *floresta* (forest), *florido* (flowery) |
| *mar* (sea) | *mar*, *marinha* (navy), *marinheiro* (sailor) |

## Try it! 🎯

1. **Translate into Portuguese**: pencil, teacher, playground, school bag, notebook.
2. **Colour the sentence correctly**: *O céu é* \_\_\_\_ *(azul/verde). A relva é* \_\_\_\_ *(azul/verde).*
3. **Body quiz**: point to each body part and say it in Portuguese — *olhos*, *nariz*, *boca*, *orelha*, *mão*, *pé*.
4. **Family connections**: who is your *avó*? Your *tio*? Draw your family tree with Portuguese labels.
