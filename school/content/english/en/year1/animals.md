# Animals

## Introduction

Animals are fascinating! Whether they live on a farm, in the wild, or in your home, knowing their names in English is very useful. Let's meet 10 common animals.

> The word **pet** refers to an animal you keep at home — like a dog, cat, rabbit, or fish. A **farm animal** lives on a farm — like a cow, pig, horse, or duck.

| English | Portuguese |
|---------|-----------|

## Animal Descriptions

| Animal | Adjective | English sentence |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| dog | friendly | Dogs are friendly. |
| cat | independent | Cats are independent. |
| bird | small | Birds are small. |
| horse | big | Horses are big. |
| rabbit | soft | Rabbits are soft. |

> [!TIP]
> To say what an animal looks like or can do, use: **A [animal] is [adjective]** or **A [animal] can [verb]**.
> - A fish can swim.
> - A bird can fly.
> - A dog can run.

---

## Plural Forms

In English, most animal names add **-s** in the plural:

| Singular | Plural |
|----------|--------|
| one dog | two dog**s** |
| one cat | three cat**s** |
| one bird | four bird**s** |
| one fish | five fish *(no change!)* |
| one cow | six cow**s** |
