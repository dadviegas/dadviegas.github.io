# Nature

## Introduction

Nature surrounds us! From mountains and rivers to trees and insects, the natural world has rich vocabulary in English. This lesson covers landscapes, plants, animals, and environmental features.

### Exercise 2 — Fill in the gaps

1. A ________ is a large body of salt water.
2. A ________ is a large area of trees.
3. An ________ is land surrounded by water.
4. A ________ is a bird of prey with sharp eyes.

### Exercise 3 — Describe a place

Choose one of these places and write 5 sentences describing what you might see there:

- A tropical beach
- A mountain forest
- An African savanna

### Exercise 4 — Nature poem

Write a short poem (4–6 lines) about your favorite element of nature (a river, a forest, the sea, etc.).

> Next time you go outside, try to name 10 things you see in nature — in English! It could be clouds, trees, birds, rocks...
