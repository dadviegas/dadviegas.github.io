## Cause and Effect Language

| Cause | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Burning fossil fuels | increases greenhouse gases |
| Deforestation | destroys habitats / causes flooding |
| Plastic pollution | harms marine life |
| Global warming | melts glaciers / raises sea levels |

Useful connectors:
- **because / because of** → Glaciers melt **because of** global warming.
- **as a result** → Forests are cut down. **As a result**, animals lose their homes.
- **therefore** → We use fossil fuels; **therefore**, CO₂ levels rise.
- **however** → Pollution is serious. **However**, there are solutions.
