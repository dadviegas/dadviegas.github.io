# Addition

**Adding** means **putting together** or **combining** groups. The result is called the **sum** or **total**.

$$\text{addend} + \text{addend} = \text{sum}$$

---

## What is addition?

Imagine you have **3 apples** and your mum gives you **4 more apples**.
How many apples do you have altogether?

$$3 + 4 = 7$$

You have **7 apples** altogether. 🍎🍎🍎 + 🍎🍎🍎🍎 = 🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎

---

## Addition on the number line

To calculate $5 + 3$, start at **5** and take **3 jumps** forward:

$$5 \xrightarrow{+1} 6 \xrightarrow{+1} 7 \xrightarrow{+1} 8$$

Therefore: $5 + 3 = 8$

---

## Column addition (vertical)

For larger sums, we line up numbers in a column:

```
  6
+ 7
---
 13
```

> [!TIP]
> Always align **ones** under **ones**.

---

## Properties of addition

**Commutative property** — the order of addends does not change the sum:

$$4 + 6 = 6 + 4 = 10$$

**Identity element** — adding 0 does not change a number:

$$9 + 0 = 9$$
