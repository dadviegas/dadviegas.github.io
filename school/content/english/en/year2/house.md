## Describing Where Things Are

Use prepositions of place:

| Preposition | Example |
|-------------|---------|
| **in** | The TV is **in** the living room. |
| **on** | The book is **on** the shelf. |
| **under** | The cat is **under** the bed. |
| **next to** | The lamp is **next to** the sofa. |
| **behind** | The garden is **behind** the house. |
| **in front of** | The sofa is **in front of** the TV. |
| **between** | The table is **between** the chairs. |

> [!TIP]
> When describing your home, say which room something is in **first**: *"In the kitchen, there is a fridge."*

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Which room?

In which room do you usually find these things?

1. fridge → ________
2. sofa → ________
3. bed → ________
4. bath → ________
5. garden table → ________

### Exercise 2 — Prepositions

Complete the sentences with the correct preposition:

1. The cat is ________ the bed. (under / on / in)
2. The lamp is ________ the sofa. (next to / behind / between)
3. The book is ________ the shelf. (in front of / on / under)

### Exercise 3 — Describe your bedroom

Write 6 sentences describing your bedroom. Include:
- What furniture is there
- Where each piece of furniture is
- What is on / under / next to each piece of furniture

### Exercise 4 — Dream house

Describe your dream house in 5–8 sentences. What rooms does it have? What is in each room?

> [!TIP]
> Walk around your home and name every object in English. Start with one room a day — by the end of the week you'll know them all!
