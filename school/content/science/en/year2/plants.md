# Plants

Plants are living things that make their own food using sunlight. They are essential for life on Earth!

---

## Parts of a Plant

Each part of a plant has an important job.

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Root | Absorbs water and minerals from the soil; anchors the plant |
| Stem | Transports water and nutrients; supports the plant |
| Leaf | Produces food using sunlight |
| Flower | Reproduction — produces seeds |
| Fruit | Protects the seeds and helps spread them |
| Seed | Can grow into a new plant |

> [!TIP]
> When you eat an apple, you are eating the **fruit**! Inside are the seeds that could grow into a new apple tree.

---

## Flowering and Non-Flowering Plants

Not all plants have flowers!

**Flowering plants** (angiosperms):
- Have flowers that produce fruits and seeds
- Examples: rose bush, orange tree, sunflower, wheat

**Non-flowering plants**:
- Reproduce using spores or other methods
- Ferns — live in damp, shady places
- Mosses — small green plants on rocks and tree trunks
- Pine trees — produce cones instead of flowers

> [!NOTE]
> The pine tree is one of the most common trees in Portugal. Its seeds are found inside **pine cones**!

---

## What Plants Need to Grow

Plants need four essential things:

1. **Sunlight** — to make their own food
2. **Water** — to carry nutrients around the plant
3. **Soil** — to anchor themselves and absorb minerals
4. **Air** — to breathe and carry out photosynthesis

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Without sun, water and soil, plants cannot survive! That is why we must take care of the environment around us.

---

## Experiment: Growing a Plant

You can grow your own plant at home!

1. Put compost or soil in a pot
2. Bury a seed (a bean works well)
3. Water with a small amount of water
4. Place near a sunny window
5. Watch what happens over the coming days

---

## Try it!

1. **Identify the parts**: look at a plant at home or at school and point to each part (root, stem, leaf, flower or fruit).
2. **Grow a bean**: follow the steps above and keep a diary with drawings showing how the plant grows each day.
3. **Flowering or not**: find two examples of each type of plant in a nearby garden or park.
4. **Light experiment**: put a plant in a dark place for 3 days. What happens to the leaves?
