# Economic Activities

## How People Work to Produce Things!

Every day, people all over the world work to create goods (things you can hold, like food or clothes) and services (things people do for you, like teaching or healthcare). All this work together makes up a country's **economy**! 🌍

---

## The Three Economic Sectors

Economic activities are divided into **three sectors**, depending on the type of work:

| Sector | What it does | Examples |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 🌾 **Primary** | Extracts resources from nature | Farming, fishing, mining, forestry |
| 🏭 **Secondary** | Transforms raw materials into products | Industry, construction, crafts |
| 🏪 **Tertiary** | Provides services | Trade, tourism, health, education |

> [!TIP]
> Easy way to remember: **Primary** = take from nature / **Secondary** = transform / **Tertiary** = serve people! 💡

---

## Primary Sector — Straight from Nature

### Farming
Portugal has many famous agricultural products!

| Product | Main Region |
|---------|------------|
| 🍊 Orange | Algarve, Ribatejo |
| 🍇 Grapes (wine) | Douro, Alentejo, Vinho Verde |
| 🫒 Olive (olive oil) | Alentejo, Trás-os-Montes |
| 🌾 Rice | Ribatejo, Sado |
| 🍅 Tomato | Ribatejo, Alentejo |
| 🍓 Strawberry | Ribatejo, Algarve |

### Livestock
Raising animals for meat, milk, wool, eggs...

- 🐄 **Cattle** — meat and milk (North and Centre)
- 🐑 **Sheep** — wool and cheese (Alentejo, Beira)
- 🐖 **Pigs** — meat (Alentejo — famous presunto ham!)
- 🐔 **Poultry** — eggs and chicken

### Fishing
Portugal has a long Atlantic coast — fishing is very important!

- 🐟 **Sardine** — the most famous Portuguese fish!
- 🐙 **Octopus and squid**
- 🐠 **Tuna** — caught in the Algarve and Azores
- 🦐 **Shellfish and seafood**

### Forestry and Mining
- 🌲 **Cork** — Portugal is the world's largest cork producer!
- 🪨 **Granite and marble** — exported worldwide

> [!NOTE]
> Portugal exports **cork** to more than 100 countries! Most wine corks in the world are made from Portuguese cork. 🍾

## Secondary Sector — Transforming Raw Materials

Industry transforms **raw materials** into **finished products**:

| Raw Material | Finished Product | Industry |
|-------------|----------------|---------|
| 🌾 Wheat | 🍞 Bread | Food |
| 🪨 Granite | 🏗️ Paving stone | Quarrying |
| 🐑 Wool | 🧥 Cloth | Textile |
| 🌲 Wood | 🪑 Furniture | Timber |
| ⛽ Oil | 🧴 Plastic | Chemical |
| 🫒 Olives | 🫙 Olive oil | Food |

### Important industries in Portugal 🇵🇹
- 👔 **Textile and clothing** — North (Braga, Porto)
- 🪵 **Paper and cork** — Centre (Aveiro, Setúbal)
- 🍷 **Food and drink** — all over the country
- 🚗 **Cars** — Setúbal (Autoeuropa factory!)
- 🏗️ **Construction** — all over the country

---

## Tertiary Sector — Services

The tertiary sector is the largest in Portugal and includes all **services** provided to people:

| Service | Examples |
|---------|---------|
| 🛍️ Trade | Shops, supermarkets, markets |
| 🏖️ Tourism | Hotels, restaurants, travel agencies |
| 🏥 Healthcare | Hospitals, clinics, pharmacies |
| 🎓 Education | Schools, universities, training centres |
| 🚌 Transport | Buses, trains, aeroplanes |
| 💰 Finance | Banks, insurance |
| 📱 Communications | Mobile phones, internet, television |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tourism** is one of the most important economic activities in Portugal. Millions of visitors come to our country every year, attracted by the climate, beaches, gastronomy and history! 🌍✈️

---

## Raw Materials vs Transformed Products

```
RAW MATERIAL → INDUSTRY → FINISHED PRODUCT

🫒 Olives → 🏭 Press → 🫙 Olive oil
🌾 Wheat → 🏭 Mill/Bakery → 🍞 Bread
🌲 Wood → 🏭 Sawmill → 🪑 Furniture
🐄 Milk → 🏭 Factory → 🧀 Cheese
🍇 Grapes → 🏭 Winery → 🍷 Wine
```

## Portugal's Economy 🇵🇹

Portugal belongs to the **European Union** and uses the **euro** (€) as its currency. The country's main wealth comes from:

- 🌞 **Climate and scenery** (tourism)
- 🌊 **Sea and fishing**
- 🫒 **Mediterranean agriculture**
- 🪨 **Mineral resources** (marble, granite, lithium)
- 🌲 **Forests** (timber, cork)
- ⚡ **Renewable energy** (wind, solar, hydroelectric)
