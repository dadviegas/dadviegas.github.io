# Animals

There are millions of animal species on our planet. Scientists organise them into groups to study them more easily!

---

## Classification: Vertebrates and Invertebrates

The first major division of animals is whether or not they have a backbone.

| Group | Characteristic | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Vertebrates** | Have a backbone (internal skeleton) | Dog, sardine, pigeon, frog, snake |
| **Invertebrates** | Do not have a backbone | Butterfly, octopus, earthworm, crab |

**Vertebrates are divided into 5 classes:**

| Class | Body covering | Breathing | Reproduction | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mammals | Fur or hair | Lungs | Live young (viviparous) | Whale, dog, human |
| Birds | Feathers | Lungs | Eggs (oviparous) | Pigeon, stork, penguin |
| Reptiles | Dry scales | Lungs | Eggs (oviparous) | Lizard, snake, crocodile |
| Amphibians | Damp bare skin | Lungs and skin | Eggs (in water) | Frog, salamander |
| Fish | Scales | Gills | Eggs (mostly) | Cod, sardine, shark |

> [!NOTE]
> Humans are **mammals**! Just like dogs, cats and dolphins — all mammals.

## Animal Adaptations

Animals have features that help them survive in their habitat.

| Animal | Adaptation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Polar bear | Thick white fur | Withstand cold; camouflage in snow |
| Camel | Hump storing fat | Energy reserve in the desert |
| Bat | Echolocation | Navigate and hunt in the dark |
| Octopus | Ink and mimicry | Escape from predators |
| Swordfish | Streamlined body | Swim at great speed |

> [!TIP]
> Adaptations take thousands of years to develop. They are the result of **evolution** — the animals best suited to their environment survive and have offspring!

## Food Chains

A **food chain** shows who eats whom in nature.

```
Plants (producers)
      ↓
Herbivores (1st-level consumers)
      ↓
Carnivores (2nd-level consumers)
      ↓
Top predators (3rd-level consumers)
```

**Example in a Portuguese forest:**
> Grass → Rabbit → Fox → Golden eagle

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If one link in a food chain disappears, the whole chain is affected! That is why it is important to protect all species.
