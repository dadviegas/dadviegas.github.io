## Uppercase and Lowercase Letters

The alphabet has two forms for each letter: **uppercase** (big) and **lowercase** (small).

| Uppercase | Lowercase | Uppercase | Lowercase |
|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|
| A | a | N | n |
| B | b | O | o |
| C | c | P | p |
| D | d | Q | q |
| E | e | R | r |
| F | f | S | s |
| G | g | T | t |
| H | h | U | u |
| I | i | V | v |
| J | j | W | w |
| L | l | X | x |
| M | m | Y | y |
| | | Z | z |

## When Do We Use Uppercase?

> [!IMPORTANT]
> We don't always write with an uppercase letter! There are rules for this.

### 1. At the beginning of a sentence

Every time you start a new sentence, the first letter is **uppercase**.

- **T**he cat sleeps on the chair.
- **M**aria went to school.
- **T**oday it's sunny! ☀️

### 2. For proper nouns

The names of **people**, **cities**, **countries** and **pets** are written with an uppercase letter.

| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| People's names | Ana, John, Sofia, Peter |
| City names | Lisbon, London, Paris |
| Country names | Portugal, Brazil, France |
| Pet names | Fluffy, Rex, Mimi 🐱 |

> [!TIP]
> Easy trick: if you can replace the name with "he" or "she", it's a proper noun and needs an uppercase letter!

## Spaces Between Words

Words need **spaces** between them so we can read them! Without spaces, everything gets confusing.

❌ **Wrong:** Thecatsleepsonthepillow.

✅ **Correct:** The cat sleeps on the pillow.

> [!NOTE]
> Golden rule: between each word there is always **one** space — not more, not less!

## Basic Accents in Portuguese

Portuguese uses accent marks that change how letters sound and the meaning of words. They are very important!

### The acute accent (´)

Makes the vowel sound **open and strong**.

| With accent | Example | Without accent |
|:-----------:|---------|:--------------:|
| **á** | pá (spade) 🪣 | a |
| **é** | pé (foot) 🦶 | e |
| **í** | aqui (here) | i |
| **ó** | só (only) | o |
| **ú** | azul (blue) | u |

### The tilde (~)

Gives a **nasal** sound to the vowel — like air coming through your nose! 👃

| Letter | Example | Image |
|:------:|---------|-------|
| **ã** | maçã (apple) | 🍎 |
| **õ** | limões (lemons) | 🍋 |

### The grave accent (`)

The grave accent appears only on **à** and shows that two words have joined together.

- Vou **à** escola. (= going to the school)

### The circumflex accent (^)

Makes the vowel sound **closed**.

| Letter | Example |
|:------:|---------|
| **â** | câmara (camera) |
| **ê** | você (you) |
| **ô** | avô (grandfather) 👴 |

> [!TIP]
> When you're unsure about accents, read the word out loud. Where your voice rises the most, there's usually an accent mark!
