# Grammar — Year 2

## Nouns (Nomes)

A **noun** (*nome*) names a person, animal, thing, place, or idea.

| Type | Portuguese | English |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Common noun | *caderno*, *cidade* | notebook, city |
| Proper noun | *Pedro*, *Lisboa* | Pedro, Lisbon |

> [!NOTE]
> Proper nouns (*nomes próprios*) always begin with a **capital letter** in Portuguese, just like in English.

---

## Adjectives (Adjetivos)

An **adjective** (*adjetivo*) describes a noun — it tells us what something is like.

| Noun | Adjective | Portuguese phrase | English |
|------|-----------|------------------|---------|
| *casa* | *grande* | *a casa grande* | the big house |
| *céu* | *azul* | *o céu azul* | the blue sky |
| *menino* | *simpático* | *o menino simpático* | the nice boy |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In Portuguese, adjectives must **agree** with the noun in gender and number:
> *livro novo* (new book, m. sg.) → *livros novos* (m. pl.)
> *flor bonita* (pretty flower, f. sg.) → *flores bonitas* (f. pl.)

---

## Articles (Artigos)

The **article** (*artigo*) comes before the noun.

| | Singular | Plural | English |
|-|----------|--------|---------|
| **Definite** (*definido*) | *o* / *a* | *os* / *as* | the |
| **Indefinite** (*indefinido*) | *um* / *uma* | *uns* / *umas* | a / an / some |

Examples:
- *o gato* — the cat (specific cat)
- *um gato* — a cat (any cat)

> [!TIP]
> Use the **definite article** (*o/a*) when you are talking about something specific or already known.
> Use the **indefinite article** (*um/uma*) when introducing something for the first time.

---

## Verbs in the Present Tense (Presente do Indicativo)

The **verb** (*verbo*) expresses an action, state, or event.

Portuguese verbs change their ending depending on who is doing the action:

| Pronoun | *cantar* (to sing) | *comer* (to eat) | English |
|---------|--------------------|------------------|---------|
| *eu* | *canto* | *como* | I sing / eat |
| *tu* | *cantas* | *comes* | you sing / eat |
| *ele/ela* | *canta* | *come* | he/she sings / eats |
| *nós* | *cantamos* | *comemos* | we sing / eat |
| *eles/elas* | *cantam* | *comem* | they sing / eat |

> [!NOTE]
> In Portuguese, you don't always need the pronoun — the verb ending already tells you who is acting: *canto* = I sing.

---

## Subject and Predicate

| Part | Portuguese | Role | Example |
|------|------------|------|---------|
| Subject | *sujeito* | who does the action | *A Marta* |
| Predicate | *predicado* | what happens | *come uma maçã* |

Full sentence: *A Marta come uma maçã.* (Marta eats an apple.)
