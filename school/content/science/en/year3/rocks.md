# Rocks & Soil

## Rocks Are Everywhere!

Look around you — rocks are found in mountains, on beaches, in buildings, and even under your feet! Rocks are natural solid materials made of minerals. Scientists who study rocks are called **geologists**! 🔬

---

## What Are Rocks?

Rocks are solid natural materials that form the Earth's crust. There are three main types, depending on how they were **formed**:

| Type | How they form | Examples |
|------|--------------|---------|
| 🌋 **Igneous** | From cooling magma | Granite, basalt |
| 💧 **Sedimentary** | From layers of sediment | Limestone, sandstone, clay |
| 🔥 **Metamorphic** | From heat and pressure | Marble, slate, schist |

---

## Igneous Rocks (or Volcanic Rocks)

These form when **magma** (melted rock) cools down and becomes solid.

- **Intrusive** — cool slowly deep inside the Earth → large crystals
- **Extrusive** — cool quickly at the surface (volcanoes!) → small crystals

### Examples:

🔵 **Granite**
- Colour: grey, pink or white with "speckles"
- Has visible crystals of quartz, feldspar and mica
- Widely used in construction (pavements, monuments!)
- Abundant in northern Portugal and Serra da Estrela

⚫ **Basalt**
- Colour: dark, almost black
- Forms during volcanic eruptions
- Very common in the Azores and Madeira 🌋

> [!NOTE]
> The famous Portuguese **cobblestone pavements** ("calçada portuguesa") are made from **granite** or **basalt**! This hand-laid stonework is a Cultural Heritage of Portugal. 🇵🇹

---

## Sedimentary Rocks

These form by the **accumulation and compaction** of sediments (sand, clay, shells of animals...) over millions of years.

⬜ **Limestone**
- Colour: white or beige
- Forms from shells and skeletons of sea creatures
- Widely used in construction (walls, lime, cement)
- Abundant in the Algarve and around Óbidos

🟤 **Sandstone**
- Compacted grains of sand
- Colour: yellow, orange or red
- Used in construction

🟡 **Clay**
- Very fine particles
- When wet it can be moulded → used in pottery and ceramics!

> [!TIP]
> Fossils 🦕 are found almost always in **sedimentary rocks**! Animals were covered by layers of sediment that preserved them for millions of years.

---

## Metamorphic Rocks

These form when other rocks are subjected to **extreme heat and pressure**, transforming them into a new rock.

⬜ **Marble**
- Formed from limestone
- Colour: white, pink or with coloured veins
- Widely used in sculpture and decoration (statues, floors)
- Portugal exports lots of marble! (Estremoz region)

🔵 **Slate**
- Forms from clay
- Splits into thin flat layers
- Used for school blackboards and roof tiles

⬛ **Schist (Xisto)**
- Dark grey colour
- Used in roofs of traditional houses in the Douro valley

## Soil

Soil is the surface layer of the Earth where plants, animals and micro-organisms live.

### Layers of Soil

```
═══════════════════════  🌿 Surface with plants
▓▓▓ Layer A (humus) ▓▓▓  Rich in organic matter — dark brown
▒▒▒ Layer B (subsoil) ▒  Less humus, more minerals — lighter
░░░ Layer C (rock) ░░░░  Fragmented rock
█████████████████████████  Solid bedrock
```

### Components of Soil

| Component | What is it | Importance |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 💧 Water | Liquid in the spaces | Essential for plants |
| 💨 Air | In spaces between particles | Needed for roots |
| 🪨 Minerals | Rock fragments | Nutrients |
| 🍂 Humus | Decomposed organic matter | Makes soil fertile |
| 🪱 Living things | Worms, bacteria, fungi | Decompose and aerate soil |

---

## Uses of Rocks

| Rock | Uses |
|------|------|
| Granite | Pavements, monuments, construction |
| Limestone | Walls, lime, cement |
| Marble | Sculptures, floors, decoration |
| Clay | Pottery, tiles, bricks |
| Slate | Blackboards, roofs, decoration |
| Basalt | Construction, pavements |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Rocks are **non-renewable natural resources** — they take millions of years to form! We must use them carefully and avoid waste. ♻️
