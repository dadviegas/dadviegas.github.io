# Word Classes

Words in Portuguese are divided into **groups** called grammatical classes. Each class has a different role in the sentence! Let's get to know them. 🚀

---

## Nouns (Substantivos)

**Nouns** are used to identify people, animals, things, places and ideas.

### Common Nouns

Refer to beings or things in a general way — they are not specific names.

| Example | Category |
|---------|----------|
| cat 🐱 | animal |
| table 🪑 | object |
| city 🏙️ | place |
| happiness 😊 | feeling |

### Proper Nouns

Identify a **specific** person or place and are written with an **uppercase** letter.

| Example | Type |
|---------|------|
| Ana, Pedro, Sofia | people 👧👦 |
| Lisbon, Paris, Rio | cities 🏙️ |
| Portugal, Brazil | countries 🌍 |
| Tagus, Amazon | rivers 🏞️ |

### Collective Nouns

A single noun that represents a **group** of beings or things.

| Collective noun | What it represents |
|----------------|-------------------|
| alcateia (pack) | group of wolves 🐺 |
| cardume (shoal) | group of fish 🐟 |
| ramalhete (bouquet) | group of flowers 💐 |
| rebanho (flock) | group of sheep 🐑 |
| floresta (forest) | group of trees 🌳 |
| biblioteca (library) | collection of books 📚 |

> [!TIP]
> Nouns can be **masculine** (o gato / the cat) or **feminine** (a gata / the she-cat); **singular** (um livro / a book) or **plural** (muitos livros / many books).

---

## Adjectives

**Adjectives** describe or qualify nouns — they tell us what people, animals or things are like.

| Noun | + Adjective | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| cat | black | The **black** cat 🐈‍⬛ |
| flower | beautiful | A **beautiful** flower 🌸 |
| boy | tall | The **tall** boy 👦 |
| house | big | A **big** house 🏠 |

**Degrees of the adjective:**

| Degree | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Positive | The cake is sweet. 🎂 |
| Comparative | This cake is sweeter than that one. |
| Superlative | This cake is the sweetest of all! |

> [!NOTE]
> In Portuguese, adjectives agree with the noun in **gender** (masc./fem.) and **number** (sing./pl.):
> - menino bonito / menina bonita (pretty boy / pretty girl)
> - meninos bonitos / meninas bonitas (pretty boys / pretty girls)

---

## Verbs

**Verbs** express actions, states or processes. They are the most important words in a sentence!

| Type | Definition | Examples |
|------|------------|---------|
| Action | What we do | to run 🏃, to eat 🍽️, to play 🎮 |
| State | How we are | to be, to remain, to stay |
| Process | What happens | to grow 🌱, to be born, to die |

**Verbs change according to:**

| Aspect | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Who does the action (person) | I **eat** / you **eat** / he **eats** |
| When it happens (tense) | I **ate** / I **eat** / I **will eat** |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In Portuguese, the verb always agrees with the **subject** — it changes according to person and number!

---

## Articles

**Articles** introduce nouns and indicate gender (masculine/feminine) and number (singular/plural).

### Definite Articles — indicate something specific

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|:--------:|:------:|
| **Masculine** | o (the) | os (the) |
| **Feminine** | a (the) | as (the) |

> o livro (the book), a mesa (the table), os livros (the books), as mesas (the tables)

### Indefinite Articles — indicate something general

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|:--------:|:------:|
| **Masculine** | um (a/an) | uns (some) |
| **Feminine** | uma (a/an) | umas (some) |

> um gato (a cat), uma flor (a flower), uns gatos (some cats), umas flores (some flowers)

---

## Pronouns

**Pronouns** replace nouns so we don't repeat the same words.

### Personal Pronouns

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|:--------:|:------:|
| 1st | eu (I) | nós (we) |
| 2nd | tu (you) | vós / vocês (you all) |
| 3rd | ele / ela (he / she) | eles / elas (they) |

**Example:**
> Ana went to school. **She** arrived early.
> ("She" replaces "Ana")

> [!TIP]
> Using pronouns avoids repetition and makes your writing flow better! ✨

---

## Adverbs

**Adverbs** modify the verb, the adjective or another adverb. They answer questions like: *when? where? how? how much?*

| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| Place | here, there, inside, outside, nearby 📍 |
| Time | today, yesterday, tomorrow, now, always ⏰ |
| Manner | well, badly, slowly, quickly, thus 🏃 |
| Quantity | very, little, enough, more, less |
| Affirmation | yes, certainly, of course ✅ |
| Negation | no, never, not ❌ |

**Example:**
> The cat runs **quickly**. (manner)
> **Today** it is sunny. (time) ☀️

---

## Prepositions

**Prepositions** link words within a sentence, indicating relationships of place, time, cause, etc.

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|:-----------:|-----|---------|
| **em** (in/on/at) | place, state | I am **at** home 🏠 |
| **de** (from/of) | origin, possession | I come **from** Lisbon |
| **a** (to/at) | direction, destination | I go **to** school |
| **para** (for/to) | purpose | It is **for** you 🎁 |
| **com** (with) | company | I go **with** Mum |
| **por** (by/through) | cause, place | I pass **through** there |
| **entre** (between) | position | I am **between** the two |
| **sobre** (on/about) | subject, position | The book is **on** the table 📚 |

> [!NOTE]
> Prepositions often merge with articles in Portuguese:
> - de + o = **do** (of the)
> - em + a = **na** (in the)
> - a + a = **à** (to the)

---

## Summary of Word Classes

| Class | Function | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Noun** | To identify | cat, Lisbon, pack |
| **Adjective** | To describe | beautiful, big, fast |
| **Verb** | To express action | to run, to eat, to be |
| **Article** | To introduce the noun | o, a, um, uma |
| **Pronoun** | To replace the noun | I, you, he, she |
| **Adverb** | To modify verb/adjective | here, well, today |
| **Preposition** | To link words | in, from, for, with |
