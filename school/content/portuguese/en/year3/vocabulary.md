# Vocabulary — Year 3

## Synonyms (Sinónimos)

**Synonyms** are words with the same or very similar meaning.

| Portuguese | Synonyms | English meaning |
|------------|---------|----------------|
| *bonito* | *lindo*, *formoso*, *belo*, *atraente* | beautiful, pretty |
| *triste* | *melancólico*, *abatido*, *pesaroso* | sad, melancholy |
| *começar* | *iniciar*, *arrancar*, *encetar* | to begin, to start |
| *falar* | *dizer*, *discursar*, *proferir* | to speak, to say |
| *ajudar* | *auxiliar*, *apoiar*, *socorrer* | to help, to support |

> [!TIP]
> Synonyms enrich your vocabulary and avoid repetition — but be careful, two synonyms are not always interchangeable in every situation!

---

## Antonyms (Antónimos)

**Antonyms** are words with opposite meanings.

| Word | Antonym | How it's formed | English |
|------|---------|----------------|---------|
| *feliz* | *infeliz* | prefix *in-* | happy / unhappy |
| *possível* | *impossível* | prefix *im-* | possible / impossible |
| *ordenado* | *desordenado* | prefix *des-* | tidy / untidy |
| *honesto* | *desonesto* | prefix *des-* | honest / dishonest |
| *capaz* | *incapaz* | prefix *in-* | capable / incapable |

> [!NOTE]
> The most common Portuguese prefixes for forming antonyms are: **in-**, **im-**, **des-**, **i-**, and **ir-**.

---

## Homophones (Homófonos)

**Homophones** are words that sound the same but have different meanings (and often different spellings).

| Pair | Meanings | Example sentence |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| *cozer* / *coser* | to cook / to sew | *Ela foi **cozer** o jantar.* (She went to cook dinner.) |
| *sela* / *cela* | saddle / prison cell | *A **sela** do cavalo é de couro.* (The horse's saddle is leather.) |
| *acento* / *assento* | accent mark / seat | *O **acento** agudo indica a sílaba tónica.* |
| *conserto* / *concerto* | repair / concert | *Fui ao **concerto** da escola.* (I went to the school concert.) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always use the context of the sentence to decide which spelling is correct!

---

## Word Families (Família de Palavras)

Words in the same family share the same **root** (*radical*).

| Root | Word family | English |
|------|-------------|---------|
| *livr-* | *livro*, *livraria*, *livreiro*, *livrete* | book, bookshop, bookseller, booklet |
| *mar-* | *mar*, *marinha*, *marinheiro*, *maré* | sea, navy, sailor, tide |
| *luz* | *luz*, *luminoso*, *iluminar*, *lustre* | light, luminous, to illuminate, chandelier |

---

## Polysemy (Polissemia) — One Word, Many Meanings

A single word can have several different meanings. This is called **polysemy** (*polissemia*).

**Example: the word *banco***

| Sentence | Meaning of *banco* |
|----------|--------------------|
| *Sentei-me no **banco** do jardim.* | bench / seat |
| *Fui ao **banco** levantar dinheiro.* | bank (financial) |
| *O **banco** de areia bloqueou o rio.* | sandbank |

> [!TIP]
> A dictionary lists all the different meanings (*aceções*) of a word — it's always worth checking!
