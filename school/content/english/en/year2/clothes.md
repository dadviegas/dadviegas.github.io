# Clothing

## Introduction

Being able to describe what people are wearing is an important everyday skill. In this lesson we learn clothing vocabulary and how to talk about what we wear in different situations.

## Talking About Clothes

| Expression | Portuguese |
|------------|-----------|
| **I'm wearing …** | Estou a usar … |
| **She's wearing …** | Ela está a usar … |
| **He is wearing a blue shirt.** | Ele está a usar uma camisa azul. |
| **I need to buy new shoes.** | Preciso de comprar sapatos novos. |
| **Can I try this on?** | Posso experimentar? |
| **What size are you?** | Qual é o teu número / tamanho? |

---

## Colors + Clothes

Combine color and clothing adjectives:

- **a red dress**
- **blue jeans**
- **black boots**
- **a white shirt**
- **green trainers**

> [!TIP]
> In English the adjective (color, size, material) comes **before** the noun: *a long blue coat*, *small white socks*.

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — What are they wearing?

Describe the outfit:

1. Blue trousers + white T-shirt + red trainers → ________
2. Black dress + black boots → ________
3. Grey coat + green scarf + gloves → ________

### Exercise 2 — What do you wear?

Answer in full sentences:

1. What do you wear when it's very cold?
2. What do you wear at the beach?
3. What is your school uniform like?

### Exercise 3 — Correct the order

Put the words in the right order:

1. boots / leather / black → ________
2. shirt / cotton / white → ________
3. long / blue / dress → ________

### Exercise 4 — Describe your outfit

Write 5 sentences describing what you are wearing today, including colors and materials.

> [!TIP]
> Each morning, try to describe your outfit to yourself in English. After a week you'll know all the clothing words by heart!
