# Geometry — Year 2

Last year we learned about **flat (2D) shapes**. Now we're going up a dimension — welcome to **3D shapes**! These are shapes you can **pick up and hold**! 🤲

---

## 3D Shapes (Solid Shapes)

**3D** means the shape has **length**, **width**, AND **height**. You can hold a 3D shape in your hand!

| Shape | Name | Real-life Example |
|-------|------|-------------------|
| 🎲 | **Cube** | Dice, ice cube |
| 🌐 | **Sphere** | Football, orange |
| 🥫 | **Cylinder** | Tin can, toilet roll |
| 🍦 | **Cone** | Ice cream cone, party hat |
| 🔺 | **Pyramid** | Egyptian pyramids |

---

## Faces, Edges, and Vertices

Every 3D shape is described by three things:

- **Face** = a flat surface (like the side of a box) 📋
- **Edge** = where two faces meet (like the corner of a book) 📐
- **Vertex** = a point where edges meet (a sharp corner) 📍

| Shape | Faces | Edges | Vertices |
|-------|-------|-------|----------|
| Cube | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| Cylinder | 3 (2 flat + 1 curved) | 2 | 0 |
| Cone | 2 (1 flat + 1 curved) | 1 | 1 |
| Sphere | 1 (all curved) | 0 | 0 |
| Square Pyramid | 5 | 8 | 5 |

> [!NOTE]
> A **sphere** has NO flat faces, NO edges, and NO vertices — it's perfectly round all the way around! ⚽

$$\text{Cube: } 6 \text{ faces} + 12 \text{ edges} + 8 \text{ vertices}$$

> [!TIP]
> **Euler's Rule** (for most solid shapes): Faces + Vertices − Edges = 2
> Check the cube: $6 + 8 - 12 = 2$ ✅ Magic! 🪄

## Rolling, Stacking, and Sliding

3D shapes can **roll**, **stack**, or **slide** depending on their faces:

| Shape | Can Roll? | Can Stack? | Can Slide? |
|-------|-----------|------------|------------|
| Cube | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Sphere | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Cylinder | ✅ (on its side) | ✅ (upright) | ✅ |
| Cone | ✅ (in circles) | ❌ | ✅ |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Shapes with **flat faces** can stack and slide. Shapes with **curved surfaces** can roll! 🔄

## Symmetry

A shape has **symmetry** when you can fold it in half and both sides match **exactly**.

The fold line is called the **line of symmetry** (or **axis of symmetry**).

```
   🦋 Butterfly — 1 line of symmetry
   Left wing = Right wing (mirror image!)
   
        |
   🌸🌸 | 🌸🌸
   🌸🌸 | 🌸🌸
        |
```

| Shape | Lines of Symmetry |
|-------|-------------------|
| Square | 4 |
| Rectangle | 2 |
| Circle | Infinite! ♾️ |
| Equilateral Triangle | 3 |
| Letter A | 1 |
| Letter B | 0 |

> [!TIP]
> Use a **mirror** to check symmetry! Place it on a shape — if the reflection looks the same as the other side, you found a line of symmetry! 🪞

## Plane Figures (2D Review)

We also call 2D shapes **plane figures** because they lie flat on a plane (a flat surface).

| Shape | Sides | Special Features |
|-------|-------|-----------------|
| Triangle | 3 | Smallest polygon |
| Square | 4 | All sides equal, all angles equal |
| Rectangle | 4 | Opposite sides equal |
| Pentagon | 5 | Like the USA's Pentagon building 🏛️ |
| Hexagon | 6 | Like a honeycomb! 🐝 |

---

## 3D Shapes from 2D Faces

Unfolding a 3D shape gives you its **net** — a flat pattern that folds up to make the shape!

- **Cube net** = 6 squares arranged in a cross shape ✝️
- **Cylinder net** = 2 circles + 1 rectangle 📋

## Try It!

1. **Shape Hunt 3D**: Find one example of a **cube**, **sphere**, **cylinder**, and **cone** in your home. (Hint: check the kitchen! 🍳)

2. **Count the Faces**: Pick up a cereal box. Count its **faces**, **edges**, and **vertices**. What 3D shape is it?

3. **Symmetry Butterfly**: Fold a piece of paper in half. Paint one side with spots. Fold and press — open it up. Both sides match! You made a symmetry butterfly! 🦋

4. **Stack the Shapes**: Which shapes from your kitchen can you **stack**? Which ones **roll**? Make two lists!

5. **Mirror Test**: Write the letters of your name. Hold a mirror next to each letter. Which letters look the same in the mirror? Those have symmetry! 🪞
