# Measurements — Year 3

Time to level up our measuring skills! This year we'll use **even smaller and bigger units**, measure **liquids**, and get really accurate with **time**! ⏱️

---

## Length — From Millimetres to Kilometres

We now have **four** units for measuring length!

| Unit | Symbol | About the size of... |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| **Millimetre** | mm | Thickness of a coin 🪙 |
| **Centimetre** | cm | Width of your finger 👆 |
| **Metre** | m | Height of a door 🚪 |
| **Kilometre** | km | About 10 minutes walk 🚶 |

### Conversions

$$1 \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ mm}$$

$$1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm} = 1000 \text{ mm}$$

$$1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m}$$

> [!TIP]
> **Kilo** always means **1000**! So 1 **kilo**metre = 1000 metres, and 1 **kilo**gram = 1000 grams. Spot the pattern! 🔑

### Converting Examples

**cm to mm:** Multiply by 10
$$3 \text{ cm} = 3 \times 10 = 30 \text{ mm}$$

**mm to cm:** Divide by 10
$$50 \text{ mm} = 50 \div 10 = 5 \text{ cm}$$

**m to cm:** Multiply by 100
$$2 \text{ m} = 2 \times 100 = 200 \text{ cm}$$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> When converting to a **smaller** unit → **multiply** (you need more of them!)
> When converting to a **bigger** unit → **divide** (you need fewer of them!) 🔄

## Mass — Grams and Kilograms

$$1 \text{ kg} = 1000 \text{ g}$$

### Converting Mass

**g to kg:** Divide by 1000
$$3000 \text{ g} = 3000 \div 1000 = 3 \text{ kg}$$

**kg to g:** Multiply by 1000
$$2.5 \text{ kg} = 2.5 \times 1000 = 2500 \text{ g}$$

| Item | Mass |
|------|------|
| Grain of rice 🍚 | about 25 mg |
| Chocolate bar 🍫 | about 100 g |
| Bag of potatoes 🥔 | 1 kg |
| Child aged 8 👦 | about 25 kg |
| Car 🚗 | about 1500 kg |

> [!NOTE]
> Doctors measure very light medicines in **milligrams (mg)**! That's 1000 times smaller than a gram. $1 \text{ g} = 1000 \text{ mg}$ 💊

## Capacity — Litres and Millilitres

**Capacity** is how much liquid a container can hold.

$$1 \text{ litre} = 1000 \text{ millilitres}$$

$$1 \text{ L} = 1000 \text{ mL}$$

| Unit | Symbol | About the size of... |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| **Millilitre** | mL | A few drops of water 💧 |
| **Litre** | L | A large water bottle 🍶 |

### Common Capacities

| Container | Capacity |
|-----------|----------|
| Teaspoon | 5 mL |
| Cup ☕ | 250 mL |
| Milk carton 🥛 | 500 mL or 1 L |
| Bathtub 🛁 | about 150 L |
| Petrol tank ⛽ | about 50 L |

> [!TIP]
> A **500 mL bottle** of water is **half a litre**! 
> $$500 \text{ mL} = \frac{1}{2} \text{ L} = 0.5 \text{ L}$$ 💧

## Time — Minutes, Seconds, and Quarter Hours

### Units of Time

$$1 \text{ minute} = 60 \text{ seconds}$$

$$1 \text{ hour} = 60 \text{ minutes}$$

$$\frac{1}{4} \text{ hour} = 15 \text{ minutes}$$

$$\frac{1}{2} \text{ hour} = 30 \text{ minutes}$$

$$\frac{3}{4} \text{ hour} = 45 \text{ minutes}$$

### Quarter Hours on the Clock

| Long Hand Position | Time Expression |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| Points to **12** | o'clock (e.g. 4 o'clock) |
| Points to **3** | quarter past (e.g. quarter past 4) |
| Points to **6** | half past (e.g. half past 4) |
| Points to **9** | quarter to (e.g. quarter to 5) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **"Quarter to"** means 15 minutes **before** the NEXT hour, not after the current hour! So "quarter to 5" = 4:45 ⏰

### Calculating Time Durations

**Example:** A film starts at 3:00 and ends at 4:45. How long is the film?

$$4{:}45 - 3{:}00 = 1 \text{ hour and } 45 \text{ minutes}$$

| Capacity | mL, L |

## Try It!

1. **Conversion Challenge**: Convert these measurements:
   - 4 cm = ___ mm
   - 300 cm = ___ m
   - 2 km = ___ m
   - 5000 g = ___ kg

2. **Liquid Hunt**: Find 5 bottles or containers at home. Read the label. Write each capacity in mL AND in L!

3. **Clock Quarter Hours**: Set an alarm or ask a grown-up to show you a clock at: 2:15, 5:30, 8:45, and 11:00. Say the time using "quarter past", "half past", or "quarter to"!

4. **Body Measurements**: Measure your height in **cm**. Convert it to **mm**. How tall are you in millimetres? 📏

5. **Recipe Maths**: A recipe needs 750 mL of milk. How many mL short are you if you only have half a litre (500 mL)? How much more do you need? 🧁
