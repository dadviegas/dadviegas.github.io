# Multiplication

## Multiplying by 1 digit

### Example: $234 \times 3$

Multiply **right to left**, carrying when needed:

```
  234
×   3
-----
  702
```

- Ones: $4 \times 3 = 12$ → write **2**, carry **1**
- Tens: $3 \times 3 + 1 = 10$ → write **0**, carry **1**
- Hundreds: $2 \times 3 + 1 = 7$ → write **7**

$$234 \times 3 = 702$$

---

## Multiplying by 2 digits

### Example: $45 \times 23$

Split the second factor: $23 = 20 + 3$

$$45 \times 23 = 45 \times 20 + 45 \times 3$$

$$= 900 + 135 = 1035$$

In column form:

```
     45
×    23
-------
    135   (45 × 3)
   900    (45 × 20)
-------
   1035
```

---

## Useful properties

**Distributive:**
$$6 \times (30 + 4) = 6 \times 30 + 6 \times 4 = 180 + 24 = 204$$

**Associative:**
$$(2 \times 5) \times 7 = 2 \times (5 \times 7) = 70$$
