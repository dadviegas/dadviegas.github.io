# Reading & Writing — Year 2

## Reading with Fluency

**Fluent reading** means reading smoothly, at a good pace, and with expression.

> [!TIP]
> When reading aloud in Portuguese, pay attention to punctuation:
> - **.** (*ponto final*) — long pause
> - **,** (*vírgula*) — short pause
> - **!** (*ponto de exclamação*) — read with feeling
> - **?** (*ponto de interrogação*) — read with curiosity

---

## Understanding What You Read

After reading a text, you should be able to answer questions about it.

**The key comprehension questions are:**

| Question | Portuguese | What to look for |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| Who? | *Quem?* | the characters |
| What? | *O quê?* | what happened |
| When? | *Quando?* | time |
| Where? | *Onde?* | the place |
| How? | *Como?* | the way it happened |
| Why? | *Porquê?* | the reason |

> [!NOTE]
> Read the text at least **twice** before answering questions — the first time to understand the general meaning, the second time to find specific details.

---

## Reading Strategies

- **Before reading**: look at the title and pictures; predict what the text will be about.
- **While reading**: underline words you don't know.
- **After reading**: summarise the text in your own words.

---

## Writing Sentences in Portuguese

A good Portuguese sentence must have:

1. **Subject** (*sujeito*) — who or what the sentence is about
2. **Predicate** (*predicado*) — what the subject does or is

Example:
> **A menina** *(subject)* **brinca no jardim** *(predicate).*
> The girl plays in the garden.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always start with a capital letter and end with a full stop. Check your spelling before you finish!

---

## Writing a Short Text

A well-structured text has three parts:

| Part | Portuguese | What to include |
|------|------------|----------------|
| Beginning | *Início* | introduce characters and setting |
| Middle | *Desenvolvimento* | the events or problem |
| End | *Conclusão* | how it ends |

> [!TIP]
> Use **connectors** to link your ideas:
> *primeiro* (first) → *depois* (then) → *a seguir* (next) → *por fim* (finally)

---

## Try it! 🎯

1. **Read and answer**: *"O Rui foi ao mercado com a avó. Comprou pão, fruta e leite."* — Who went to the market? What did they buy? Who went with Rui?
2. **Put the sentences in order** to make a story with a beginning, middle, and end.
3. **Write 3 sentences** about your day using *primeiro* (first), *depois* (then), and *por fim* (finally).
4. **Underline the subject** and **circle the predicate**: *O cão ladra muito.* / *A Ana lê um livro.*
