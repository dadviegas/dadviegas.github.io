## Hundreds, tens and ones

$$347 = 300 + 40 + 7$$

## Place value

In the number **726**:

| Digit | Position | Value              |
|-------|----------|--------------------|
| 7     | Hundreds | $7 \times 100 = 700$ |
| 2     | Tens     | $2 \times 10 = 20$ |
| 6     | Ones     | $6 \times 1 = 6$   |

$$726 = 700 + 20 + 6$$

## Comparing 3-digit numbers

Compare from **left to right**:

$$512 < 521 \quad \text{(hundreds equal, 1 ten} < \text{2 tens)}$$

$$830 > 389 \quad \text{(8 hundreds} > \text{3 hundreds)}$$
