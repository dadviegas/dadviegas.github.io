# Travel Vocabulary

## Introduction

Whether you're planning a holiday, reading about a destination, or having a conversation about travel, English travel vocabulary is incredibly useful. Let's explore it!

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Transport match

Match the transport to the place you find it:

| Transport | | Place |
|----------|-|-------|
| plane | | bus stop |
| train | | airport |
| bus | | harbour |
| ferry | | train station |

### Exercise 2 — What do you need?

Write the English word:

1. You need this to enter another country: ________
2. This shows you where your gate is: ________
3. You carry your clothes in this: ________
4. You stay here during your holiday: ________

### Exercise 3 — Travel dialogue

Write a dialogue at a hotel reception (8–10 lines). Include:
- A greeting
- Confirming a reservation
- Asking about facilities
- Getting a key card
- Saying goodbye

### Exercise 4 — My dream holiday

Write a paragraph (8–10 sentences) describing your dream holiday:
- Where you would go
- How you would travel
- Where you would stay
- What you would visit
- Why you chose this destination

> [!TIP]
> Many English travel words are the same or very similar in Portuguese: *hotel, tourist, passport, visa, taxi*. These are called **cognates** and they make learning easier!
