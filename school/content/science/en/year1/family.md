# My Family

A family is made up of the people who live with us and care for us. All families are different and special!

---

## Family Members

A family can have many different people in it.

| Member | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Mother and Father | Your parents look after you |
| Brother and Sister | Your siblings grow up with you |
| Grandfather and Grandmother | Your parents' parents |
| Uncle and Aunt | Your parents' brothers and sisters |
| Cousin | Your aunts' and uncles' children |

> [!NOTE]
> Some families are large, with many members. Others are smaller. All families are equally special!

---

## The Family Tree

A **family tree** shows how family members are connected to each other.

```
    Grandparents          Grandparents
           \                  /
        Dad  ——————————  Mum
                   |
                  ME
             /         \
         Brother      Sister
```

> [!TIP]
> Ask an adult to help you make your own family tree. You can draw each person's face!

---

## Roles in the Family

Each person in the family has important things they do.

- **Parents and carers** — work, look after the home and their children
- **Children** — go to school, play and help at home
- **Grandparents** — share stories, experiences and wisdom
- **Everyone together** — helps each other with love and respect

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Everyone in the family deserves **respect and kindness**. We should listen to older family members and share with younger ones.

## Try it! 🎯

1. **Draw your family**: make a drawing with all the members of your family. Don't forget any pets!
2. **Family interview**: ask an older family member what school was like when they were young.
3. **Family tree**: with the help of an adult, write the name of every family member on your tree.
4. **I help at home**: make a list of three things you can do to help your family.
