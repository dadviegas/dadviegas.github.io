# Animals

Animals are fascinating living things! There are thousands of different species all over the world.

---

## Domestic and Wild Animals

Animals can be **domestic** or **wild**.

| Type | Characteristics | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic | Live with humans, cared for by us | Dog, cat, rabbit, fish |
| Wild | Live in nature, find their own food | Wolf, deer, eagle, fox |

> [!NOTE]
> Some animals that are domestic today, like the dog, are descended from wild animals (the wolf) that were tamed thousands of years ago!

---

## Animal Characteristics

Animals have characteristics that help them survive.

- **Body covering**: fur, feathers, scales or bare skin
- **How they move**: walking, flying, swimming, crawling
- **How they are born**: oviparous (hatched from eggs) or viviparous (born from mother's body)
- **How they breathe**: lungs or gills

| Animal | Body covering | Born from | Breathes with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dog | Fur | Mother's body | Lungs |
| Hen | Feathers | Egg | Lungs |
| Sardine | Scales | Egg | Gills |
| Frog | Bare damp skin | Egg (in water) | Lungs and skin |

---

## What Animals Eat

| Type | What it eats | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Herbivores | Plants | Rabbit, cow, horse |
| Carnivores | Other animals | Lion, eagle, wolf |
| Omnivores | Both plants and animals | Bear, wild boar, humans |

> [!TIP]
> Look at an animal's teeth to work out what it eats! Sharp pointed teeth = carnivore. Flat wide teeth = herbivore.

---

## Where Animals Live

The place where an animal lives is called its **habitat**.

| Habitat | Animals that live there |
|---|---|
| Forest | Wolf, deer, wild boar, owl |
| Sea | Dolphin, shark, octopus |
| River | Otter, trout, heron |
| Countryside | Rabbit, hare, stork |
| Desert | Camel, scorpion, snake |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> We must **respect animals** and their habitats. We should not capture wild animals or leave litter in nature!
