# Areas & Perimeters

Knowing how to calculate areas and perimeters helps us solve everyday problems — painting a wall, fencing a garden, tiling a floor! 🏡

---

## What is Perimeter?

The **perimeter** is the total distance around the outside of a shape.

> 💡 Imagine an ant walking all the way around the edge of a shape — the perimeter is how far it travels!

## What is Area?

The **area** is the amount of space inside a shape — the surface it covers.

> 💡 Imagine how many 1 cm × 1 cm squares fit inside the shape — that is the area!

---

## Square

$$\text{Side} = l$$

| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Perimeter** | $P = 4 \times l$ |
| **Area** | $A = l \times l = l^2$ |

**Example:** Square with side 5 cm

$$P = 4 \times 5 = 20 \text{ cm}$$

$$A = 5 \times 5 = 25 \text{ cm}^2$$

---

## Rectangle

$$\text{Length} = l \quad \text{Width} = w$$

| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Perimeter** | $P = 2 \times (l + w)$ |
| **Area** | $A = l \times w$ |

**Example:** Rectangle with length 8 cm and width 3 cm

$$P = 2 \times (8 + 3) = 2 \times 11 = 22 \text{ cm}$$

$$A = 8 \times 3 = 24 \text{ cm}^2$$

---

## Triangle

$$\text{Base} = b \quad \text{Height} = h \quad \text{Sides} = a, b, c$$

| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Perimeter** | $P = a + b + c$ |
| **Area** | $A = \dfrac{b \times h}{2}$ |

**Example:** Triangle with base 6 cm, height 4 cm and sides 5 cm, 6 cm, 5 cm

$$P = 5 + 6 + 5 = 16 \text{ cm}$$

$$A = \frac{6 \times 4}{2} = \frac{24}{2} = 12 \text{ cm}^2$$

> 💡 The height is always **perpendicular** to the base — it forms a right angle!

---

## Circle ⭕

$$\text{Radius} = r \quad \text{Diameter} = d = 2r \quad \pi \approx 3.14$$

| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Perimeter (circumference)** | $P = 2 \times \pi \times r$ |
| **Area** | $A = \pi \times r^2$ |

**Example:** Circle with radius 4 cm

$$P = 2 \times 3.14 \times 4 = 25.12 \text{ cm}$$

$$A = 3.14 \times 4^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = 50.24 \text{ cm}^2$$

> 💡 $\pi$ (pi) is a special number that starts with **3.14159…** and never ends!

---

## Summary Table

| Shape | Perimeter | Area |
|---|---|---|
| **Square** | $P = 4l$ | $A = l^2$ |
| **Rectangle** | $P = 2(l + w)$ | $A = l \times w$ |
| **Triangle** | $P = a + b + c$ | $A = \dfrac{b \times h}{2}$ |
| **Circle** | $P = 2\pi r$ | $A = \pi r^2$ |

## Units of Area

Area uses **squared units** (²):

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Square millimetre | mm² | — |
| Square centimetre | cm² | $1 \text{ cm}^2 = 100 \text{ mm}^2$ |
| Square decimetre | dm² | $1 \text{ dm}^2 = 100 \text{ cm}^2$ |
| Square metre | m² | $1 \text{ m}^2 = 100 \text{ dm}^2$ |
| Hectare | ha | $1 \text{ ha} = 10{,}000 \text{ m}^2$ |
| Square kilometre | km² | $1 \text{ km}^2 = 1{,}000{,}000 \text{ m}^2$ |

---

## Tips to Avoid Mistakes

- 📏 **Perimeter** uses simple units (cm, m…)
- 📐 **Area** uses **squared** units (cm², m²…)
- ✏️ Always draw the shape with the measurements labelled
- 🔁 Check your units — don't mix cm and m!
- 🔺 In a triangle, the height can be **outside** the shape for obtuse triangles
