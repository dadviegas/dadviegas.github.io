# The Human Body

## An Amazing Machine!

Your body is the most incredible machine ever made! It works 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — even while you are sleeping! 😴 It is made of trillions of tiny cells, which group together to form **tissues**, which form **organs**, which work together in **systems**.

---

## Organisation of the Body

```
Cells → Tissues → Organs → Systems → Organism
```

| Level | Example |
|-------|---------|
| 🔵 Cell | Muscle cell, neuron |
| 🟡 Tissue | Muscle tissue, nervous tissue |
| 🟠 Organ | Heart, lung, liver |
| 🔴 System | Circulatory system, digestive system |
| 🟢 Organism | The complete human body |

---

## The Body's Systems

### The Digestive System

**Function:** Break down food into nutrients that the blood can carry to every cell.

| Organ | Function |
|-------|---------|
| 👄 Mouth | Chews and mixes food with saliva |
| 🟡 Oesophagus | Carries food down to the stomach |
| 🟠 Stomach | Mixes food with gastric juices |
| 🟤 Small intestine | Absorbs nutrients into the blood |
| 🟤 Large intestine | Absorbs water, forms solid waste |
| 🟡 Liver | Produces bile, filters blood |
| 🟡 Pancreas | Produces digestive enzymes |

**Journey:** Mouth → Oesophagus → Stomach → Small Intestine → Large Intestine → Anus

---

### The Respiratory System

**Function:** Bring oxygen into the blood and remove carbon dioxide.

| Organ | Function |
|-------|---------|
| 👃 Nose/Mouth | Air enters, is filtered and warmed |
| 🟡 Trachea (windpipe) | Carries air to the lungs |
| 🫁 Lungs | Exchange oxygen for CO₂ |
| 🟤 Diaphragm | Muscle that controls breathing |

> [!TIP]
> We breathe about **15 times per minute** and **21,600 times per day**! An adult's lungs can hold about 6 litres of air. 💨

---

### The Circulatory System

**Function:** Transport blood, oxygen, and nutrients throughout the body.

| Organ | Function |
|-------|---------|
| 🫀 Heart | Pumps blood — beats ~70 times/minute |
| 🔴 Arteries | Carry blood from the heart to the body |
| 🔵 Veins | Return blood to the heart |
| 🟡 Capillaries | Very thin vessels reaching every cell |
| 🩸 Blood | Carries oxygen, nutrients and defences |

> [!NOTE]
> Your heart beats about **100,000 times per day** and pumps about **5 litres of blood** per minute! Over a lifetime, it beats more than 2 billion times! 🫀

---

### The Excretory System

**Function:** Remove waste products from the body.

| Organ | What it removes |
|-------|---------------|
| 🫘 Kidneys | Filters blood → produces urine |
| 💧 Bladder | Stores urine |
| 💦 Skin | Removes sweat (water + salts) |
| 🫁 Lungs | Expels CO₂ and water vapour |
| 🟤 Large intestine | Removes solid waste (faeces) |

---

### The Nervous System

**Function:** Control and coordinate all body functions.

| Organ | Function |
|-------|---------|
| 🧠 Brain | Control centre — thought, memory, emotions |
| 🔵 Cerebellum | Coordinates movement and balance |
| 🟡 Brain stem | Controls automatic functions (breathing, heartbeat) |
| 🔴 Spinal cord | Connects brain to the rest of the body |
| ⚡ Nerves | Transmit electrical signals throughout the body |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The brain weighs only ~1.4 kg, but uses 20% of all the body's energy! It has approximately **86 billion neurons** (nerve cells). 🧠

---

### The Muscular System

**Function:** Enable movement of the body.

| Type | Characteristics | Examples |
|------|----------------|---------|
| 💪 Voluntary | We control them | Biceps, quadriceps |
| ❤️ Involuntary | Work automatically | Heart, stomach |
| 🏃 Smooth | Line organ walls | Intestines, blood vessels |

- The body has about **640 muscles**
- The largest muscle is the **gluteus maximus** (buttocks)
- The strongest (relative to size) is the **masseter** (jaw muscle)

---

### The Skeletal System

**Function:** Support the body, protect organs, and enable movement.

| Part | Function | Examples |
|------|---------|---------|
| 🦴 Bones | Support and protection | Skull, ribs, femur |
| 🔗 Joints | Connect bones | Knee, elbow, hip |
| 🟡 Cartilage | Cushioning at joints | Knees, ears, nose |

**Fascinating Facts:**
- An adult has **206 bones**
- Babies are born with ~300 bones (some fuse together as we grow!)
- The longest bone is the **femur** (thigh bone)
- The smallest bone is the **stapes** (in the ear) — just 3mm long!

---

## Systems Summary

| System | Main Function | Main Organ |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| 🍽️ Digestive | Digest food | Stomach |
| 🫁 Respiratory | Breathe | Lungs |
| 🫀 Circulatory | Transport blood | Heart |
| 🚽 Excretory | Remove waste | Kidneys |
| 🧠 Nervous | Control the body | Brain |
| 💪 Muscular | Move the body | Muscles |
| 🦴 Skeletal | Support the body | Bones |

> [!TIP]
> Look after your body! 🥗 Eat well, exercise, sleep 9–10 hours a night and drink plenty of water. Your body will thank you! 💪
