# Division

**Dividing** means **sharing equally** into groups.

$$\text{dividend} \div \text{divisor} = \text{quotient}$$

---

## What is division?

You have **12 oranges** to share equally among **3 friends**.
How many oranges does each friend get?

$$12 \div 3 = 4$$

Each friend gets **4 oranges**. 🍊🍊🍊🍊

---

## The link with multiplication

Division and multiplication are **inverse** operations:

$$12 \div 3 = 4 \quad \Leftrightarrow \quad 4 \times 3 = 12$$

> [!TIP]
> To calculate $35 \div 7$, ask yourself: **what number times 7 gives 35?**
> $5 \times 7 = 35$, so $35 \div 7 = 5$.

---

## Worked examples

| Operation   | Think…          | Answer |
|-------------|-----------------|--------|
| $20 \div 4$ | $5 \times 4 = 20$ | $5$  |
| $36 \div 6$ | $6 \times 6 = 36$ | $6$  |
| $63 \div 7$ | $9 \times 7 = 63$ | $9$  |
| $56 \div 8$ | $7 \times 8 = 56$ | $7$  |

---

## Dividing by 1 and itself

$$n \div 1 = n \qquad \text{(dividing by 1 leaves the number unchanged)}$$

$$n \div n = 1 \qquad \text{(any number divided by itself is 1)}$$
