# Plants

Plants are living things that are essential to life on Earth. They produce the oxygen we breathe and form the base of almost every food chain.

---

## Parts of a Plant

A complete plant has several parts, each with a specific function:

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| **Root** | Anchors the plant in the soil and absorbs water and minerals |
| **Stem** | Supports the plant and transports water and nutrients |
| **Leaf** | Carries out photosynthesis and transpiration |
| **Flower** | Organ of sexual reproduction |
| **Fruit** | Protects the seeds and helps disperse them |
| **Seed** | Contains the embryo of a new plant |

> [!TIP]
> Not all plants have every part! Flowerless plants such as ferns and mosses have no flowers, fruits or seeds.

---

## Photosynthesis

**Photosynthesis** is the process by which plants make their own food. It takes place mainly in the **leaves**, which are green because they contain **chlorophyll**.

**The photosynthesis recipe:**

$$\text{Water} + \text{Carbon Dioxide} \xrightarrow{\text{Sunlight}} \text{Glucose} + \text{Oxygen}$$

Plants need:
- **Sunlight** (energy)
- **Water** (absorbed by the roots)
- **Carbon dioxide** (CO₂, absorbed through the leaves)

And they produce:
- **Glucose** (food for the plant)
- **Oxygen** (released into the atmosphere)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Plants are the only living things that can make their own food. That is why we call them **producers**. All other living things depend on them, directly or indirectly.

## Flowering and Non-Flowering Plants

### Flowering Plants (Angiosperms)

The most common type of plant. The flower is the reproductive organ. After **pollination** (transfer of pollen), a fruit forms containing seeds.

Examples: rose bush, orange tree, sunflower, wheat.

### Non-Flowering Plants

| Group | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Ferns | Have leaves and stems, no flowers or seeds | Maidenhair fern, bracken |
| Mosses | Very small, grow in damp places | Wall moss |
| Algae | Live in water, no roots, stem or leaves | Red algae, sea lettuce |
| Conifers | Produce cones instead of flowers | Pine, cypress, cedar |

---

## How Plants Reproduce

Plants can reproduce in two ways:

- **Sexual reproduction** — through seeds, formed after pollination.
- **Asexual (vegetative) reproduction** — from parts of the plant (cuttings, bulbs, rhizomes).

### How Do Seeds Spread?

| Method | How it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Wind | Light seeds or seeds with "parachutes" | Dandelion, maple |
| Animals | Tasty fruits or seeds with hooks | Acorn (oak), burdock |
| Water | Floating seeds | Coconut, water lily |
| Explosion | The plant shoots out its seeds | Squirting cucumber |

> [!NOTE]
> **Pollination** can be carried out by wind or by animals such as bees, butterflies and birds, which carry pollen from flower to flower. Without pollinators, most flowering plants could not reproduce.
