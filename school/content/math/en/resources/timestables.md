Times tables are the foundation of multiplication. Learn them well and maths becomes so much easier! 🌟

## The 1 Times Table

$$1 \times 1 = 1 \quad 1 \times 2 = 2 \quad 1 \times 3 = 3 \quad 1 \times 4 = 4 \quad 1 \times 5 = 5$$
$$1 \times 6 = 6 \quad 1 \times 7 = 7 \quad 1 \times 8 = 8 \quad 1 \times 9 = 9 \quad 1 \times 10 = 10$$

> 💡 **Trick:** Any number multiplied by 1 stays the same!

---

## The 2 Times Table

$$2 \times 1 = 2 \quad 2 \times 2 = 4 \quad 2 \times 3 = 6 \quad 2 \times 4 = 8 \quad 2 \times 5 = 10$$
$$2 \times 6 = 12 \quad 2 \times 7 = 14 \quad 2 \times 8 = 16 \quad 2 \times 9 = 18 \quad 2 \times 10 = 20$$

> 💡 **Trick:** Multiplying by 2 is the same as doubling! $2 \times 7 = 7 + 7 = 14$

---

## The 5 Times Table

$$5 \times 1 = 5 \quad 5 \times 2 = 10 \quad 5 \times 3 = 15 \quad 5 \times 4 = 20 \quad 5 \times 5 = 25$$
$$5 \times 6 = 30 \quad 5 \times 7 = 35 \quad 5 \times 8 = 40 \quad 5 \times 9 = 45 \quad 5 \times 10 = 50$$

> 💡 **Trick:** The answers always end in **0** or **5**!

---

## The 10 Times Table

$$10 \times 1 = 10 \quad 10 \times 2 = 20 \quad 10 \times 3 = 30 \quad 10 \times 4 = 40 \quad 10 \times 5 = 50$$
$$10 \times 6 = 60 \quad 10 \times 7 = 70 \quad 10 \times 8 = 80 \quad 10 \times 9 = 90 \quad 10 \times 10 = 100$$

> 💡 **Trick:** Just add a **0** to the number! $10 \times 7 = 70$

---

## The 9 Times Table

$$9 \times 1 = 9 \quad 9 \times 2 = 18 \quad 9 \times 3 = 27 \quad 9 \times 4 = 36 \quad 9 \times 5 = 45$$
$$9 \times 6 = 54 \quad 9 \times 7 = 63 \quad 9 \times 8 = 72 \quad 9 \times 9 = 81 \quad 9 \times 10 = 90$$

> 💡 **Finger trick:** For $9 \times 3$, fold down your 3rd finger — 2 fingers left, 7 right → **27**!
> 💡 **Digit sum trick:** The digits of the answer always add up to **9**! $9 \times 7 = 63 \rightarrow 6+3=9$

---

## Properties of Multiplication

| Property | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Commutative** | $3 \times 4 = 4 \times 3$ | Order doesn't change the result |
| **Associative** | $(2 \times 3) \times 4 = 2 \times (3 \times 4)$ | Grouping doesn't change the result |
| **Identity** | $7 \times 1 = 7$ | Multiplying by 1 gives the same number |
| **Zero** | $7 \times 0 = 0$ | Multiplying by 0 always gives 0 |

---

## Tips to Help You Remember

- 🔁 **Say them out loud** — recite the tables every day for 5 minutes
- 🎵 **Sing them** — make up a song with the answers
- 🃏 **Flashcards** — write the question on one side, the answer on the other
- ✍️ **Write them out** — copy the tables several times
- 🎮 **Play games** — use maths games to practise
- 👫 **Work in pairs** — ask someone to quiz you
- ⭐ **Start with the easy ones** — 1, 2, 5 and 10 first, then 3, 4, 6, then 7, 8, 9
