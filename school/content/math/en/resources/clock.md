# Interactive Clock

Learn to tell the time with this clock! Use the buttons to change the time or tap the presets.

---

```clock
locale: en
```

---

## How to Read the Time

### Whole Hours (O'Clock)

When the big hand (minutes) points to **12**, it's a whole hour.

| Small hand | Time |
|---|---|
| At 1 | 1 o'clock |
| At 3 | 3 o'clock |
| At 6 | 6 o'clock |
| At 12 | 12 o'clock (noon or midnight) |

---

### Half Past

When the big hand points to **6**, it's half past the hour.

| Example | Read as |
|---|---|
| 3:30 | half past three |
| 7:30 | half past seven |
| 12:30 | half past twelve |

---

### Quarter Past and Quarter To

| Minutes | Name | Big hand points to |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min | quarter past | 3 |
| 30 min | half past | 6 |
| 45 min | quarter to | 9 |

| Example | Read as |
|---|---|
| 2:15 | quarter past two |
| 5:45 | quarter to six |
| 8:15 | quarter past eight |

> [!TIP]
> The **small** hand shows the **hour**. The **big** hand (blue) shows the **minutes**!

---

## 24-Hour Clock

A day has **24 hours**. After noon (12:00), we keep counting:

| 24-hour | 12-hour | Time of day |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00 | 12:00 AM | Midnight |
| 06:00 | 6:00 AM | Morning |
| 12:00 | 12:00 PM | Noon |
| 13:00 | 1:00 PM | Afternoon |
| 18:00 | 6:00 PM | Evening |
| 21:00 | 9:00 PM | Night |
