# Animals

Animals are living things that move, feed and reproduce. There are millions of animal species on our planet, with very different shapes, sizes and ways of life.

---

## Classifying Animals

Animals are divided into two main groups: **vertebrates** (with a backbone) and **invertebrates** (without a backbone).

### Vertebrates

| Class | Key Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Mammals | Have fur/hair, warm-blooded, feed young with milk | Dog, dolphin, whale, bat |
| Birds | Have feathers, a beak, two wings, warm-blooded | Eagle, pigeon, penguin, ostrich |
| Reptiles | Have scales, cold-blooded, mostly lay eggs | Snake, lizard, crocodile, turtle |
| Amphibians | Live in water and on land, moist skin, cold-blooded | Frog, toad, salamander, newt |
| Fish | Live in water, have scales and fins, cold-blooded | Sardine, cod, shark |

### Invertebrates

Invertebrates have no backbone. The most familiar groups are:

- **Insects** — 6 legs, body in 3 parts (head, thorax, abdomen). E.g. butterfly, bee, ant.
- **Arachnids** — 8 legs. E.g. spider, scorpion.
- **Crustaceans** — hard shell, mostly aquatic. E.g. crab, lobster.
- **Molluscs** — soft body, often with a shell. E.g. snail, octopus, mussel.

> [!NOTE]
> Insects are the **most species-rich** animal group on the planet — over one million known species have been described!

## How Animals Feed

| Type | What they eat | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Herbivores | Plants only | Rabbit, cow, horse |
| Carnivores | Other animals | Lion, shark, wolf |
| Omnivores | Plants and animals | Bear, wild boar, human |
| Insectivores | Insects | Bat, stork |

---

## Food Chains

A **food chain** shows the order in which living things feed on each other.

**Example:**
> Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Eagle

- **Producers** — plants that make their own food through photosynthesis.
- **Primary consumers** — herbivores that eat plants.
- **Secondary consumers** — animals that eat herbivores.
- **Tertiary consumers** — animals at the top of the chain.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If one species disappears from a food chain, the whole chain can become unbalanced. This is why it is so important to protect biodiversity!

---

## Animal Adaptations

Animals have developed **adaptations** to survive in their habitat:

- The **duck** has webbed feet for swimming.
- The **camel** stores fat in its hump to survive in the desert.
- The **octopus** changes colour to camouflage itself.
- The **mole** has broad, shovel-like paws for digging.

## Animals in Portugal

Portugal is home to animals such as the **Iberian wolf**, the **Iberian lynx**, the **white stork**, the **sardine** and the **Iberian wall lizard**. Many are protected by law.

> [!TIP]
> The **Iberian lynx** is one of the most endangered wild cats in the world. Conservation programmes in Portugal and Spain have helped its numbers grow in recent years.
