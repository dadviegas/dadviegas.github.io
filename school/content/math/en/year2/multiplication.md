# Times Tables × 2, × 5 and × 10

**Multiplication** is **repeated addition**. Instead of adding many times, we multiply!

$$3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 4 \times 3 = 12$$

---

## The 2 times table

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 × 2 | = 2 |
| 2 × 2 | = 4 |
| 3 × 2 | = 6 |
| 4 × 2 | = 8 |
| 5 × 2 | = 10 |
| 6 × 2 | = 12 |
| 7 × 2 | = 14 |
| 8 × 2 | = 16 |
| 9 × 2 | = 18 |
| 10 × 2 | = 20 |

> [!TIP]
> The 2 times table always gives **even** numbers: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, …

---

## The 5 times table

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 × 5 | = 5 |
| 2 × 5 | = 10 |
| 3 × 5 | = 15 |
| 4 × 5 | = 20 |
| 5 × 5 | = 25 |
| 6 × 5 | = 30 |
| 7 × 5 | = 35 |
| 8 × 5 | = 40 |
| 9 × 5 | = 45 |
| 10 × 5 | = 50 |

> [!TIP]
> The 5 times table always ends in **0** or **5**.

---

## The 10 times table

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 × 10 | = 10 |
| 2 × 10 | = 20 |
| 3 × 10 | = 30 |
| 4 × 10 | = 40 |
| 5 × 10 | = 50 |
| 6 × 10 | = 60 |
| 7 × 10 | = 70 |
| 8 × 10 | = 80 |
| 9 × 10 | = 90 |
| 10 × 10 | = 100 |

> [!NOTE]
> Multiplying by 10 is simple — just add a **zero**! $7 \times 10 = 70$
