# Comparatives and Superlatives

## Introduction

We use **comparatives** to compare two things and **superlatives** to say which is the most extreme in a group. These are essential for describing and discussing almost anything!

---

## Comparatives — Comparing Two Things

### Short adjectives (1–2 syllables) → add **-er + than**

| Adjective | Comparative | Example |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **tall** | tall**er than** | Tom is **taller than** Sam. |
| **short** | short**er than** | Sam is **shorter than** Tom. |
| **fast** | fast**er than** | A cheetah is **faster than** a lion. |
| **slow** | slow**er than** | A tortoise is **slower than** a rabbit. |
| **old** | old**er than** | My dad is **older than** my mum. |
| **young** | young**er than** | I am **younger than** my sister. |
| **big** | bigg**er than** | An elephant is **bigger than** a horse. |
| **hot** | hott**er than** | Summer is **hotter than** spring. |

### Long adjectives (3+ syllables) → **more + adjective + than**

| Adjective | Comparative | Example |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **beautiful** | **more beautiful than** | Roses are **more beautiful than** weeds. |
| **expensive** | **more expensive than** | Gold is **more expensive than** silver. |
| **interesting** | **more interesting than** | Fiction is **more interesting than** that report. |
| **comfortable** | **more comfortable than** | This sofa is **more comfortable than** that chair. |

---

## Superlatives — The Most Extreme

### Short adjectives → **the + adjective + -est**

| Adjective | Superlative | Example |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **tall** | the **tallest** | She is **the tallest** in the class. |
| **fast** | the **fastest** | The cheetah is **the fastest** land animal. |
| **big** | the **biggest** | The blue whale is **the biggest** animal. |
| **old** | the **oldest** | My grandpa is **the oldest** in the family. |

### Long adjectives → **the most + adjective**

| Adjective | Superlative | Example |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **expensive** | the **most expensive** | That's **the most expensive** car in the shop. |
| **beautiful** | the **most beautiful** | It's **the most beautiful** place I've seen. |
| **interesting** | the **most interesting** | This is **the most interesting** book ever! |

---

## Spelling Rules for Comparatives/Superlatives

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Most adjectives → add **-er / -est** | tall → taller / tallest |
| Adjective ends in **-e** → add **-r / -st** | nice → nicer / nicest |
| Adjective ends in consonant-vowel-consonant → double the consonant | big → bigger / biggest |
| Adjective ends in **-y** → change to **-ier / -iest** | happy → happier / happiest |

---

## Irregular Forms

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **good** | **better than** | **the best** |
| **bad** | **worse than** | **the worst** |
| **much / many** | **more than** | **the most** |
| **little** | **less than** | **the least** |
| **far** | **farther / further than** | **the farthest / furthest** |

---

## Useful Structures

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| A is [comparative] than B | London is **bigger than** Lisbon. |
| A is the [superlative] in/of … | The Amazon is **the longest** river in South America. |
| A is as … as B | A cat is **as fast as** a small dog. |
| A is not as … as B | A bicycle is **not as fast as** a car. |
