# The Human Body

Our body has systems that work together to keep us alive and healthy. Let's explore them!

---

## The Digestive System

The digestive system breaks food down into energy for our body.

**The journey of food:**

1. **Mouth** — chewing and mixing with saliva
2. **Oesophagus** — the tube that carries food to the stomach
3. **Stomach** — mixes and starts digesting the food
4. **Small intestine** — absorbs nutrients into the blood
5. **Large intestine** — absorbs water; forms waste
6. **Anus** — removes waste from the body

> [!NOTE]
> The small intestine is about **6 metres long**! It is folded many times to fit inside the abdomen.

---

## The Respiratory System

The respiratory system supplies the body with oxygen and removes carbon dioxide.

**How we breathe:**

1. We breathe in through the **nose** or mouth
2. Air travels down the **trachea** (windpipe)
3. It enters the **lungs** through the bronchi
4. In the **alveoli**, oxygen passes into the blood
5. We breathe out **carbon dioxide**

| Phase | What enters or leaves |
|---|---|
| Breathing in (inhalation) | Air rich in oxygen (O₂) enters |
| Breathing out (exhalation) | Air rich in carbon dioxide (CO₂) leaves |

> [!TIP]
> Breathe in slowly and count to 4. Now breathe out and count to 6. This exercise calms the body and mind!

---

## The Circulatory System

The circulatory system carries blood, oxygen and nutrients throughout the body.

- The **heart** is the engine of the system — it beats about **70 to 100 times per minute**
- Blood travels through **arteries** (away from the heart) and **veins** (back to the heart)
- Blood carries **oxygen and nutrients** to every cell in the body

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The heart never rests! Over a lifetime it beats more than **2.5 billion times**. That is why we need to look after it with exercise and a healthy diet.

---

## The Three Systems Working Together

These systems are all connected:

- The digestive system provides **nutrients** to the blood
- The respiratory system provides **oxygen** to the blood
- The circulatory system delivers **nutrients and oxygen** to the whole body

1. **Feel your heartbeat**: place your hand on your chest. How many times does it beat in 15 seconds? Multiply by 4 to find your rate per minute.
2. **Before and after exercise**: count your heartbeats at rest, run for 2 minutes, then count again. What changed?
3. **Draw the journey**: make a simple drawing of the digestive system with the name of each organ.
4. **Breathing experiment**: blow up a balloon — you are using your lungs to push out air containing CO₂!
