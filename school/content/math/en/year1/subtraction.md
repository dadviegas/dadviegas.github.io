# Subtraction

**Subtracting** means **taking away**. The result is called the **difference**.

$$\text{minuend} - \text{subtrahend} = \text{difference}$$

---

## What is subtraction?

You have **8 sweets** and eat **3**. How many are left?

$$8 - 3 = 5$$

**5 sweets** remain. 🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬 ~~🍬🍬🍬~~

---

## Subtraction on the number line

To calculate $9 - 4$, start at **9** and take **4 jumps** back:

$$9 \xrightarrow{-1} 8 \xrightarrow{-1} 7 \xrightarrow{-1} 6 \xrightarrow{-1} 5$$

Therefore: $9 - 4 = 5$

## The link between addition and subtraction

Addition and subtraction are **inverse** operations. We can check a subtraction with addition:

$$7 - 3 = 4 \quad \Leftrightarrow \quad 4 + 3 = 7$$

> [!NOTE]
> If $a - b = c$, then $c + b = a$. This helps you check your work!
