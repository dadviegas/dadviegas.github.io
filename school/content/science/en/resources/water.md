# The Water Cycle

## Water: The Magic Substance!

Water is one of the most amazing substances on Earth! It is the only natural substance that exists in **three different states** on our planet — and it is always moving and changing! Life as we know it is completely impossible without water. 🌍💙

---

## The Three States of Water

| State | Form | Temperature | Examples |
|-------|------|-------------|---------|
| 🧊 **Solid** | Rigid, fixed shape | Below 0°C | Ice, snow, hail |
| 💧 **Liquid** | Flows, takes shape of container | Between 0°C and 100°C | Rain, rivers, sea |
| 💨 **Gas** | Invisible, spreads out | Above 100°C | Water vapour, clouds |

---

## Changes of State

Water changes state when temperature rises or falls:

```
         MELTING               EVAPORATION
🧊 Solid ────────→ 💧 Liquid ──────────→ 💨 Gas
         ←────────             ←──────────
        FREEZING               CONDENSATION

         🧊 ──────────────────────────────→ 💨
                     SUBLIMATION
```

| Change | From | To | Temperature |
|--------|------|----|-------------|
| 🔥 **Melting** | Solid | Liquid | 0°C (heating) |
| ❄️ **Freezing** | Liquid | Solid | 0°C (cooling) |
| 🌡️ **Evaporation** | Liquid | Gas | 100°C (heating) |
| 💧 **Condensation** | Gas | Liquid | Cooling |
| ❄️ **Sublimation** | Solid | Gas | Directly |

> [!TIP]
> Do you see the "steam" rising from a hot cup of tea or boiling pans? That is not actually smoke — it is **water vapour** condensing as it hits cooler air! 💨

## The Water Cycle

Water travels continuously between the Earth's surface and the atmosphere — this journey is called the **water cycle** or **hydrological cycle**!

### The Stages of the Cycle

**1. Evaporation ☀️**
- The sun heats water in oceans, rivers and lakes
- Water turns into **vapour** and rises into the atmosphere
- Plants also release water vapour (transpiration 🌿)

**2. Condensation ☁️**
- Water vapour rises and cools down
- It turns into tiny droplets of water
- This forms **clouds** and **fog**!

**3. Precipitation 🌧️**
- When clouds become too heavy, water falls back down
- It can fall as **rain** 🌧️, **snow** 🌨️, or **hail** 🌩️
- Precipitation happens in different amounts around the world

**4. Run-off and Infiltration 🌊**
- Some water soaks into the ground (**infiltration**) → forms underground water
- Some flows over the surface → forms rivers and lakes
- Water returns to the sea... and the cycle begins again!

```
         ☁️ Clouds (condensation)
        ↗️             ↘️
💨 Vapour               🌧️ Precipitation
  ↑                          ↓
☀️ Evaporation          🌊 Run-off → 🌊 Ocean
  ↑                          ↓
🌊 Oceans, Rivers       🌱 Infiltration → 🪨 Groundwater
```

## Why Is Water So Important?

Water is essential for all life on Earth:

| For what | Why |
|----------|-----|
| 🌱 Plants | Photosynthesis, transporting nutrients |
| 🐟 Animals | Habitat, hydration, reproduction |
| 👤 Humans | Drinking, cooking, hygiene, farming |
| 🌡️ Climate | Regulates the planet's temperature |
| 🏭 Industry | Production, cooling, cleaning |
| ⚡ Energy | Dams produce electricity! |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The human body is made up of about **60–70% water**! We lose water through sweating, urinating and breathing. That is why it is important to drink at least **1.5 litres of water per day**! 💧

---

## How Water Is Distributed on Earth

| Type of water | Percentage |
|--------------|------------|
| 🌊 Oceans (salt water) | 97.5% |
| 🧊 Glaciers and snow | 1.75% |
| 💧 Accessible fresh water | 0.75% |

> [!NOTE]
> Even though Earth is called the "blue planet" 🔵, only **0.75%** of all water is available for us to drink! Most is in the oceans (salty) or locked in glaciers (frozen).

---

## How to Save Water

Fresh water is a **precious and limited** resource. We can all help:

- 🚿 Have short showers (5 minutes maximum)
- 🚰 Turn the tap off while brushing your teeth
- 🌱 Water plants in the early morning (less evaporation)
- 🚗 Do not wash the car with a hosepipe — use a bucket
- 🌧️ Collect rainwater to water the garden
- 🍽️ Only run the dishwasher when it is full
- 💧 Fix dripping taps and leaking pipes

> [!TIP]
> Leaving the tap running while you brush your teeth wastes about **6 litres of water per minute**! By turning it off, you save a huge amount of water every day. 💚
