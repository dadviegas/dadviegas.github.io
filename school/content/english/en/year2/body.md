# The Human Body

## Introduction

Knowing the parts of the body in English is very useful — at the doctor's, in PE class, or when describing someone. Let's learn all the main body parts!

> [!TIP]
> Some body parts only come in pairs: **eyes**, **ears**, **hands**, **feet**, **legs**, **arms**. Others are singular: **nose**, **mouth**, **heart**.

## Saying Something Hurts

Use **"My … hurts"** or **"I have a … ache"**:

| Phrase | Portuguese |
|--------|-----------|
| **My head hurts.** | A minha cabeça dói. |
| **My leg hurts.** | A minha perna dói. |
| **I have a headache.** | Tenho dor de cabeça. |
| **I have a stomachache.** | Tenho dor de barriga. |
| **I have a toothache.** | Tenho dor de dentes. |
| **I have a sore throat.** | Tenho a garganta inflamada. |

> [!TIP]
> At the doctor's: **"Where does it hurt?"** → Point and say **"It hurts here"** or **"My [body part] hurts."**

## Actions with Body Parts

| English | Portuguese |
|---------|-----------|
| **to see** | ver |
| **to hear** | ouvir |
| **to smell** | cheirar |
| **to taste** | saborear / provar |
| **to touch** | tocar |

The five senses:
- **Eyes** → we see
- **Ears** → we hear
- **Nose** → we smell
- **Tongue** → we taste
- **Skin** → we touch
