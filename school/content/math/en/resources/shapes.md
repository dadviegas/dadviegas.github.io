# Geometric Shapes

Geometry is everywhere — in windows, balls, boxes! Let's get to know the shapes. 🌍

---

## 2D Shapes (Flat Shapes)

2D shapes are **flat** — they have length and width, but no height.

### Table of 2D Shapes

| Shape | Emoji | Sides | Vertices | Angles | Fun Fact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Square | 🟥 | 4 equal | 4 | 4 right angles (90°) | All sides are equal |
| Rectangle | 🟦 | 4 (2 pairs equal) | 4 | 4 right angles (90°) | Opposite sides are equal |
| Triangle | 🔺 | 3 | 3 | 3 (add up to 180°) | The most rigid shape |
| Circle | ⭕ | 0 (curved) | 0 | — | All points equidistant from centre |
| Rhombus | 🔷 | 4 equal | 4 | 2 pairs equal | Like a tilted square |
| Trapezium | — | 4 | 4 | 4 | One pair of parallel sides |
| Pentagon | ⬠ | 5 | 5 | 5 (add up to 540°) | Shape of the STOP sign |
| Hexagon | ⬡ | 6 | 6 | 6 (add up to 720°) | Shape of honeycomb cells 🍯 |

---

### Types of Triangle

**By sides:**

| Type | Sides | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Equilateral** | 3 equal sides | A perfect triangle 🔺 |
| **Isosceles** | 2 equal, 1 different | An arrow pointing up ⬆️ |
| **Scalene** | All sides different | A "wonky" triangle 📐 |

**By angles:**

| Type | Angles |
|---|---|
| **Acute** | All angles < 90° |
| **Right-angled** | One angle = 90° |
| **Obtuse** | One angle > 90° |

---

### Circle — Key Parts ⭕

| Part | Description |
|---|---|
| **Centre** | The middle point |
| **Radius (r)** | Distance from centre to the edge |
| **Diameter (d)** | Distance from edge to edge through the centre |
| **Circumference** | The curved line that forms the circle |

$$d = 2r$$

## 3D Shapes (Solid Shapes)

3D shapes have **length, width and height** — they are three-dimensional objects!

### Table of 3D Shapes

| Shape | Emoji | Faces | Edges | Vertices | Real-life example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cube** | 🧊 | 6 | 12 | 8 | Dice, Rubik's Cube |
| **Cuboid** | 📦 | 6 | 12 | 8 | Shoebox, brick |
| **Sphere** | 🔴 | 0 (curved) | 0 | 0 | Ball, orange |
| **Cylinder** | 🥫 | 3 (2 flat + 1 curved) | 2 | 0 | Tin can, glass |
| **Cone** | 🍦 | 2 (1 flat + 1 curved) | 1 | 1 | Ice cream cone, party hat |
| **Pyramid** | 🔺 | 5 (square-based) | 8 | 5 | Egyptian pyramids |

---

### Euler's Formula

For all solid shapes with flat faces (polyhedra):

$$\text{Faces} + \text{Vertices} - \text{Edges} = 2$$

**Check with a cube:**

$$6 + 8 - 12 = 2 \checkmark$$

**Check with a square pyramid:**

$$5 + 5 - 8 = 2 \checkmark$$

## Important Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Side** | A straight line that makes up a shape |
| **Vertex** | A corner where two sides meet |
| **Angle** | The opening between two sides at a vertex |
| **Face** | A flat surface of a solid shape |
| **Edge** | The line where two faces meet |
| **Parallel** | Lines that never cross |
| **Perpendicular** | Lines that cross at a right angle (90°) |

## Where Can You See These Shapes?

- 🟥 **Square** — tiles, chessboard, sticky notes
- 🟦 **Rectangle** — doors, windows, books, screens
- 🔺 **Triangle** — rooftops, traffic signs, pizza slices
- ⭕ **Circle** — wheels, clocks, coins
- ⬡ **Hexagon** — honeycomb, some nuts and bolts
- 📦 **Cuboid** — boxes, bricks, fridges
- 🔴 **Sphere** — balls, planets, balloons
- 🥫 **Cylinder** — cans, cups, batteries
