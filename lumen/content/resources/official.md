## Stay current

The model line moves fast. Set a recurring reminder:

- Once a quarter: skim the **What's new** section of the docs.
- Once a month: re-check **Prompt caching** and **Tool use** for new features.
- When a new model ships: read the model card, run your evals against it, decide if you migrate.
