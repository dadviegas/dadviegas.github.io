# Spelling

Good spelling in Portuguese requires knowing the rules for **accents**, **digraphs**, and **tricky letter combinations**.

---

## Accents (*Acentos*)

Portuguese uses three accent marks:

| Accent | Name | Effect | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| ´ | Acute (*agudo*) | open vowel, stress | *café*, *pé*, *óculos* |
| ^ | Circumflex (*circunflexo*) | closed vowel, stress | *avô*, *mês*, *pêssego* |
| ` | Grave (*grave*) | used only with *à* (contraction) | *à*, *àquele* |

### Tilde (*til*) ~
Marks a nasal vowel: *mão*, *irmão*, *atenção*, *nação*

---

## Accentuation Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Words are accented based on their **stress pattern** (*tonicidade*).

| Type | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Oxytone** (*oxítona*) — stress on last syllable | accent if ends in -a(s), -e(s), -o(s), -em, -ens | *café*, *inglês*, *avó* |
| **Paroxytone** (*paroxítona*) — stress on 2nd-to-last | accent if **not** ending in -a(s), -e(s), -o(s), -am, -em | *fácil*, *árvore*, *júri* |
| **Proparoxytone** (*proparoxítona*) — stress on 3rd-to-last | **always** accented | *médico*, *lâmpada*, *público* |

---

## Digraphs (*Dígrafos*)

Two letters that make **one sound**:

| Digraph | Sound | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **lh** | like *ll* in million | *filho*, *folha*, *palha* |
| **nh** | like *ny* in canyon | *ninho*, *manhã*, *vinho* |
| **ch** | like *sh* | *chave*, *chuva*, *chocolate* |
| **ss** | strong /s/ | *pássaro*, *osso*, *passar* |
| **rr** | strong /r/ | *carro*, *terra*, *ferro* |

---

## Tricky Spellings

### *ç* vs *ss* vs *c* vs *s*
All can make the /s/ sound — the spelling depends on the word:
- **ç** — before *a*, *o*, *u*: *açúcar*, *coração*, *almoço*
- **ss** — between vowels: *passo*, *osso*, *massa*
- **c** — before *e* or *i*: *cidade*, *cebola*, *cedo*

### *x* spellings
The letter *x* can sound like /sh/, /ks/, /s/, or /z/:
- /sh/: *caixa*, *peixe*, *deixar*
- /ks/: *táxi*, *texto*, *fixo*
- /s/: *máximo*, *próximo*

---

## Hyphen (*Hífen*)

Used in compound words and with certain prefixes:

- Compound words: *guarda-chuva*, *fim-de-semana*, *beija-flor*
- With prefix ending in vowel + word starting with *h*, *r*, or *s*: *anti-herói*, *pré-escolar*

> [!NOTE]
> Since the 2009 Orthographic Agreement, many hyphens were removed: *autoestrada* (not *auto-estrada*), *antecâmara* (not *ante-câmara*).

## Try it! 🎯

1. Add the correct accent: *arvore*, *facil*, *cafe*, *avó*, *mao*.
2. Fill in the gap with *ç*, *ss*, or *c*: *a\_\_úcar*, *pa\_\_o*, *\_\_idade*, *cora\_\_ão*.
3. Write the correct digraph: *fi\_\_o* (son), *ma\_\_ã* (morning), *\_\_ave* (key).
4. Which of these need a hyphen? *guardachuva*, *autoestrada*, *beija-flor*, *fimdesemana*.
