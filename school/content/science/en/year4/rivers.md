# Rivers & Mountains of Portugal

## Portugal's Amazing Landscape!

Portugal has beautiful and varied landscapes — from high mountain peaks dusted with snow 🏔️ to long sandy beaches 🏖️, from lush green valleys to golden plains. Let's explore the rivers and mountains that shape our country! 🇵🇹

---

## Portugal's Main Rivers

Rivers start in higher areas (source) and flow downhill until they reach the sea or another river (mouth).

| River | Source | Mouth | Passes through |
|-------|--------|-------|---------------|
| 🌊 **Tagus (Tejo)** | Sierra de Albarracín (Spain) | Lisbon (Atlantic Ocean) | Santarém, Lisbon |
| 🌊 **Douro** | Sierra de Urbión (Spain) | Porto (Atlantic Ocean) | Douro region |
| 🌊 **Mondego** | Serra da Estrela | Figueira da Foz | Coimbra |
| 🌊 **Guadiana** | Spain (La Mancha) | Vila Real de Sto. António | Alentejo, Algarve |
| 🌊 **Minho** | Sierra de Meira (Spain) | Caminha | Border with Spain |
| 🌊 **Sado** | Spain (Badajoz) | Setúbal | Alentejo |

> [!NOTE]
> The **Tagus** is the longest river in the Iberian Peninsula! It starts in Spain and flows west across Portugal until it reaches the Atlantic in Lisbon, forming the famous **Tagus Estuary**.

---

## River Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| 🏔️ **Source** | Where the river begins (usually on a mountain) |
| 💧 **Mouth** | Where the river ends (flows into the sea or another river) |
| 🏞️ **Bed/Channel** | The channel through which the river flows |
| 🌊 **Tributary** | A river that flows into another river |
| 🏝️ **Estuary** | The wide mouth where fresh water meets the sea |
| 🏞️ **Valley** | A low-lying area formed by a river |
| 🔵 **Drainage basin** | All the land drained by a river and its tributaries |

## The River Tagus in Detail

The Tagus is the **most important river in Portugal**:

- 📏 Total length: ~1,007 km (278 km in Portugal)
- 🏙️ Cities: Santarém, Lisbon
- 🌉 Flows through Lisbon — that is why we have the Ponte 25 de Abril and Ponte Vasco da Gama!
- 🐦 The Tagus estuary is an important area for migratory birds

---

## The River Douro

The Douro is famous for its **vineyard-covered valleys**:

- 📏 Length in Portugal: ~213 km
- 🍷 The **Alto Douro Wine Region** is a UNESCO World Heritage Site!
- 🏙️ Flows into the sea at Porto — the city is named after the river!
- 🚢 Traditional **rabelo boats** once transported wine barrels to Porto

---

## The River Mondego

The only major river with both its **source and mouth** in Portugal:

- 🏔️ Starts at Serra da Estrela (the highest point of mainland Portugal)
- 🏛️ Passes through Coimbra — Portugal's historic university city
- 🌾 The Mondego valley is famous for rice growing
- 📏 Length: ~234 km

## Portugal's Mountain Ranges

Mountain ranges are high-altitude areas. The most important in Portugal are:

| Mountain Range | Highest Point | Region | Notable Feature |
|---------------|--------------|--------|----------------|
| 🏔️ **Serra da Estrela** | 1,993 m (Torre) | Centre | Highest point on the mainland! Ski in winter ❄️ |
| 🌲 **Serra do Gerês** | 1,548 m | North | Portugal's only National Park 🦌 |
| 🌊 **Serra da Arrábida** | 501 m | Setúbal | Natural park with stunning beaches 🏖️ |
| 🟤 **Serra de Sintra** | 528 m | Lisbon | Pena Palace, UNESCO Heritage 🏰 |
| 🟤 **Serra de Monchique** | 902 m | Algarve | Brings rain to the Algarve |
| 🟤 **Serra da Lousã** | 1,205 m | Centre | Schist villages, rural tourism |

> [!TIP]
> **Torre**, in Serra da Estrela, is the highest point in mainland Portugal at 1,993 metres! In winter it is covered in snow ❄️ and is the only place in mainland Portugal where you can ski.

## Portugal's Landforms

Portugal has many different types of landscape:

| Landform | Definition | Examples |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| 🏔️ **Mountain range** | A long mountainous area | Estrela, Gerês, Arrábida |
| 🏔️ **Peak** | An isolated high point | Monte de Sta. Luzia |
| 🌄 **Plateau** | A high, flat area | Miranda plateau |
| 🌾 **Plain** | A flat, low-lying area | Alentejo plain |
| 🏞️ **Valley** | A low area between mountains | Douro Valley |
| 🌊 **Coast** | Land beside the sea | Atlantic coast |

## Portugal's Coastline

Portugal has approximately **943 km of Atlantic coastline** (mainland)!

- 🏖️ **Beaches** — beautiful sandy beaches
- 🪨 **Cliffs** — dramatic high rock faces (Algarve!)
- 🏝️ **Estuaries** — wide river mouths (Tagus, Sado)
- 🌊 **Lagoons** — coastal lakes (Óbidos, Albufeira lagoons)
- 🐟 **Rias** — sea inlets in river valleys (Aveiro, Viana)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Portugal is a **maritime nation** at heart! Our history is deeply linked to the sea — the Age of Discovery, cod fishing, commercial ports. The sea is part of our identity! ⚓🇵🇹
