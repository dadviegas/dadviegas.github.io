# Health & Hygiene

Understanding illnesses and knowing how to protect ourselves helps us live more healthily!

---

## Common Illnesses and Prevention

Some illnesses are very common, especially in winter.

| Illness | Cause | Symptoms | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common cold | Virus | Runny nose, sneezing | Wash hands, avoid close contact |
| Flu | Virus | Fever, aches, tiredness | Vaccination, hand hygiene |
| Gastroenteritis | Virus / bacteria | Vomiting, diarrhoea | Food hygiene, hand washing |
| Tooth decay | Bacteria | Toothache | Brush teeth, less sugar |
| Conjunctivitis | Virus / bacteria | Red eyes with discharge | Don't touch eyes with hands |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> When you are ill, stay at home so you don't pass the illness to your classmates. Cover your mouth when you cough or sneeze!

---

## Vaccination

**Vaccines** are one of the greatest achievements of medicine!

- Vaccines teach our immune system to recognise and fight diseases
- Children follow a **National Vaccination Programme** with free vaccines
- Some vaccines protect for life; others need booster doses

> [!NOTE]
> Thanks to vaccines, serious diseases such as **polio** and **smallpox** have almost been wiped out. Vaccines save lives!

**Some vaccines in the Portuguese schedule:**
- BCG — tuberculosis
- Hepatitis B
- Measles, mumps and rubella (MMR)
- Tetanus and diphtheria

## Basic First Aid

First aid is the immediate help we can give before medical professionals arrive.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Small cut | Wash with water, disinfect, apply a plaster |
| Minor burn | Hold under cool running water for 10 minutes |
| Insect sting | Remove the sting, apply ice, do not scratch |
| Nosebleed | Lean the head forward, pinch the nose |
| Choking | Call an adult immediately |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In an emergency in Portugal, the emergency number is **112**. Learn this number by heart!

> [!TIP]
> You should never try to deal with serious situations on your own. Always call an adult or dial 112!
