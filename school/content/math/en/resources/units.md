# Units of Measurement

Units of measurement let us compare and communicate quantities precisely. We use them every single day! ⏱️

---

## Length

Measures distances and sizes.

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent in metres |
|---|---|---|
| Kilometre | km | 1 km = 1,000 m |
| Hectometre | hm | 1 hm = 100 m |
| Decametre | dam | 1 dam = 10 m |
| **Metre** | **m** | **base unit** |
| Decimetre | dm | 1 dm = 0.1 m |
| Centimetre | cm | 1 cm = 0.01 m |
| Millimetre | mm | 1 mm = 0.001 m |

### Most Used Conversions

$$1 \text{ km} = 1{,}000 \text{ m} \qquad 1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm} \qquad 1 \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ mm}$$

> 💡 To convert to a **larger** unit, divide by 10, 100 or 1,000.
> To convert to a **smaller** unit, multiply by 10, 100 or 1,000.

## Mass

Measures how heavy objects are.

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent in grams |
|---|---|---|
| Tonne | t | 1 t = 1,000,000 g |
| Kilogram | kg | 1 kg = 1,000 g |
| Hectogram | hg | 1 hg = 100 g |
| Decagram | dag | 1 dag = 10 g |
| **Gram** | **g** | **base unit** |
| Decigram | dg | 1 dg = 0.1 g |
| Centigram | cg | 1 cg = 0.01 g |
| Milligram | mg | 1 mg = 0.001 g |

### Most Used Conversions

$$1 \text{ t} = 1{,}000 \text{ kg} \qquad 1 \text{ kg} = 1{,}000 \text{ g} \qquad 1 \text{ g} = 1{,}000 \text{ mg}$$

---

## Capacity

Measures volumes of liquids.

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent in litres |
|---|---|---|
| Kilolitre | kl | 1 kl = 1,000 l |
| Hectolitre | hl | 1 hl = 100 l |
| Decalitre | dal | 1 dal = 10 l |
| **Litre** | **l** | **base unit** |
| Decilitre | dl | 1 dl = 0.1 l |
| Centilitre | cl | 1 cl = 0.01 l |
| Millilitre | ml | 1 ml = 0.001 l |

### Most Used Conversions

$$1 \text{ l} = 10 \text{ dl} \qquad 1 \text{ l} = 100 \text{ cl} \qquad 1 \text{ l} = 1{,}000 \text{ ml}$$

> 💡 $1 \text{ l} = 1 \text{ dm}^3 = 1{,}000 \text{ cm}^3$

## Time

| Unit | Equivalent |
|---|---|
| 1 minute (min) | 60 seconds (s) |
| 1 hour (h) | 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds |
| 1 day | 24 hours |
| 1 week | 7 days |
| 1 fortnight | 14 days |
| 1 month | 28, 29, 30 or 31 days |
| 1 quarter | 3 months |
| 1 half-year | 6 months |
| 1 year | 12 months = 52 weeks = 365 days |
| 1 leap year | 366 days |
| 1 decade | 10 years |
| 1 century | 100 years |
| 1 millennium | 1,000 years |

$$1 \text{ h} = 60 \text{ min} \qquad 1 \text{ min} = 60 \text{ s} \qquad 1 \text{ h} = 3{,}600 \text{ s}$$

### Months of the Year

| Month | Days | Month | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | 31 | July | 31 |
| February | 28 (or 29) | August | 31 |
| March | 31 | September | 30 |
| April | 30 | October | 31 |
| May | 31 | November | 30 |
| June | 30 | December | 31 |

> 💡 **Knuckle trick:** Months on "knuckles" (bumps) have 31 days, gaps have 30 (or 28/29)!
