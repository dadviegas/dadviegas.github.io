# Reading & Writing — Year 1

## The Portuguese Alphabet

The Portuguese alphabet has **26 letters**. It looks very similar to the English alphabet!

| Letter | Name (PT) | Letter | Name (PT) |
|--------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| A a | á | N n | ene |
| B b | bê | O o | ó |
| C c | cê | P p | pê |
| D d | dê | Q q | quê |
| E e | é | R r | erre |
| F f | efe | S s | esse |
| G g | gê | T t | tê |
| H h | agá | U u | u |
| I i | i | V v | vê |
| J j | jota | X x | xis |
| L l | ele | Z z | zê |
| M m | eme | | |

> [!NOTE]
> In Portuguese, the letters **K**, **W**, and **Y** exist but are mainly used in foreign words and names.

---

## Vowels and Consonants

Portuguese has the same vowels as English: **A, E, I, O, U**

All other letters are **consonants**.

> [!TIP]
> Portuguese vowels can have accents that change how they sound:
> - **á** (open, like "ah") — *pá* (spade)
> - **â** (closed) — *câmara* (camera)
> - **ã** (nasal) — *mã**ã*** (apple)

---

## Syllables (Sílabas)

A **syllable** is a unit of sound. Every syllable has at least one vowel.

| Word | Syllables | Count |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **ca**-**sa** (house) | ca / sa | 2 |
| **bo**-**la** (ball) | bo / la | 2 |
| **sol** (sun) | sol | 1 |
| **bor**-**bo**-**le**-**ta** (butterfly) | bor/bo/le/ta | 4 |

> [!TIP]
> Clap your hands to count syllables — one clap for each syllable!

---

## Reading Simple Words

In Portuguese, most letters have a **consistent sound** — words are spelled as they sound!

| Portuguese | Sounds like | English meaning |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| *bola* | BO-la | ball |
| *casa* | KA-za | house |
| *mesa* | MEH-za | table |
| *pato* | PA-too | duck |

---

## Writing Your Name

In Portuguese, proper names always start with a **capital letter**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Capital letters (letras maiúsculas) are used for:
> - Names of people: **Ana**, **Tomás**
> - Names of places: **Lisboa** (Lisbon), **Portugal**
> - The beginning of every sentence
