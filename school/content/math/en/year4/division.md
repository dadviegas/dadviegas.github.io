# Division with Remainder

Division is not always exact. When things don't share equally, there is a **remainder**.

$$\text{dividend} = \text{divisor} \times \text{quotient} + \text{remainder}$$

> [!NOTE]
> The **remainder** is always **smaller** than the divisor: $0 \leq \text{remainder} < \text{divisor}$

---

## Short division (bus stop method)

### Example: $85 \div 4$

```
  85 ÷ 4
  ------
  8 ÷ 4 = 2  →  2 × 4 = 8,  remainder 0,  bring down the 5
  5 ÷ 4 = 1  →  1 × 4 = 4,  remainder 1
  ------
  = 21 (remainder 1)
```

- How many times does 4 go into 8? **2 times** (remainder 0). Bring down the 5.
- How many times does 4 go into 5? **1 time** (remainder 1).

$$85 \div 4 = 21 \text{ remainder } 1$$

**Check:** $21 \times 4 + 1 = 84 + 1 = 85$ ✓

---

## Another example: $137 \div 6$

```
  137 ÷ 6
  -------
  12 ÷ 6 = 2  →  2 × 6 = 12,  remainder 0,  bring down the 7
  17 ÷ 6 = 2  →  2 × 6 = 12,  remainder 5
  -------
  = 22 (remainder 5)
```

$$137 \div 6 = 22 \text{ remainder } 5$$

**Check:** $22 \times 6 + 5 = 132 + 5 = 137$ ✓

---

## Exact vs. remainder division

| Division      | Quotient | Remainder | Exact? |
|---------------|----------|-----------|--------|
| $24 \div 4$   | 6        | 0         | ✓ Yes |
| $25 \div 4$   | 6        | 1         | ✗ No  |
| $100 \div 10$ | 10       | 0         | ✓ Yes |
| $103 \div 10$ | 10       | 3         | ✗ No  |
