# Colors

## Introduction

Colors are all around us! Once you know color words in English you can describe almost everything. Let's learn 10 basic colors and how to use them in sentences.

---

## The 10 Basic Colors

| Emoji | Color | Portuguese |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| 🔴 | **red** | vermelho |
| 🔵 | **blue** | azul |
| 🟢 | **green** | verde |
| 🟡 | **yellow** | amarelo |
| 🟠 | **orange** | laranja |
| 🟣 | **purple** | roxo / violeta |
| 🩷 | **pink** | cor-de-rosa |

> [!TIP]
> In English, the color always comes **before** the noun: **red apple**, **blue sky**, **green tree**.

---

## Asking About Colors

**Question:** What color is it?

**Answer:** It's + color.

Examples:
- **What color is the banana?** → **It's yellow!**
- **What color is the sky?** → **It's blue!**
- **What color is your bag?** → **It's red!**

---

## My Favorite Color

Learn to express your favorite color:

> **My favorite color is blue.** — A minha cor favorita é azul.

**Question:** What is your favorite color?
**Answer:** My favorite color is __________.

---

## Color Combinations

| Mix | Result |
|-----|--------|
| red + blue | purple |
| red + yellow | orange |
| blue + yellow | green |
| black + white | grey |

> [!TIP]
> Grey (or gray) is also a common color — it is between black and white!
