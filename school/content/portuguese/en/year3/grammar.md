# Grammar — Year 3

## Word Classes (Classes de Palavras)

Portuguese words are grouped into **classes** based on their function in a sentence.

| Class | Portuguese | What it does | Example |
|-------|------------|-------------|---------|
| Noun | *Nome* | names a person, animal, thing | *livro*, *Pedro*, *cidade* |
| Adjective | *Adjetivo* | describes a noun | *bonito*, *grande*, *azul* |
| Verb | *Verbo* | expresses action or state | *correr*, *ser*, *chover* |
| Determiner | *Determinante* | comes before a noun | *o*, *a*, *um*, *este* |
| Personal pronoun | *Pronome pessoal* | replaces a noun | *eu*, *tu*, *ele*, *nós* |

> [!TIP]
> To identify a word's class, look at its **role in the sentence**, not just its form.

## Degrees of Adjectives (Grau dos Adjetivos)

Adjectives can be used at different **degrees** to compare things.

### Positive (Normal)
> *O Miguel é alto.* — Miguel is tall.

### Comparative

| Type | Structure | Example | English |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Superiority | *mais … do que* | *mais alto do que* o Rui | taller than |
| Equality | *tão … como* | *tão alto como* o João | as tall as |
| Inferiority | *menos … do que* | *menos alto do que* o André | less tall than |

### Superlative

| Type | Example | English |
|------|---------|---------|
| Relative (*o mais…*) | *o mais alto da turma* | the tallest in the class |
| Absolute (*muito / -íssimo*) | *muito alto / altíssimo* | very tall / extremely tall |

> [!NOTE]
> Some adjectives have irregular superlative forms:
> *grande* (big) → *grandíssimo* / *ótimo*
> *bom* (good) → *ótimo* (excellent)
> *mau* (bad) → *péssimo* (terrible)

## Verb Tenses (Tempos Verbais)

### Present Tense (Presente do Indicativo)
Used for actions happening now or regularly.

| Pronoun | *andar* (walk) | *correr* (run) | English |
|---------|---------------|----------------|---------|
| *eu* | *ando* | *corro* | I walk / run |
| *tu* | *andas* | *corres* | you walk / run |
| *ele/ela* | *anda* | *corre* | he/she walks / runs |
| *nós* | *andamos* | *corremos* | we walk / run |
| *eles/elas* | *andam* | *correm* | they walk / run |

### Imperfect Past (Pretérito Imperfeito)
Used for **habitual or ongoing actions in the past** — like "used to" or "was doing" in English.

| Pronoun | *andar* | *correr* | English |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| *eu* | *andava* | *corria* | I used to walk / was walking |
| *tu* | *andavas* | *corrias* | you used to walk |
| *ele/ela* | *andava* | *corria* | he/she used to walk |
| *nós* | *andávamos* | *corríamos* | we used to walk |
| *eles/elas* | *andavam* | *corriam* | they used to walk |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Use the imperfect to describe past habits or background situations: *Quando era criança, brincava todos os dias.* (When I was a child, I used to play every day.)

## Personal Pronouns (Pronomes Pessoais)

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|----------|--------|
| 1st | *eu* (I) | *nós* (we) |
| 2nd | *tu* (you) | *vós* (you all) |
| 3rd | *ele/ela* (he/she) | *eles/elas* (they) |
