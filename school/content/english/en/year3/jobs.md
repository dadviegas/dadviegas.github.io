# Professions and Jobs

## Introduction

Talking about jobs and careers is a common topic in English. In this lesson we learn the names of many professions, how to talk about what people do, and vocabulary related to the world of work.

## Describing Jobs

Use **"He/She is a …"** or **"He/She works as a …"**:

| English | Portuguese |
|---------|-----------|
| **He is a doctor.** | Ele é médico. |
| **She works as a teacher.** | Ela trabalha como professora. |
| **They are nurses.** | Eles são enfermeiros. |
| **I want to be a scientist.** | Eu quero ser cientista. |
| **What do you want to be?** | O que queres ser? |
| **What does your father do?** | O que faz o teu pai? |
| **My mother is a lawyer.** | A minha mãe é advogada. |

> [!TIP]
> In English, we usually say **"He is a doctor"** (with the article **a/an**) when talking about jobs. Use **an** before vowel sounds: *She is **an** engineer*, *He is **an** artist*.

## What Do They Do? — Describing Job Duties

| Job | What they do |
|-----|-------------|
| A teacher | teaches students at school |
| A doctor | treats sick people |
| A firefighter | puts out fires and rescues people |
| A chef | cooks meals in a restaurant |
| A programmer | writes code for computers |
| A pilot | flies aeroplanes |
| A journalist | writes news articles |

Structure: **A [job] + verb + object/complement.**

### Exercise 3 — Write sentences

1. Maria / nurse → ________

### Exercise 4 — My future job

Write a paragraph (5–7 sentences) about the job you would like to do when you grow up. Include:
- What the job is
- Why you like it
- What skills you need
- Where you would work

> [!TIP]
> Some jobs have different words in British and American English: *lawyer* (UK) = *attorney* (US), *mum* (UK) = *mom* (US), *football* (UK) = *soccer* (US). Both are correct!
