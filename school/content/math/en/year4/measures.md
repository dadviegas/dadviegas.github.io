# Measurements — Year 4

In Year 4 we bring together **all** the measurement units we've learned and go even further! We'll master the full family of length, area, mass, and capacity units — and convert between them like pros! ⚡

---

## Length — The Complete Family

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Kilometre** | km | 1 km = 1000 m |
| **Metre** | m | 1 m = 100 cm |
| **Centimetre** | cm | 1 cm = 10 mm |
| **Millimetre** | mm | smallest common unit |

### Full Conversion Table

| From → To | Operation | Example |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| km → m | × 1000 | 3 km = 3000 m |
| m → cm | × 100 | 5 m = 500 cm |
| cm → mm | × 10 | 7 cm = 70 mm |
| m → mm | × 1000 | 2 m = 2000 mm |
| mm → cm | ÷ 10 | 40 mm = 4 cm |
| cm → m | ÷ 100 | 350 cm = 3.5 m |
| m → km | ÷ 1000 | 4500 m = 4.5 km |

$$\text{km} \xrightarrow{\times 1000} \text{m} \xrightarrow{\times 100} \text{cm} \xrightarrow{\times 10} \text{mm}$$

$$\text{mm} \xrightarrow{\div 10} \text{cm} \xrightarrow{\div 100} \text{m} \xrightarrow{\div 1000} \text{km}$$

> [!TIP]
> Moving to a **smaller unit** = **multiply**. Moving to a **bigger unit** = **divide**. Think: you need MORE small pieces to make the same length! 📏

## Area Units

Area is measured in **square units** — square centimetres or square metres.

$$1 \text{ m}^2 = 10{,}000 \text{ cm}^2$$

$$1 \text{ cm}^2 = 100 \text{ mm}^2$$

| Unit | Symbol | About the size of... |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| **Square millimetre** | mm² | Tip of a pencil ✏️ |
| **Square centimetre** | cm² | Fingernail 💅 |
| **Square metre** | m² | A single floor tile 🟦 |
| **Square kilometre** | km² | A small village 🏘️ |

> [!NOTE]
> The area of a football pitch is about **7,000 m²**. The area of Portugal is about **92,000 km²**! Huge difference! ⚽🇵🇹

## Mass — The Full Picture

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Milligram** | mg | 1 mg = 0.001 g |
| **Gram** | g | 1 g = 1000 mg |
| **Kilogram** | kg | 1 kg = 1000 g |
| **Tonne** | t | 1 t = 1000 kg |

$$\text{mg} \xrightarrow{\times 1000} \text{g} \xrightarrow{\times 1000} \text{kg} \xrightarrow{\times 1000} \text{t}$$

### Real-world Mass Examples

| Object | Mass |
|--------|------|
| A grain of sand 🏖️ | about 1 mg |
| A paperclip 📎 | about 1 g |
| An apple 🍎 | about 200 g |
| A bag of sugar 🍬 | 1 kg |
| A horse 🐴 | about 500 kg |
| A small car 🚗 | about 1 t (1000 kg) |
| A blue whale 🐋 | about 150 t |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tonnes** are used for very heavy things like lorries, ships, and elephants! 
> $$1 \text{ tonne} = 1000 \text{ kg} = 1{,}000{,}000 \text{ g}$$ 🐘

## Capacity — Litres and Their Family

| Unit | Symbol | Equivalent |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Millilitre** | mL | smallest unit |
| **Centilitre** | cL | 1 cL = 10 mL |
| **Decilitre** | dL | 1 dL = 100 mL |
| **Litre** | L | 1 L = 1000 mL |

$$1 \text{ L} = 10 \text{ dL} = 100 \text{ cL} = 1000 \text{ mL}$$

### Conversion Examples

| Convert | Calculation | Answer |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| 2.5 L to mL | 2.5 × 1000 | 2500 mL |
| 750 mL to L | 750 ÷ 1000 | 0.75 L |
| 3 dL to mL | 3 × 100 | 300 mL |
| 500 cL to L | 500 ÷ 100 | 5 L |

> [!TIP]
> Look at **drinks packaging**! You'll find cL (centilitres) on wine and juice bottles (e.g. 75 cL = 750 mL = 0.75 L). Now you can decode any label! 🍷

---

## Comparing All Units at a Glance

| Measurement | Smallest → Largest |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Length** | mm → cm → m → km |
| **Area** | mm² → cm² → m² → km² |
| **Mass** | mg → g → kg → t |
| **Capacity** | mL → cL → dL → L |

> [!NOTE]
> The prefix **milli-** always means **÷ 1000**, **centi-** means **÷ 100**, and **kilo-** means **× 1000**. These prefixes work across ALL types of measurement! 🌍

1. **Conversion Race**: Convert these as fast as you can! ⏱️
   - 4.5 km = ___ m
   - 2300 g = ___ kg
   - 0.75 L = ___ mL
   - 3 m = ___ cm = ___ mm

2. **Label Detective**: Look at 5 food or drink packages at home. Write down every unit of measurement you find. Which units appeared the most? 🔍

3. **Area tiling**: Your kitchen floor tiles are 30 cm × 30 cm. What is the area of ONE tile in cm²? If a room has 20 tiles, what is the total floor area?

4. **Heavy Vehicles**: Find out the mass of 3 different vehicles (car, lorry, bicycle). Write them in kg AND in tonnes. Which is heaviest?

5. **Capacity challenge**: You have a 2 L bottle of orange juice. You pour 350 mL for breakfast and 250 mL for lunch. How many mL are left? Convert your answer to litres! 🍊
