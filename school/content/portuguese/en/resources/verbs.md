# Verb Conjugation

Verbs are the heart of a sentence! They change form according to the **person**, **number** and **tense**. Let's learn how to conjugate regular verbs. 💪

---

## The Three Conjugations

In Portuguese, verbs are grouped into **three conjugations** according to their ending in the infinitive:

| Conjugation | Ending | Example |
|:-----------:|:------:|---------|
| **1st** | **-ar** | falar (to speak) 🗣️ |
| **2nd** | **-er** | comer (to eat) 🍽️ |
| **3rd** | **-ir** | partir (to leave) ✈️ |

> [!NOTE]
> Regular verbs always follow the same pattern within their conjugation. Irregular verbs (ser/to be, ter/to have, ir/to go, fazer/to do) change in unexpected ways — those must be memorised!

---

## 1st Conjugation — Verb FALAR (to speak) — -ar

### Present Tense (Presente do Indicativo)

*What I am doing now / what I usually do*

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **falo** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **falas** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você (he / she / you) | **fala** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **falamos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **falais** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês (they / you all) | **falam** |

> Example: Eu **falo** português. 🇵🇹 Tu **falas** inglês. 🇬🇧

### Simple Past (Pretérito Perfeito do Indicativo)

*What happened and finished in the past*

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **falei** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **falaste** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **falou** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **falámos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **falastes** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **falaram** |

> Example: Yesterday I **falei** (spoke) with the teacher. 📞 He **falou** (spoke) very loudly.

### Future Tense (Futuro do Indicativo)

*What will happen*

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **falarei** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **falarás** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **falará** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **falaremos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **falareis** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **falarão** |

> Example: Tomorrow I **falarei** (will speak) with the headteacher. 📅 They **falarão** (will speak) at the theatre.

## 2nd Conjugation — Verb COMER (to eat) — -er

### Present Tense

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **como** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **comes** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **come** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **comemos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **comeis** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **comem** |

> Example: Eu **como** sopa ao almoço. 🍲 (I eat soup at lunch.) She **come** (eats) fruit every day. 🍎

### Simple Past

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **comi** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **comeste** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **comeu** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **comemos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **comestes** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **comeram** |

> Example: Yesterday I **comi** (ate) pizza. 🍕 They **comeram** (ate) all the cake. 🎂

### Future Tense

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **comerei** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **comerás** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **comerá** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **comeremos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **comereis** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **comerão** |

> Example: Tonight I **comerei** (will eat) with the family. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Tomorrow we **comeremos** (will eat) bacalhau (salt cod). 🐟

## 3rd Conjugation — Verb PARTIR (to leave / to depart) — -ir

### Present Tense

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **parto** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **partes** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **parte** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **partimos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **partis** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **partem** |

> Example: The plane **parte** (leaves) at 10am. ✈️ We **partimos** (leave) tomorrow morning.

### Simple Past

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **parti** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **partiste** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **partiu** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **partimos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **partistes** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **partiram** |

> Example: João **partiu** (left) last night. 🌙 They **partiram** (left) on holiday. 🏖️

### Future Tense

| Person | Pronoun | Verb form |
|--------|---------|:---------:|
| 1st singular | eu (I) | **partirei** |
| 2nd singular | tu (you) | **partirás** |
| 3rd singular | ele / ela / você | **partirá** |
| 1st plural | nós (we) | **partiremos** |
| 2nd plural | vós (you all) | **partireis** |
| 3rd plural | eles / elas / vocês | **partirão** |

> Example: In summer we **partiremos** (will leave) for the Algarve. ☀️

## Comparison Table — All Three Tenses

| Tense | 1st (-ar) falar | 2nd (-er) comer | 3rd (-ir) partir |
|-------|:--------------:|:--------------:|:---------------:|
| **Present** (I) | falo | como | parto |
| **Past** (I) | falei | comi | parti |
| **Future** (I) | falarei | comerei | partirei |

> [!TIP]
> To conjugate a regular verb, remove the **-ar / -er / -ir** ending and add the tense endings to the verb stem:
> - fal**ar** → stem: **fal-**
> - com**er** → stem: **com-**
> - part**ir** → stem: **part-**

## Most Common Irregular Verbs

These verbs **do not follow the rules** — they must be memorised!

| Infinitive | Present (I) | Past (I) | Future (I) |
|:----------:|:-----------:|:--------:|:----------:|
| **ser** (to be) | sou | fui | serei |
| **ter** (to have) | tenho | tive | terei |
| **ir** (to go) | vou | fui | irei |
| **fazer** (to do/make) | faço | fiz | farei |
| **vir** (to come) | venho | vim | virei |
| **poder** (to be able) | posso | pude | poderei |
| **querer** (to want) | quero | quis | quererei |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> "ser" (to be) and "ir" (to go) share the same past tense form: **fui**! Context tells us which is which:
> - Eu **fui** médico. (I was a doctor — ser)
> - Eu **fui** ao mercado. (I went to the market — ir) 🛒
