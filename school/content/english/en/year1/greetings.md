# Greetings in English

## Introduction

Greetings are the first words we use when we meet someone. In English there are many ways to say hello and goodbye depending on the time of day and how well you know the person. Let's learn them all!

---

## Saying Hello

| Expression | When to use |
|------------|-------------|
| **Hello** | Any time of day — always polite |
| **Hi** | Informal — with friends and family |
| **Hey** | Very casual — close friends |

> [!TIP]
> **Hello** is the safest greeting to use with anyone — a teacher, a stranger, or a friend!

---

## Time-of-Day Greetings

Use these greetings depending on what time it is:

| Expression | Meaning | Time |
|------------|---------|------|
| **Good morning** | Bom dia | Until noon |
| **Good afternoon** | Boa tarde | Noon until 6 pm |
| **Good evening** | Boa noite (arriving) | After 6 pm |
| **Good night** | Boa noite (leaving/sleeping) | At bedtime only |

> [!TIP]
> **Good evening** is said when you *arrive* somewhere at night. **Good night** is only said when you *leave* or go to sleep!

## Saying Goodbye

| Expression | Portuguese equivalent |
|------------|-----------------------|
| **Goodbye** | Adeus |
| **Bye / Bye bye** | Tchau |
| **See you later** | Até logo |
| **See you tomorrow** | Até amanhã |
| **Take care** | Cuida-te |

---

## Asking "How are you?"

The most common question after a greeting is:

> **How are you?** — Como estás?

### Common replies:

| Reply | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **I'm fine, thank you!** | Estou bem, obrigado/a! |
| **I'm very well!** | Estou muito bem! |
| **I'm okay.** | Estou bem / mais ou menos. |
| **Not bad!** | Não está mau! |
| **I'm great!** | Estou ótimo/a! |

> [!TIP]
> After answering, it is polite to ask back: **"And you?"** — E tu?

---

## Example Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 — Morning at school

**Alice:** Good morning, Sam!
**Sam:** Good morning, Alice! How are you?
**Alice:** I'm fine, thank you! And you?
**Sam:** I'm very well, thanks!

---

### Dialogue 2 — Meeting a friend in the afternoon

**Tom:** Hi, Emma!
**Emma:** Hello, Tom! How are you?
**Tom:** I'm great! And you?
**Emma:** I'm okay. Bye, Tom! See you tomorrow!
**Tom:** Bye bye, Emma! Take care!
