# Measurements — Year 2

Now that we know about comparing lengths, let's use **proper units** to measure things accurately! This year we'll measure length, mass, time, and money like real scientists! 🔬

---

## Length — Centimetres and Metres

We measure length using **centimetres (cm)** and **metres (m)**.

$$1 \text{ metre} = 100 \text{ centimetres}$$

$$1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm}$$

| Unit | About the size of... | Best for measuring... |
|------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Centimetre (cm)** | Your fingernail width | Pencils, books, hands |
| **Metre (m)** | A big step | Rooms, tall people, cars |

> [!TIP]
> Your **hand span** (thumb to little finger) is about **15–20 cm**. Your full **arm span** is about **1 metre**! 🙌

```
0 cm ────────── 5 cm ────────── 10 cm ────────── 15 cm
     |||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||
```

### Measuring with a Ruler
1. Line up the **zero end** of the ruler with one end of the object
2. Read the number at the **other end**
3. That's your measurement in **cm**!

> [!NOTE]
> Always check the units! **5 cm** and **5 m** are very different — one is the size of a thumb, the other is as tall as a room! 📏🏠

## Mass — Kilograms and Grams

We measure how heavy something is using **kilograms (kg)** and **grams (g)**.

$$1 \text{ kilogram} = 1000 \text{ grams}$$

$$1 \text{ kg} = 1000 \text{ g}$$

| Unit | About the weight of... |
|------|------------------------|
| **Gram (g)** | A small paperclip 📎 |
| **Kilogram (kg)** | A bag of sugar 🍬 |

| Item | Approximate Mass |
|------|-----------------|
| Apple 🍎 | about 150 g |
| Bag of flour | 1 kg |
| New pencil ✏️ | about 5 g |
| School bag 🎒 | about 3–5 kg |
| Cat 🐱 | about 4 kg |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> We **weigh** things using **scales**. There are kitchen scales (for grams) and bathroom scales (for kilograms)! ⚖️

## Time — Hours and Half-Hours

### Reading the Clock

| Long hand position | Time |
|--------------------|------|
| Points to **12** | Exact hour (e.g. 3 o'clock) |
| Points to **6** | Half past (e.g. half past 3) |

$$\text{Half past 4} = \text{4 hours and 30 minutes} = \text{4:30}$$

> [!TIP]
> When the long hand points to **6**, half an hour has passed since the last full hour. "Half past" means **30 minutes after** the hour! ⏰

### Units of Time

$$1 \text{ minute} = 60 \text{ seconds}$$

$$1 \text{ hour} = 60 \text{ minutes}$$

$$1 \text{ day} = 24 \text{ hours}$$

$$1 \text{ week} = 7 \text{ days}$$

## Euros and Cents — Making Change

$$1 \text{ euro} = 100 \text{ cents}$$

### Adding Coins

If you have:
- 1 coin of 50c
- 1 coin of 20c
- 1 coin of 10c

$$50 + 20 + 10 = 80 \text{ cents} = 0.80 \text{ euros}$$

### Making Change

If something costs **35c** and you pay with **50c**:

$$\text{Change} = 50 - 35 = 15 \text{ cents}$$

| Costs | You Pay | Change |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 40c | 50c | 10c |
| 75c | €1 | 25c |
| €1.20 | €2 | 80c |

> [!TIP]
> **Count up** to find change! If something costs 35c and you pay 50c: count up 35 → 40 → 50, that's 5 + 10 = **15c change**! 🧮

## Try It!

1. **Measure your bedroom**: Use a ruler to measure the length of your desk, your bed, and your favourite book. Write them down in **cm**!

2. **Kitchen weigh-in**: With a grown-up's help, use kitchen scales to find something that weighs about **100g**, **500g**, and **1kg**.

3. **Half-past practice**: Ask someone to set a clock to 3:30, 7:30, and 12:30. Can you say the time correctly each time?

4. **Birthday countdown**: Look at a calendar. How many **months** and **days** until your next birthday? 🎂

5. **Shop game**: Ask a grown-up to play "shop" with you. Price items at 30c, 55c, and €1.20. Practice paying and giving change! 🛒
