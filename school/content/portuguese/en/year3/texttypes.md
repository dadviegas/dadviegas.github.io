# Text Types — Year 3

## Narrative Text (Texto Narrativo)

A **narrative text** tells a story with characters, a setting, and a sequence of events.

**Elements of a narrative:**

| Element | Portuguese | Question it answers |
|---------|------------|-------------------|
| Narrator | *Narrador* | Who is telling the story? |
| Characters | *Personagens* | Who acts in the story? |
| Setting | *Espaço* | Where does it happen? |
| Time | *Tempo* | When does it happen? |
| Plot | *Ação* | What happens? |

> [!TIP]
> Use time connectors to organise your narrative: *primeiro* (first), *depois* (then), *entretanto* (meanwhile), *mais tarde* (later), *de repente* (suddenly), *por fim* (finally).

---

## Descriptive Text (Texto Descritivo)

A **descriptive text** presents the characteristics of a person, animal, place, or object.

**Tips for a good description:**
- Use **adjectives** to characterise
- Engage the **five senses** — what can be seen, heard, smelled, touched, or tasted?
- Move from the general to the specific

**Example:**
> *A minha escola é um edifício branco e alegre. Tem janelas largas por onde entra a luz do sol. No recreio há uma árvore enorme que nos dá sombra no verão.*
> (My school is a white, cheerful building. It has wide windows where the sunlight comes in. In the playground there is a huge tree that shades us in summer.)

> [!NOTE]
> In a description there is **no action** — you are simply saying what something is like.

---

## Instructional Text (Texto Instrucional)

An **instructional text** explains how to do something with clear, ordered steps.

**Structure:**
1. Title (*Título*) — what you will make or do
2. Materials (*Material*) — what you need
3. Steps (*Passos*) — numbered instructions with verbs in the **imperative** or **infinitive**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Verbs in instructional texts are usually in the **imperative** (*dobra* — fold, *corta* — cut) or the **infinitive** (*dobrar*, *cortar*).

## Poetic Text (Texto Poético)

**Poetry** uses creative language to express emotions and ideas.

**Key features:**

| Feature | Portuguese | Explanation |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| Line | *Verso* | each line of the poem |
| Stanza | *Estrofe* | a group of lines |
| Rhyme | *Rima* | lines ending with the same sound |
| Rhythm | *Ritmo* | the musical beat |
| Simile | *Comparação* | comparing using *como* (like/as) |
| Metaphor | *Metáfora* | describing something as something else |

**Simile example:** *A lua é **como** uma lanterna no céu.* (The moon is **like** a lantern in the sky.)

**Metaphor example:** *A lua é uma lanterna no céu.* (The moon **is** a lantern in the sky.)

> [!TIP]
> You don't always need rhyme — a poem can simply have rhythm and vivid images!
