# Geometry — Year 1

Welcome to the world of **shapes**! Shapes are everywhere around us — in our homes, in nature, and even in our food! 🌍

---

## 2D Shapes (Flat Shapes)

**2D** means the shape is **flat** — it only has length and width, no thickness.

| Shape | Name | Sides | Corners |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| 🟥 | **Square** | 4 equal sides | 4 |
| 🟦 | **Rectangle** | 2 long + 2 short sides | 4 |
| 🔺 | **Triangle** | 3 sides | 3 |
| ⭕ | **Circle** | 0 (curved!) | 0 |

> [!NOTE]
> A **square** is a special rectangle where ALL four sides are the same length! 🌟

## Straight Lines and Curved Lines

Shapes are made up of **lines** — and lines come in two types:

### Straight Lines
- They go in **one direction** without bending
- Squares, rectangles, and triangles have **straight sides**
- Also called **line segments** when they have two endpoints

### Curved Lines
- They **bend** smoothly
- Circles are made of **one big curved line**

$$\text{Sides of a square} = 4 \text{ straight sides}$$

$$\text{Sides of a circle} = 0 \text{ straight sides, 1 curve}$$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A shape made of **only straight lines** is called a **polygon**! Triangles, squares, and rectangles are all polygons. Circles are NOT polygons. 🎓

## Interior, Exterior, and Boundary

Every closed shape divides space into three parts:

```
         EXTERIOR (outside)
    ┌─────────────────────────┐
    │                         │ ← BOUNDARY (the line itself)
    │      INTERIOR           │
    │       (inside)          │
    └─────────────────────────┘
         EXTERIOR (outside)
```

- **Interior** = the space **inside** the shape 🏠
- **Exterior** = the space **outside** the shape 🌳
- **Boundary** = the **line** that forms the shape 🖊️

> [!TIP]
> Think of the **boundary** as a fence, the **interior** as the garden inside, and the **exterior** as the street outside! 🌷

## Corners (Vertices)

The points where two sides meet are called **corners** or **vertices** (one corner = one **vertex**).

| Shape | Number of Corners |
|-------|-------------------|
| Triangle | 3 corners |
| Square | 4 corners |
| Rectangle | 4 corners |
| Circle | 0 corners |

---

## Sorting Shapes

We can sort shapes by:
- **Number of sides** (3 sides, 4 sides...)
- **Type of lines** (straight or curved)
- **Size** (big or small)

## Try It!

1. **Shape Hunt**: Walk around your home and find **3 rectangles**, **2 circles**, and **1 triangle**. Draw them in your notebook!

2. **Draw & Colour**: Draw a **house** using only shapes you know — use a square for the walls, a triangle for the roof, rectangles for windows, and a circle for the sun!

3. **Inside or Outside?**: Draw a big circle on paper. Put your finger **inside** the circle (interior), then **outside** it (exterior), then on the **line** (boundary)!

4. **Straight or Curved?**: Look at these shapes — triangle, circle, square, rectangle. Which ones have **only straight sides**? Which have a **curved side**?

5. **Count the corners**: Draw a triangle, a square, and a rectangle. Count and write the number of corners in each shape. Which has the most? Which has the fewest?
