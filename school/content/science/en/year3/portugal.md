# Portugal

Portugal is our country! It is located in Europe and has an incredibly rich history, nature and culture.

---

## Location in Europe

Portugal is in the **south-west of Europe**, on the Iberian Peninsula.

- It borders **Spain** to the **north and east**
- To the **west and south** it is bordered by the **Atlantic Ocean**
- The capital city is **Lisbon**
- Portugal also includes the islands of the **Azores** and **Madeira**

> [!NOTE]
> Portugal was one of the first countries to explore the oceans! The Portuguese Age of Discovery, in the 15th and 16th centuries, connected Europe, Africa, Asia and the Americas.

---

## Districts of Mainland Portugal

Mainland Portugal is divided into **18 districts**.

| Region | Districts (examples) |
|---|---|
| North | Viana do Castelo, Braga, Porto, Vila Real, Bragança |
| Centre | Aveiro, Coimbra, Viseu, Guarda, Castelo Branco, Leiria |
| Lisbon and Tagus Valley | Lisbon, Santarém, Setúbal |
| Alentejo | Portalegre, Évora, Beja |
| Algarve | Faro |

> [!TIP]
> The city of **Porto** gave Portugal its name! Portus Cale was a Roman settlement at the mouth of the Douro river.

---

## Relief: Mountains and Plains

Portugal has very varied landscapes — mountains in the north and centre, plains in the south!

**Main mountain ranges (serras):**

| Mountain range | Location | Highest point |
|---|---|---|
| Serra da Estrela | Centre | Torre — 1,993 m (highest point of mainland Portugal) |
| Serra do Gerês | North | 1,545 m |
| Serra de Sintra | Near Lisbon | 529 m |
| Serra de Monchique | Algarve | Fóia — 902 m |

**Main plains:**
- **Alentejo** — a vast plain in the south-centre, with wheat fields and cork oak woodland
- **Lezíria do Tejo** — a fertile floodplain along the Tagus river

> [!NOTE]
> The highest point of **all Portuguese territory** is Pico, on Pico Island in the Azores, at **2,351 metres**!

---

## Main Rivers

Portugal's rivers are important for farming, energy and the environment.

| River | Rises in | Flows into | Length in Portugal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tagus (Tejo) | Spain | Atlantic (Lisbon) | 275 km |
| Douro | Spain | Atlantic (Porto) | 213 km |
| Guadiana | Spain | Atlantic (Algarve) | 260 km |
| Mondego | Serra da Estrela | Atlantic (Figueira da Foz) | 234 km (rises in Portugal!) |
| Minho | Serra Meira (Spain) | Atlantic (Caminha) | 75 km |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The **Mondego** is the longest river that both rises and ends entirely in Portugal. The other major rivers rise in Spain!
