# My Body

Your body is amazing! It has many parts that work together so you can run, play and learn.

---

## Parts of the Body

Our body has **external** parts we can see and touch.

| Body Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Head | Thinking, seeing, hearing, smelling and tasting |
| Trunk | Protecting our internal organs |
| Arms and hands | Grabbing, writing and hugging |
| Legs and feet | Walking, running and jumping |

> [!NOTE]
> When you are fully grown, your body will have **206 bones**! As a baby you actually have more, but some fuse together as you grow.

---

## The Five Senses

Our senses help us understand the world around us.

| Sense | Organ | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Sight | Eyes | See colours, shapes and movement |
| Hearing | Ears | Hear sounds and music |
| Smell | Nose | Smell flowers, food and more |
| Taste | Tongue | Sense sweet, salty, sour and bitter |
| Touch | Skin | Feel warm, cold, soft and rough |

> [!TIP]
> Close your eyes and ask a friend to give you something to smell or touch. Can you guess what it is using only your other senses?

---

## Basic Hygiene

Looking after your body is very important for staying healthy.

- **Wash your hands** before eating and after using the toilet
- **Brush your teeth** in the morning and before going to bed
- **Have a bath or shower** every day
- **Cut your nails** regularly
- **Change into clean clothes** every day

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Washing your hands properly with soap for at least **20 seconds** helps to keep many illnesses away!

## Try it!

1. **Draw your body** on a large sheet of paper and write the names of five body parts.
2. **Senses game**: place different objects inside a bag. Put your hand in without looking — can you guess what each one is?
3. **Name and touch**: say the name of ten body parts out loud while touching each one.
4. **Hygiene chart**: make a chart for this week and add a tick every time you brush your teeth.
