# Health and the Body

## Introduction

Knowing health vocabulary in English is vital — for doctor's visits, understanding medical information, or simply talking about how you feel. This lesson covers illnesses, symptoms, remedies, and healthy lifestyle language.

## Healthy Lifestyle

| Expression | Portuguese |
|------------|-----------|
| **eat a balanced diet** | ter uma alimentação equilibrada |
| **drink plenty of water** | beber bastante água |
| **exercise regularly** | fazer exercício regularmente |
| **get enough sleep** | dormir o suficiente |

| **wash your hands** | lavar as mãos |
| **manage stress** | gerir o stress |
| **fresh air** | ar fresco |

> [!TIP]
> In the UK, you call your GP when you're unwell — you **make an appointment**. In an emergency, you call **999** or go to **A&E** (Accident and Emergency).

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Match the symptom to the illness

| Symptom | | Illness |
|--------|-|---------|
| high temperature, cough, sore throat | | broken bone |
| pain when eating or drinking | | flu |
| very painful after falling | | toothache |
| red, itchy skin after eating nuts | | allergy |

### Exercise 2 — What should you do?

Give advice using **"You should …"**:

1. You have a fever. → You should ________
2. You have a bad cough. → ________
3. You broke your arm. → ________
4. You have a headache. → ________

### Exercise 3 — At the doctor's

Write a dialogue (8–10 lines) between a patient and a doctor. Include:
- The greeting
- Describing symptoms
- The doctor asking questions
- The diagnosis
- The treatment / prescription

### Exercise 4 — Healthy living

Write 6 tips for a healthy lifestyle using **"You should"** and **"You shouldn't"**.

> [!TIP]
> In British English, the place you go for a non-emergency appointment is called a **surgery** or **clinic**. In American English it is usually called a **doctor's office**.
