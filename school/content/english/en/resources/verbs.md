# Verb Tables — Regular and Irregular

## Introduction

Verbs are the engine of every sentence. This reference guide gives you the complete tables for regular and irregular verbs in the most important tenses.

---

## Regular Verbs — How They Work

Regular verbs follow predictable patterns in all tenses.

### Present Simple

| Subject | Rule | Example (talk) |
|---------|------|---------------|
| I / You / We / They | base form | I **talk** |
| He / She / It | base form + **-s** | She **talks** |

### Past Simple

Add **-ed** to the base form (for all subjects):

> talk → talk**ed** / play → play**ed** / work → work**ed**

### Present Continuous

**am / is / are** + verb + **-ing**

> I **am talking** / She **is playing** / They **are working**

### Past Continuous

**was / were** + verb + **-ing**

> I **was talking** / They **were playing**

### Present Perfect

**have / has** + past participle (-ed for regular verbs)

> I **have talked** / She **has played**

### Will Future

**will** + base form

> I **will talk** / She **will play**
