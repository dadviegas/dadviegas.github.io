# Food Vocabulary

## Introduction

Food is one of the most useful topics in any language! Whether you're ordering at a restaurant, shopping, or just talking about what you like to eat, knowing food vocabulary in English is essential.

## Expressing Likes and Dislikes

| Expression | Portuguese |
|------------|-----------|
| **I like …** | Eu gosto de … |
| **I love …** | Eu adoro … |
| **I don't like …** | Eu não gosto de … |
| **My favorite food is …** | A minha comida favorita é … |
| **I'm hungry.** | Estou com fome. |
| **I'm thirsty.** | Estou com sede. |
| **It's delicious!** | É delicioso/a! |
| **It's disgusting!** | É horrível! |

## Meals of the Day

| Meal | Time |
|------|------|
| **breakfast** | morning |
| **lunch** | midday |
| **snack** | afternoon |
| **dinner / supper** | evening |

Examples:
- **For breakfast I eat bread and drink milk.**
- **For lunch I have rice and chicken.**
- **For dinner my family eats soup and fish.**

> [!TIP]
> **Countable nouns** (apple, carrot, egg) can be counted: *one apple, two eggs*. **Uncountable nouns** (bread, rice, milk) cannot: *some bread, a glass of milk*.
