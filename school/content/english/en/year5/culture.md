# English-Speaking Countries and Cultures

## Introduction

English is spoken natively by around 400 million people and as a second language by over a billion more. It is the official or widely spoken language in more than 50 countries across every continent. Let's explore the main English-speaking nations and their cultures!

---

## The United Kingdom

The UK consists of four nations:

| Nation | Capital | Symbol |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **England** | London | Red cross on white (St George's cross) |
| **Scotland** | Edinburgh | White diagonal cross on blue |
| **Wales** | Cardiff | Red dragon |
| **Northern Ireland** | Belfast | Red hand |

**Famous for:** The Royal Family, Big Ben, double-decker buses, fish and chips, Shakespeare, The Beatles, football.

**Currency:** British Pound (£)
**Driving:** Left side of the road

---

## The United States of America

**Capital:** Washington D.C.
**Largest city:** New York City
**States:** 50 states

**Famous for:** Hollywood, New York City, the Statue of Liberty, Thanksgiving, hamburgers, baseball, NASA, Silicon Valley.

**Currency:** US Dollar ($)
**National holiday:** Independence Day (4th July)

---

## Australia

**Capital:** Canberra
**Largest city:** Sydney

**Famous for:** The Sydney Opera House, the Great Barrier Reef, kangaroos, koalas, Uluru, surfing, barbecues ("barbie"), and a very relaxed culture.

**Currency:** Australian Dollar (AUD)
**Climate:** Opposite seasons to the Northern Hemisphere — Christmas is in summer!

> [!TIP]
> Australian English has its own slang: *arvo* (afternoon), *brekkie* (breakfast), *servo* (petrol station), *G'day* (hello). It's the same language — but with a unique flavour!

---

## Canada

**Capital:** Ottawa
**Official languages:** English and French

**Famous for:** Maple syrup, ice hockey, Niagara Falls, vast forests, moose, and cold winters.

**Currency:** Canadian Dollar (CAD)
**National symbol:** The maple leaf

---

## Ireland

**Capital:** Dublin

**Famous for:** Traditional music (fiddles, bodhráns), the colour green, shamrocks, St Patrick's Day, Guinness, cliffs, and a rich literary tradition (James Joyce, Oscar Wilde, Samuel Beckett).

**Currency:** Euro (€)

## English Varieties — Same Language, Different Words!

| British English | American English | Australian English | Meaning |
|----------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------|
| lift | elevator | lift | ascensor |
| flat | apartment | unit / flat | apartamento |
| rubbish | garbage / trash | rubbish / trash | lixo |
| motorway | freeway | motorway | autoestrada |
| autumn | fall | autumn | outono |
| post | mail | post | correio |
