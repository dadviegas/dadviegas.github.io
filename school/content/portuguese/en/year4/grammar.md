# Grammar

In Year 4 we study the main **word classes** and learn to conjugate verbs in different tenses.

---

## Word Classes

| Class | Portuguese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Noun (*nome*) | names people, places, things | *casa*, *João*, *amor* |
| Verb (*verbo*) | expresses actions or states | *correr*, *ser*, *ter* |
| Adjective (*adjetivo*) | describes a noun | *bonito*, *grande*, *frio* |
| Determiner (*determinante*) | introduces a noun | *o*, *a*, *um*, *uma* |
| Pronoun (*pronome*) | replaces a noun | *ele*, *ela*, *eles* |
| Adverb (*advérbio*) | modifies a verb or adjective | *rapidamente*, *hoje*, *muito* |
| Preposition (*preposição*) | links words | *de*, *em*, *com*, *por* |

---

## Nouns

Nouns in Portuguese have **gender** (masculine/feminine) and **number** (singular/plural).

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Masculine | o *gato* | os *gatos* |
| Feminine | a *cadeira* | as *cadeiras* |

> [!TIP]
> Most nouns ending in **-o** are masculine; most ending in **-a** are feminine. There are exceptions — *o mapa*, *a mão*.

---

## Adjectives

Adjectives **agree** with the noun they describe in gender and number.

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | o livro *novo* | a mesa *nova* |
| Plural | os livros *novos* | as mesas *novas* |

---

## Verb Tenses

### Present (*Presente*)

Used for actions happening now or habitually.

| Person | *falar* (to speak) | *comer* (to eat) |
|---|---|---|
| eu | fal**o** | com**o** |
| tu | fal**as** | com**es** |
| ele/ela | fal**a** | com**e** |
| nós | fal**amos** | com**emos** |
| eles/elas | fal**am** | com**em** |

### Simple Past (*Pretérito Perfeito*)

Used for completed actions in the past.

| Person | *falar* | *comer* |
|---|---|---|
| eu | fal**ei** | com**i** |
| tu | fal**aste** | com**este** |
| ele/ela | fal**ou** | com**eu** |
| nós | fal**ámos** | com**emos** |
| eles/elas | fal**aram** | com**eram** |

### Future (*Futuro*)

| Person | *falar* | *comer* |
|---|---|---|
| eu | falar**ei** | comer**ei** |
| tu | falar**ás** | comer**ás** |
| ele/ela | falar**á** | comer**á** |
| nós | falar**emos** | comer**emos** |
| eles/elas | falar**ão** | comer**ão** |

---

## Sentence Types

- **Simple sentence** (*frase simples*) — one subject, one verb: *O cão ladrou.*
- **Complex sentence** (*frase complexa*) — two or more clauses: *O cão ladrou quando chegou o carteiro.*
