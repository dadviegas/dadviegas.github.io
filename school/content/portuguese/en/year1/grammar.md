# Grammar — Year 1

## Sentences (Frases)

A **sentence** (*frase*) is a group of words that makes complete sense.

In Portuguese, every sentence:
- Starts with a **capital letter**
- Ends with a **punctuation mark** (. ! ?)

| Portuguese sentence | English meaning | Type |
|--------------------|-----------------|------|
| *O gato dorme.* | The cat sleeps. | statement |
| *A Maria corre!* | Maria runs! | exclamation |
| *Tens um lápis?* | Do you have a pencil? | question |

> [!TIP]
> In Portuguese, punctuation marks work the same way as in English. A full stop (.) is called a *ponto final*.

---

## Nouns (Nomes)

A **noun** (*nome*) is a word that names a person, animal, or thing.

| Type | Portuguese | English |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Person (*pessoa*) | *menina*, *professor*, *pai* | girl, teacher, father |
| Animal (*animal*) | *cão*, *gato*, *pássaro* | dog, cat, bird |
| Thing (*coisa*) | *mesa*, *bola*, *livro* | table, ball, book |

> [!NOTE]
> In Portuguese, all nouns have a **gender** — they are either masculine or feminine. There is no neutral "it"!

---

## Gender: Masculine and Feminine

Every Portuguese noun is either **masculine** (masculino) or **feminine** (feminino).

| Masculine | Feminine | English |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| *o menino* | *a menina* | boy / girl |
| *o gato* | *a gata* | male cat / female cat |
| *o professor* | *a professora* | male teacher / female teacher |
| *o rei* | *a rainha* | king / queen |

> [!TIP]
> Many feminine nouns end in **-a**. The word before the noun also changes:
> - **o** (the) is used with masculine nouns
> - **a** (the) is used with feminine nouns

---

## Singular and Plural

**Singular** = one thing. **Plural** = more than one thing.

| Singular | Plural | Rule |
|----------|--------|------|
| *livro* (book) | *livros* | add **-s** |
| *flor* (flower) | *flores* | add **-es** |
| *pão* (bread) | *pães* | change ending |
| *lápis* (pencil) | *lápis* | no change! |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The most common rule for forming plurals in Portuguese is to add **-s** to the singular form.
