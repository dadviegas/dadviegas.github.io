# Text Types

Different situations call for different types of writing. In Year 4 we learn to **read and write** five main text types.

---

## Narrative Text (*Texto Narrativo*)

Tells a story — real or imaginary.

**Key features:**
- Characters (*personagens*)
- Setting (*espaço* and *tempo*)
- Plot: introduction → development → conclusion
- Usually written in the past tense
- Uses dialogue and description

> [!TIP]
> A good narrative answers: **Who? Where? When? What happened? How did it end?**

---

## Descriptive Text (*Texto Descritivo*)

Paints a picture with words — describes a person, place, or object.

**Key features:**
- Rich adjectives and sensory details
- Present tense (usually)
- Organised from general to specific (or top to bottom, inside to outside)

**Example:** *A casa era antiga e pequena. As paredes brancas estavam cobertas de hera verde. Pelo jardim, cheirava a jasmim.*

---

## Instructional Text (*Texto Instrucional*)

Explains how to do or make something (recipes, manuals, rules of games).

**Key features:**
- Numbered steps in sequence
- Imperative verbs (*Adiciona…*, *Corta…*, *Mistura…*)
- Lists of materials/ingredients
- Clear, concise language

---

## Poetic Text (*Texto Poético*)

Uses language in a creative, musical way.

**Key features:**
- Lines (*versos*) grouped into stanzas (*estrofes*)
- Rhyme (*rima*) — may or may not be present
- Rhythm and sound devices: alliteration, repetition
- Figurative language: metaphors, similes

---

## News Article (*Notícia*)

Reports a real event factually.

**Key features:**
- Headline (*título*) — grabs attention
- Lead paragraph answers: **Who? What? Where? When? Why?**
- Body gives details and quotes
- Neutral, factual tone
- Written in the past tense

---

## Summary Table

| Text Type | Purpose | Tense | Key Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative | Tell a story | Past | Plot structure |
| Descriptive | Describe | Present | Adjectives |
| Instructional | Explain how to | Imperative | Numbered steps |
| Poetic | Express / create beauty | Varies | Rhythm & rhyme |
| News | Report facts | Past | Who/What/Where/When/Why |
