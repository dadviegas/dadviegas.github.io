# Vocabulary — Year 2

## Synonyms (Sinónimos)

**Synonyms** are words that mean the same, or nearly the same, thing.

| Portuguese word | Synonyms | English meaning |
|----------------|---------|----------------|
| *bonito* | *lindo*, *belo*, *formoso* | beautiful, pretty |
| *contente* | *feliz*, *alegre* | happy, content |
| *rápido* | *veloz*, *ligeiro* | fast, quick |
| *falar* | *dizer*, *contar* | to speak, to say |
| *grande* | *enorme*, *imenso* | big, large, huge |

> [!TIP]
> Using synonyms makes your writing more interesting and avoids repeating the same word!

---

## Antonyms (Antónimos)

**Antonyms** are words with opposite meanings.

| Word | Antonym | English |
|------|---------|---------|
| *frio* | *quente* | cold / hot |
| *alto* | *baixo* | tall / short |
| *cheio* | *vazio* | full / empty |
| *abrir* | *fechar* | to open / to close |
| *dia* | *noite* | day / night |
| *dentro* | *fora* | inside / outside |

> [!NOTE]
> Many antonyms in Portuguese are formed by adding a **prefix**:
> - **in-**: *feliz* (happy) → *infeliz* (unhappy)
> - **des-**: *fazer* (to do) → *desfazer* (to undo)
> - **im-**: *possível* (possible) → *impossível* (impossible)

---

## Word Families (Família de Palavras)

Words in the same **family** share a common root and have related meanings.

| Root | Word family | English meanings |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| *peixe* | *peixe*, *peixaria*, *peixeiro* | fish, fish shop, fishmonger |
| *leite* | *leite*, *leiteiro*, *leitaria* | milk, milkman, dairy |
| *terra* | *terra*, *terreno*, *enterrar* | earth, land/terrain, to bury |
| *água* | *água*, *aguado*, *aguarela* | water, watery, watercolour |

---

## Lexical Fields (Campos Lexicais)

A **lexical field** is a group of words all related to the same topic.

**Lexical field: Sport (Desporto)**
*bola* (ball), *campo* (field), *equipa* (team), *golo* (goal), *árbitro* (referee), *treino* (training)

**Lexical field: Nature (Natureza)**
*árvore* (tree), *flor* (flower), *rio* (river), *sol* (sun), *chuva* (rain), *floresta* (forest)

> [!NOTE]
> A lexical field can include nouns, adjectives, and verbs — all connected by topic, not by word form.
