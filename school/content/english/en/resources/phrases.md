# Useful Everyday Phrases

## Introduction

This guide collects the most useful English phrases for everyday situations — greetings, shopping, transport, school, social situations, and more. Knowing these phrases will help you communicate naturally and confidently.

---

## Greetings and Farewells

| Phrase | Use | Portuguese |
|--------|-----|-----------|
| **Hello / Hi** | General greeting | Olá |
| **Good morning** | Until midday | Bom dia |
| **Good afternoon** | Midday – 6 pm | Boa tarde |
| **Good evening** | After 6 pm | Boa noite (chegada) |
| **Good night** | At bedtime only | Boa noite (despedida) |
| **How are you?** | Asking how someone is | Como estás? |
| **I'm fine, thanks.** | Standard reply | Estou bem, obrigado/a. |
| **Not bad.** | So-so reply | Não está mau. |
| **Goodbye / Bye** | Farewell | Adeus / Tchau |
| **See you later / tomorrow** | Informal farewell | Até logo / amanhã |
| **Take care!** | Warm farewell | Cuida-te! |
| **Have a good day!** | Friendly farewell | Bom dia! |
| **Nice to meet you.** | First time meeting | Prazer em conhecer-te. |
| **It's nice to see you.** | Meeting again | Fico contente de te ver. |
