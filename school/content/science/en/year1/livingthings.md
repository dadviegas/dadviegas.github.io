# Living Things

## What Makes Something Alive?

Living things are special — they grow, breathe, eat, move, and have babies! But a rock, a chair, or a cloud cannot do any of these things. Can you spot the difference? 👀

---

## Living vs Non-Living

| Living Thing 🌱 | Non-Living Thing 🪨 |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Dog 🐶 | Rock |
| Tree 🌳 | Water |
| Mushroom 🍄 | Chair |
| Butterfly 🦋 | Cloud |
| Grass | Sand |
| You! 🧒 | Pencil |

> [!NOTE]
> Living things **are born**, **grow**, **eat**, **breathe**, **reproduce**, and **die**. Non-living things do none of these things!

---

## The 7 Signs of Life

All living things share these 7 characteristics:

| Sign | What it means |
|------|--------------|
| 🍽️ **Nutrition** | They eat and get energy from food |
| 💨 **Respiration** | They breathe to release energy |
| 🚽 **Excretion** | They get rid of waste |
| 🏃 **Movement** | They move (even plants bend towards light!) |
| 💊 **Sensitivity** | They react to changes around them |
| 🌱 **Growth** | They grow and develop |
| 👶 **Reproduction** | They have offspring |

## Pet Animals and Wild Animals

### Pet Animals
They live with people and need our care:

- 🐶 **Dog** — loyal and playful
- 🐱 **Cat** — independent and curious
- 🐠 **Fish** — silent and beautiful
- 🐰 **Rabbit** — fluffy and shy
- 🐦 **Canary** — sings beautifully
- 🐹 **Hamster** — loves to run on wheels!

### Wild Animals
They live in nature and look after themselves:

- 🦁 **Lion** — king of the savanna
- 🐘 **Elephant** — the largest land animal
- 🦊 **Fox** — clever and quick
- 🦅 **Eagle** — soars high above
- 🐍 **Snake** — slithers on the ground

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never approach wild animals without an adult! They can be dangerous — and remember, they are afraid of us too! 🚨

## Plants

Plants are very special living things — they make their own food using sunlight! ☀️ This process is called **photosynthesis**.

### Parts of a Plant

```
        🌸 Flower
         |
        🌿 Leaf
         |
        🟤 Stem
         |
       〰️ Root
```

| Part | What does it do? |
|------|-----------------|
| 🌱 **Root** | Absorbs water and minerals from the soil. Holds the plant in place. |
| 🟤 **Stem** | Supports the plant and carries water up to the leaves. |
| 🌿 **Leaf** | Makes food through photosynthesis (uses sunlight!). |
| 🌸 **Flower** | Attracts insects and allows the plant to reproduce. |
| 🍎 **Fruit** | Protects the seeds. Many fruits are delicious to eat! |

> [!TIP]
> Flowers turn into fruit after they are pollinated by bees 🐝 and other insects. That is why bees are so incredibly important — without them, we would have no fruits or vegetables!

## Caring for Plants and Animals

### How to care for a plant
1. 💧 Water it regularly (but not too much!)
2. ☀️ Place it somewhere with good light
3. 🪣 Use good soil and the right pot
4. ✂️ Remove dead or yellow leaves
5. 🌡️ Protect it from frost and extreme cold

### How to care for a pet
1. 🍽️ Give it food and fresh water every day
2. 🏃 Play with it and let it exercise
3. 🏥 Take it to the vet for check-ups
4. 🛁 Keep it clean and groomed
5. 💕 Treat it with love and respect

> [!NOTE]
> Having a pet is a big responsibility! It is like having a friend who depends completely on you for everything. 🤝

## Experimenta!

**Activity: The Magic Bean 🌱**

1. Place damp cotton wool in a transparent cup
2. Press a bean against the glass
3. Water it a little every day
4. Watch what happens over a week!

Draw what you see each day in your notebook. How many days until the first root appears? 🔍

**What you will see:**
- Day 1–2: The bean swells up
- Day 3–4: The first root (radicle) appears
- Day 5–7: A small shoot pushes upward!
