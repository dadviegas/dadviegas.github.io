# Health & Hygiene

Being **healthy** means feeling well physically, mentally and socially. To stay healthy, we need to look after our bodies every single day.

---

## Personal Hygiene

Good personal hygiene prevents illness and keeps our bodies clean and healthy.

| Habit | How often? |
|---|---|
| Washing hands | Before meals, after the toilet, after sneezing or coughing |
| Brushing teeth | At least twice a day (after meals) |
| Showering / bathing | Daily |
| Washing hair | Regularly |
| Cutting nails | Regularly — keep them short and clean |

> [!TIP]
> Wash your hands for at least **20 seconds** with soap and water — that is about the time it takes to sing "Happy Birthday" twice!

---

## Healthy Eating

A healthy diet gives the body the **nutrients** it needs to grow and function well.

### The Food Wheel

Portugal uses a **Mediterranean Food Wheel** that divides foods into groups:

| Group | Examples | How much? |
|---|---|---|
| Cereals & derivatives | Bread, pasta, rice | Most of the diet |
| Vegetables | Lettuce, carrot, broccoli | Most of the diet |
| Fruit | Apple, orange, banana | Most of the diet |
| Dairy | Milk, yoghurt, cheese | Moderate amounts |
| Meat, fish, eggs & pulses | Chicken, tuna, beans | Moderate amounts |
| Fats & oils | Olive oil | Small amounts |

> [!NOTE]
> Water should be the main drink! Children should drink about **1.5 to 2 litres** of water every day.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Foods high in sugar, salt and fat (cakes, crisps, fizzy drinks) should be eaten **very rarely** — they are not part of the healthy food wheel!

## Physical Exercise

Exercise is essential for good health. Its benefits include:

- Strengthens muscles and bones
- Improves heart and lung health
- Helps maintain a healthy weight
- Improves mood and reduces stress
- Improves sleep quality

> [!TIP]
> The World Health Organisation recommends at least **60 minutes** of moderate physical activity every day for children.

---

## Vaccination

**Vaccines** protect us from serious diseases. When we are vaccinated, our immune system learns to recognise and fight a virus or bacteria without becoming seriously ill.

Portugal's **National Vaccination Plan (PNV)** is free and includes vaccines against measles, polio, tetanus and many other diseases.

---

## Sleep and Rest

Getting enough sleep is vital for health! Children aged 9–10 need **9 to 11 hours** of sleep per night to grow, learn and restore energy.
