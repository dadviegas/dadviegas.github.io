## 🅰 Vowels and Consonants

Letters are divided into two large groups: **vowels** and **consonants**.

### The Vowels

Vowels are letters where air flows freely from the mouth without obstruction.

| Vowel | Possible sounds | Examples |
|:-----:|----------------|---------|
| **A** | open a, closed a, nasal a | **a**mo (love), câ**m**ara (chamber), m**ã**o (hand) |
| **E** | open e, closed e, silent e | p**é** (foot), voc**ê** (you), leit**e** (milk) |
| **I** | i | **i**lha (island), liv**r**o (book) |
| **O** | open o, closed o | p**ó** (dust), av**ô** (grandfather) |
| **U** | u | **u**va (grape), lug**ar** (place) |

> [!NOTE]
> In Portuguese, vowels can have an acute accent (´), grave accent (`), circumflex (^) or tilde (~), which changes their sound!

### The Consonants

Consonants are all the other letters: **B, C, D, F, G, H, J, L, M, N, P, Q, R, S, T, V, W, X, Y, Z**.

> [!TIP]
> Trick to remember the vowels: **A-E-I-O-U** — learn this sequence by heart! 🎵

## Digraphs

Two letters that together make just **one special sound**.

| Digraph | Sound | Examples | Image |
|:-------:|-------|---------|-------|
| **ch** | sh (like "shoe") | chave (key), chuva (rain) | 🔑 ☔ |
| **lh** | ly (like "million") | folha (leaf), milho (corn) | 🍃 🌽 |
| **nh** | ny (like "canyon") | ninho (nest), banho (bath) | 🐣 🛁 |
| **rr** | strong r (rolling) | carro (car), terra (earth) | 🚗 🌍 |
| **ss** | strong s | passo (step), osso (bone) | 🦴 |
| **qu** | k (before e/i) | queijo (cheese), quilo (kilo) | 🧀 |
| **gu** | g (before e/i) | guerra (war), guitarra (guitar) | 🎸 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In digraphs, the two letters are inseparable — they always go together! 🤝

## Diphthongs

A **diphthong** is the combination of a vowel and a semivowel in the same syllable. They are sounds that "glide"!

### Oral Diphthongs

| Diphthong | Examples (Portuguese → English) | Image |
|:---------:|--------------------------------|-------|
| **ai** | pai (father), vai (goes) | 👨 |
| **ei** | lei (law), rei (king), peixe (fish) | 👑 🐟 |
| **oi** | boi (ox), noite (night), coisa (thing) | 🐂 🌃 |
| **ui** | fui (I went), cuida (takes care) | |
| **au** | mau (bad), pausa (pause) | |
| **eu** | meu (my), deus (god) | |
| **ou** | vou (I go), outro (other), pouco (little) | |

### Nasal Diphthongs

| Diphthong | Examples (Portuguese → English) | Image |
|:---------:|--------------------------------|-------|
| **ão** | mão (hand), pão (bread), avião (plane) | ✋ 🍞 ✈️ |
| **ãe** | mãe (mother), pães (breads) | 👩 🍞 |
| **õe** | limões (lemons), aviões (planes) | 🍋 ✈️ |
| **ãi** | cãibra (cramp) | |
| **ui** | muito (much), ruim (bad) | |

> [!TIP]
> Nasal diphthongs have the tilde (~) — that "through-the-nose" sound that exists in Portuguese! 👃

## Rhyme and Syllables

Each word is divided into **syllables** — chunks of sound. Clapping your hands helps you count them!

| Word | Syllables | Number of syllables |
|------|-----------|:-------------------:|
| sol (sun) | sol | 1 |
| ca-sa (house) | ca / sa | 2 |
| e-le-fan-te (elephant) | e / le / fan / te | 4 |
| a-be-lha (bee) | a / be / lha | 3 |

> [!NOTE]
> To divide a word into syllables, count the vowels — each syllable has (at least) one vowel!
