# Numbers 1–10

## Introduction

Numbers are everywhere! We use them to count toys, people, books, and much more. Let's learn the numbers from **one** to **ten** in English.

---

| 2 | **two** | dois / duas |
| 3 | **three** | três |
| 4 | **four** | quatro |

| 6 | **six** | seis |
| 7 | **seven** | sete |

> Count your fingers in English! Hold up one finger and say **one**, two fingers and say **two**, all the way to **ten**!

## Numbers in Sentences

| English | Portuguese |
|---------|-----------|
| I have **one** cat. | Eu tenho **um** gato. |
| I see **two** birds. | Eu vejo **dois** pássaros. |
| There are **three** apples. | Há **três** maçãs. |
| I have **four** pencils. | Eu tenho **quatro** lápis. |
| I see **five** dogs. | Eu vejo **cinco** cães. |
| There are **six** fish. | Há **seis** peixes. |
| I have **seven** books. | Eu tenho **sete** livros. |
| There are **eight** stars. | Há **oito** estrelas. |
| I see **nine** flowers. | Eu vejo **nove** flores. |
| I have **ten** fingers. | Eu tenho **dez** dedos. |

> [!TIP]
> **I have** = eu tenho | **I see** = eu vejo | **There are** = há / existem

---

## Asking and Answering "How many?"

**Question:** How many … are there?

**Answer:** There are + number + noun.

Examples:
- **How many apples are there?** → **There are three apples.**
- **How many cats are there?** → **There is one cat.** (singular: *there is*)

---

## Simple Maths in English

| Operation | English |
|-----------|---------|
| + | plus |
| − | minus |
| = | equals |

Examples:
- **One plus two equals three.** (1 + 2 = 3)
- **Five minus two equals three.** (5 − 2 = 3)
- **Four plus four equals eight.** (4 + 4 = 8)
