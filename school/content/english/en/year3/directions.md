# Directions and Prepositions of Place

## Introduction

Getting from A to B requires clear directions! In this lesson we learn how to ask for and give directions in English, as well as prepositions to describe exactly where things are.

## Giving Directions

| Instruction | Portuguese |
|-------------|-----------|
| **Go straight on / ahead.** | Vá em frente. |
| **Turn left.** | Vire à esquerda. |
| **Turn right.** | Vire à direita. |
| **Take the first left.** | Tome a primeira à esquerda. |
| **Take the second right.** | Tome a segunda à direita. |
| **Go past the …** | Passe pelo/pela … |
| **Cross the …** | Atravesse o/a … |
| **It's on the left.** | É do lado esquerdo. |
| **It's on the right.** | É do lado direito. |
| **It's opposite the …** | É em frente ao/à … |
| **It's next to the …** | É ao lado do/da … |
| **It's at the end of the road.** | É no fim da rua. |
| **It's about … metres away.** | Fica a cerca de … metros. |

## Asking for Directions

| Question | Portuguese |
|----------|-----------|
| **Excuse me, how do I get to …?** | Com licença, como chego a …? |
| **Where is the … ?** | Onde fica o/a …? |
| **Is there a … near here?** | Há um/a … perto daqui? |
| **How far is it?** | A que distância fica? |
| **Can you show me on the map?** | Pode mostrar-me no mapa? |

## Prepositions of Place

| Preposition | Portuguese | Example |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| **in** | em / dentro de | The café is **in** the square. |
| **on** | em / sobre | The book is **on** the table. |
| **at** | em / a | Meet me **at** the bus stop. |
| **next to** | ao lado de | The bank is **next to** the park. |
| **opposite** | em frente de | The school is **opposite** the library. |
| **between** | entre | The café is **between** the bank and the post office. |
| **behind** | atrás de | The car park is **behind** the cinema. |
| **in front of** | em frente de / diante de | There is a fountain **in front of** the museum. |
| **around the corner** | na esquina / ao virar da esquina | The pharmacy is **around the corner**. |

> [!TIP]
> When giving directions, use **imperative** forms: *Turn left. Go straight on. Cross the road.* No subject needed!

---

## Sample Dialogue

**Tourist:** Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?
**Local:** Go straight on for about 200 metres, then turn left at the traffic lights. The station is on your right, opposite the park.
**Tourist:** How far is it?
**Local:** It's about 5 minutes on foot.
**Tourist:** Thank you very much!
**Local:** You're welcome!

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Follow the directions

Start at the school. Follow these instructions and write where you end up:

1. Go straight on for two blocks.
2. Turn right.
3. Go past the park.
4. Turn left.
5. It's on your right.

Where are you? ________

### Exercise 2 — Give directions

Look at this simple map and write directions from the bank to the library:

```
[Park] [Bank] [Post Office]
[School]  [Road]  [Library]
[Museum] [Cinema] [Hospital]
```

### Exercise 3 — Fill in the preposition

1. The bank is ________ the park and the post office.
2. The museum is ________ the cinema.
3. The library is ________ the school.

### Exercise 4 — Write a dialogue

Write a dialogue (8–10 lines) where someone asks how to get from the museum to the hospital (use the map above).

> [!TIP]
> Next time you're walking somewhere with family, try to give the directions in English! *"Turn left here. Go straight on."*
