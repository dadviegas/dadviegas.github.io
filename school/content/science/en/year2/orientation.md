# Orientation & Maps

## Finding Your Way!

Have you ever been lost? Knowing how to find your direction is a really useful skill! People have been navigating — finding their way — for thousands of years using the sun, stars, and maps. 🌍

---

## The Cardinal Points

There are **4 main cardinal points** that help us find direction:

```
           NORTH (N)
               ⬆️
               |
WEST (W) ⬅️ ---+--- ➡️ EAST (E)
               |
               ⬇️
           SOUTH (S)
```

> [!TIP]
> An easy way to remember the order: **"Never Eat Sour Worms"** → **N**orth, **E**ast, **S**outh, **W**est! 😄 Go clockwise around the compass!

## How Do We Find Our Direction?

### Using the Sun

The Sun is our best "natural compass"!

| Time of day | Sun's position | Direction |
|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Morning (sunrise) | Rising | **East** |
| Midday | High in the sky | **South** (in Portugal) |
| Evening (sunset) | Setting | **West** |

> [!NOTE]
> In Portugal and the UK, at midday the Sun is always in the **south**. So if you stand facing the Sun at noon, North is behind you, East is to your left, and West is to your right!

### Using a Compass

A compass has a magnetic needle that always points to **North**. It is the most famous navigation tool!

### Using the Stars

At night, the **Pole Star** (Polaris) always indicates North. It stays in the same position in the sky all night!

## The Compass Rose

The compass rose shows all the directions of orientation:

```
        N
        ⬆️
   NW ↖️  ↗️ NE
        +
   SW ↙️  ↘️ SE
        ⬇️
        S
```

| Abbreviation | Full name |
|-------------|-----------|
| **N** | North |
| **S** | South |
| **E** | East |
| **W** | West |
| **NE** | North-east |
| **NW** | North-west |
| **SE** | South-east |
| **SW** | South-west |

## Maps

A map is a **representation of a space seen from above**, as if you were a bird flying overhead! 🦅

### Elements of a Map

Every map has:

- 🧭 **Compass rose** — shows which way North is
- 📏 **Scale** — tells us what distance each measurement represents
- 🔑 **Legend/Key** — explains the symbols used
- 📝 **Title** — says what the map shows

### Types of Maps

| Type | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| 🌍 **World map** | The entire world |
| 🌏 **Planisphere** | The world "opened up" flat |
| 🇵🇹 **Country map** | A whole country |
| 🏙️ **City map** | A city or neighbourhood |
| 🏫 **Floor plan** | The inside of a building |

> [!TIP]
> **Scale** is very useful! If a map has a scale of 1:1000, then 1 cm on the map = 1000 cm (10 metres) in real life. This lets us measure real distances on maps!

## Routes and Itineraries

A route (or itinerary) is the **journey we take** from one place to another.

To describe a route, we use:
- Reference points (traffic lights, monuments, schools...)
- Directions (left, right, straight ahead)
- Cardinal points (go North, turn West...)

**Example:** *"Leave the school, turn right, go past the traffic lights, continue straight ahead to the park and the library is on your left."* 📍

## The Classroom Floor Plan

A floor plan is a **view from above** of an indoor space — like looking down through the ceiling!

How to make a floor plan of your classroom:
1. Imagine you are looking from above (like a helicopter 🚁)
2. Draw the outline of the room (usually a rectangle)
3. Mark the windows (openings in the walls)
4. Mark the door (opening with an arc)
5. Place the furniture (desks, board, cupboards)
6. Add the compass rose!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In a floor plan, **walls** are shown as thick lines, **doors** as an arc, and **windows** as thin lines. Every symbol goes in the legend! 🔑
