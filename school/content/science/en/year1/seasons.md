# Seasons of the Year

## The Four Seasons

Every year has **four seasons**. Each season brings different weather, colours, and activities! The seasons happen because the Earth tilts as it travels around the Sun. 🌍

---

## The Four Seasons at a Glance

| Season | Months | Symbol |
|--------|--------|--------|
| 🌸 **Spring** | March, April, May | Flowers blooming |
| ☀️ **Summer** | June, July, August | Bright sunshine |
| 🍂 **Autumn** | September, October, November | Leaves falling |
| ❄️ **Winter** | December, January, February | Cold and rain |

---

## Spring (March — May)

Spring is the season of **new beginnings**!

**What happens in nature:**
- 🌼 Flowers open and fill the air with perfume
- 🌿 Trees fill up with fresh green leaves
- 🐦 Birds sing more and build nests
- 🐛 Insects appear — bees, butterflies, ladybirds
- 🌧️ There are gentle showers and longer days
- 🐣 Baby animals are born!

**What we wear:**
- Light jacket 🧥
- T-shirt or jumper
- Light trousers or skirt

> [!TIP]
> Spring is a fantastic time to plant seeds! 🌱 The soil is moist and the sun is warming up — perfect growing conditions!

---

## Summer (June — August)

Summer is the season of **heat and holidays**!

**What happens in nature:**
- 🌞 Days are very long and hot
- 🏖️ Beaches fill up with people
- 🍓 Lots of fruit ripens (strawberries, watermelons, peaches)
- 🌵 Grass turns yellow in the heat
- 🌩️ Thunderstorms can happen in the afternoon

**What we wear:**
- T-shirt and shorts 🩳
- Light dress 👗
- Sandals and a sun hat ☀️
- Sun cream! 🧴

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In summer, drink lots of water 💧 and avoid direct sun between 12pm and 4pm. Too much sun can make you ill — always wear sun cream and a hat!

---

## Autumn (September — November)

Autumn is the season of **warm colours**!

**What happens in nature:**
- 🍁 Leaves turn yellow, orange, and red
- 🍂 Leaves fall from the trees
- 🍄 Mushrooms grow on the damp ground
- 🌧️ Rain returns more often
- 🌰 It is the season for chestnuts and acorns
- 🐿️ Squirrels collect food for winter

**What we wear:**
- Warmer jacket 🧥
- Woolly jumper
- Long trousers
- Waterproof boots 👢

> [!NOTE]
> Trees that lose their leaves in autumn and winter are called **deciduous** trees (oak, chestnut). Trees that stay green all year are called **evergreen** (pine, holly). 🌲

---

## Winter (December — February)

Winter is the season of **cold and rest**!

**What happens in nature:**
- ❄️ It can snow in colder areas
- 🌫️ There is frost and fog in the mornings
- 🌲 Many trees have no leaves (deciduous trees)
- 🐻 Some animals hibernate (sleep all winter!)
- 🌧️ It is the rainiest season
- 🌅 Days are very short and nights are long

**What we wear:**
- Thick coat and scarf 🧣
- Woolly hat and gloves 🧤
- Warm boots
- Thermal underwear 🧦

> [!TIP]
> Some animals **hibernate** in winter — like hedgehogs 🦔, dormice, and bats. They eat lots in autumn to build up fat, then sleep through the coldest months!
