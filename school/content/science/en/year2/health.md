# Health & Hygiene

Being healthy is really important for feeling happy and learning well. Let's find out how to look after our bodies!

---

## Personal Hygiene

Good personal hygiene protects us from illness and makes us feel great.

| Habit | When to do it |
|---|---|
| Wash hands | Before meals, after the toilet, after coughing |
| Brush teeth | After every meal and before bed |
| Shower or bath | Every day |
| Wash hair | Regularly |
| Cut nails | When they get long |
| Change clothes | Every day, especially after sport |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> We should brush our teeth for **2 minutes** — don't forget the back teeth and your tongue!

## The Food Wheel (Roda dos Alimentos)

The **Portuguese Food Wheel** shows us how to eat in a balanced way.

| Group | Examples | How much |
|---|---|---|
| Cereals and grains | Bread, rice, pasta | Large — the base of our diet |
| Vegetables | Lettuce, carrot, tomato | Large — eat plenty! |
| Fruit | Apple, orange, banana | Medium |
| Dairy | Milk, yoghurt, cheese | Medium |
| Meat, fish and eggs | Chicken, sardine, egg | Small |
| Legumes | Beans, chickpeas, lentils | Small |
| Fats and oils | Olive oil | Very small |

> [!TIP]
> Think of a colourful plate: the more colours you have (from vegetables and fruit!), the healthier your meal!

---

## Healthy Eating Habits

- Drink **6 to 8 glasses of water** every day
- Eat fruit and vegetables at **every meal**
- Avoid too much sugar, salt and fat
- Have **5 meals** a day: breakfast, morning snack, lunch, afternoon snack and dinner
- Never skip **breakfast** — it is the most important meal of the day!

> [!NOTE]
> Breakfast gives us the energy to learn and play all morning. Without it, we find it much harder to concentrate!

---

## Physical Exercise

Exercise is essential for a healthy life.

- Strengthens **muscles and bones**
- Improves **mood** and reduces stress
- Helps us **sleep better**
- Children are recommended at least **60 minutes** of physical activity every day

---

## Try it!

1. **My healthy plate**: draw a plate and fill it with foods from the different groups of the Food Wheel.
2. **Water diary**: count how many glasses of water you drink today. Did you reach 6?
3. **Exercise challenge**: do 10 minutes of physical activity (jumping, dancing, running) and write down how you felt.
4. **Hygiene routine**: make a poster showing your daily hygiene routine, with pictures and times.
