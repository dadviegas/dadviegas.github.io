# Measurements — Year 1

Measuring helps us understand the world! We measure things every day — how tall we are, how long it takes to eat breakfast, and how much things cost. Let's learn! 🌟

---

## Comparing Lengths

We can **compare** lengths without even using a ruler!

### Longer or Shorter?

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Longer** | Takes up more space | A bus is longer than a car 🚌 |
| **Shorter** | Takes up less space | A pencil is shorter than a ruler ✏️ |
| **Taller** | Higher up | A giraffe is taller than a dog 🦒 |
| **Same length** | Equal | Two twins can be the same height 👯 |

> [!TIP]
> To compare lengths fairly, **line up one end** of both objects at the same point! 📐

### Comparing with Objects
Before rulers, people used **hands**, **feet**, and **steps** to measure!

$$\text{length of table} = 5 \text{ hands long}$$

$$\text{length of room} = 10 \text{ steps long}$$

> [!NOTE]
> These are called **non-standard units** of measurement. They're fun to use, but a ruler gives the same answer for everyone! 🎯

## Months of the Year

There are **12 months** in a year:

| # | Month | Season (Northern Hemisphere) |
|---|-------|------------------------------|
| 1 | January | ❄️ Winter |
| 2 | February | ❄️ Winter |
| 3 | March | 🌸 Spring |
| 4 | April | 🌸 Spring |
| 5 | May | 🌸 Spring |
| 6 | June | ☀️ Summer |
| 7 | July | ☀️ Summer |
| 8 | August | ☀️ Summer |
| 9 | September | 🍂 Autumn |
| 10 | October | 🍂 Autumn |
| 11 | November | 🍂 Autumn |
| 12 | December | ❄️ Winter |

$$\text{1 year} = 12 \text{ months} = 52 \text{ weeks} = 365 \text{ days}$$

## Reading the Clock — Whole Hours

A clock has two hands:
- The **short hand** (hour hand) → tells us the **hour** ⏰
- The **long hand** (minute hand) → when it points to **12**, it's an exact hour!

| Clock Shows | We Say |
|-------------|--------|
| Short hand → 1, Long hand → 12 | **1 o'clock** |
| Short hand → 6, Long hand → 12 | **6 o'clock** |
| Short hand → 9, Long hand → 12 | **9 o'clock** |

> [!TIP]
> When the long hand points straight **up** to the 12, it's always an **exact hour** — like 3 o'clock or 7 o'clock! ⬆️

## Euro Coins

In Europe we use **euros (€)** and **cents (c)**. There are **100 cents in 1 euro**!

$$1 \text{ euro} = 100 \text{ cents}$$

| Coin | Value |
|------|-------|
| 🟡 | 1 cent |
| 🟡 | 2 cents |
| 🟡 | 5 cents |
| ⚪ | 10 cents |
| ⚪ | 20 cents |
| ⚪ | 50 cents |
| 🥇 | 1 euro |
| 🥇 | 2 euros |

> [!NOTE]
> The **gold-coloured** coins are worth less (1c, 2c, 5c). The **silver-coloured** coins are worth more (10c, 20c, 50c). The **bi-colour** coins are worth the most (€1, €2)! 💰

## Try It!

1. **Longer or Shorter?**: Find 5 objects at home. Put them in order from **shortest to longest**! Draw and label them.

2. **Days of the Week Song**: Can you say all 7 days of the week **backwards**? Sunday, Saturday, Friday... keep going! 🎵

3. **My Birthday Month**: Which month is your birthday in? Count how many months away it is from January!

4. **Clock Reading**: Ask a grown-up to show you a clock. When the long hand is on **12**, what hour is it? Check at breakfast, lunch, and dinner!

5. **Coin Hunt**: Look at some euro coins. Can you find one of each type? Sort them from **smallest value** to **biggest value**! 💶
