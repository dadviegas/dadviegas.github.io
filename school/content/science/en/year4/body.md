# The Human Body

The human body is an amazing machine! It is made up of several **systems** that work together to keep us alive and healthy.

---

## Body Systems Overview

| System | Main Function | Key Organs |
|---|---|---|
| Digestive | Breaks food down into nutrients | Mouth, oesophagus, stomach, intestines |
| Respiratory | Brings oxygen into the blood and removes CO₂ | Nose, trachea, lungs |
| Circulatory | Transports blood, nutrients and oxygen | Heart, arteries, veins, capillaries |
| Nervous | Controls and coordinates the body | Brain, spinal cord, nerves |
| Skeletal | Supports and protects the body | Bones, cartilage |
| Muscular | Enables movement | Muscles |

---

## Digestive System

The digestive system turns food into **nutrients** the body can use.

1. The **mouth** chews food and mixes it with saliva.
2. The **oesophagus** carries food down to the stomach.
3. The **stomach** continues digestion with gastric juices.
4. The **small intestine** absorbs nutrients into the blood.
5. The **large intestine** absorbs water and passes out waste.

> [!TIP]
> Digestion begins in the mouth! Chewing your food well helps your stomach work less hard.

---

## Respiratory System

We breathe about **20,000 times a day**! Air enters through the nose, travels down the trachea and reaches the **lungs**, where oxygen passes into the blood and carbon dioxide is breathed out.

> [!NOTE]
> The lungs contain millions of tiny air sacs called **alveoli**, where gas exchange takes place. Spread flat, they would cover the size of a tennis court!

---

## Circulatory System

The **heart** is the engine of the circulatory system. It beats about **70 times per minute** and pumps blood around the whole body through:

- **Arteries** — carry oxygen-rich blood from the heart to the body.
- **Veins** — return blood carrying carbon dioxide back to the heart.
- **Capillaries** — very thin vessels that connect arteries and veins.

---

## Nervous System

The **brain** is the body's control centre. It controls movement, thought, emotions and the senses. The **spinal cord** connects the brain to nerves throughout the rest of the body.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always wear a helmet when cycling — it protects the skull and brain, which cannot easily repair itself if seriously injured.

---

## Skeletal and Muscular Systems

The human **skeleton** has **206 bones** that protect internal organs and give the body its shape. **Muscles** are attached to bones by **tendons** and allow movement when they contract.

> [!NOTE]
> The smallest bone in the body is the **stirrup (stapes)** inside the ear — about 3 mm long. The largest is the **femur** (thigh bone).
