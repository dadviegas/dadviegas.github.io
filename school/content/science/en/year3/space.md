# Space & Stars

## Looking Up at the Universe!

Space is everything beyond the Earth's atmosphere — a vast, dark, mostly empty place filled with stars, planets, moons, and galaxies. It is so enormous that distances are measured in **light-years** (how far light travels in a whole year)! 🚀

---

## What Are Stars, Planets and Moons?

| Type | Characteristics | Examples |
|------|----------------|---------|
| ⭐ **Stars** | Produce their own light and heat | Sun, Sirius, Polar Star |
| 🪐 **Planets** | Orbit a star, do not produce light | Earth, Mars, Jupiter |
| 🌙 **Moons** | Orbit planets | The Moon |
| ☄️ **Comets** | Rock and ice in long orbits | Halley's Comet |
| 🪨 **Asteroids** | Rocky fragments | Asteroid Belt |

---

## The Sun — Our Star

The Sun is a **star** — an enormous ball of gas producing light and heat through nuclear fusion!

**Facts about the Sun:**
- 🔥 Surface temperature: ~5,500 °C
- 📏 Diameter: 109 times wider than Earth!
- ⏱️ Sunlight takes 8 minutes to reach Earth
- 🌟 It is the closest star to Earth
- ⚡ Without the Sun there would be no life on Earth!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never look directly at the Sun! 🚫 Ultraviolet rays can seriously damage your eyes, even through ordinary sunglasses. Use special astronomy glasses only!

## Earth — Our Planet

Earth is the **third planet** from the Sun and the only one known to have life!

### Earth's Movements

**1. Rotation** (spinning on its own axis)
- Takes **24 hours** (1 day)
- Causes **day and night** ☀️🌙
- Earth spins from West to East

**2. Revolution/Orbit** (travelling around the Sun)
- Takes **365 days** (1 year)
- Causes the **seasons** 🌸☀️🍂❄️
- The orbit is slightly oval (an ellipse)

> [!NOTE]
> Every 4 years there is a **leap year** with 366 days! That is because the revolution actually takes 365 days and **6 hours** — those extra hours add up, and every 4 years we add an extra day: 29th February! 📅

## The Moon — Our Natural Satellite

The Moon is Earth's **only natural satellite**. It has no light of its own — it reflects sunlight!

### Phases of the Moon

The Moon takes about **29 days** to orbit the Earth. Depending on its position relative to the Sun, we see different amounts lit up:

| Phase | Appearance | What we see |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| 🌑 New Moon | Not visible | Nothing (dark side facing us) |
| 🌒 Waxing Crescent | Growing | Right side illuminated |
| 🌕 Full Moon | Round | Completely illuminated |
| 🌘 Waning Crescent | Shrinking | Left side illuminated |

> [!TIP]
> **Trick to remember!** When the Moon looks like the letter **"D"** it is **D**eveloping (waxing/growing). When it looks like the letter **"C"** it is **C**rumbling (waning/shrinking)! 🌙

### Moon Facts
- The Moon always shows us the same face — because it rotates at the same speed as it orbits!
- Astronauts **Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin** were the first humans to walk on the Moon, in **1969**! 👨‍🚀

## The Solar System

The Solar System is made up of the Sun and everything that orbits around it!

### The 8 Planets

| # | Planet | Symbol | Fun Fact |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Mercury | ⚫ | Closest to the Sun and smallest planet |
| 2 | Venus | 🟡 | The hottest planet (greenhouse effect!) |
| 3 | Earth | 🔵 | The only one with life! |
| 4 | Mars | 🔴 | The red planet |
| 5 | Jupiter | 🟠 | The biggest of all |
| 6 | Saturn | 💛 | Has spectacular rings! |
| 7 | Uranus | 🔵 | Rolls on its side! |
| 8 | Neptune | 💙 | Furthest from the Sun |

> [!NOTE]
> **Pluto** was once the 9th planet, but in 2006 it was reclassified as a **dwarf planet**. Other dwarf planets include Eris, Ceres, and Haumea.

## Day and Night

Day and night are caused by the **rotation of the Earth**:

- The side facing the Sun → **day** ☀️
- The side facing away from the Sun → **night** 🌙

When it is daytime in Portugal, it is night-time on the other side of the world (such as Australia)! 🌏
