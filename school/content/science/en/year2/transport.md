# Transport

## How Do We Get Around?

Transport helps people and goods move from one place to another. There are three main types of transport depending on where they travel! 📦

---

## Types of Transport

| Type | Where they travel | Examples |
|------|------------------|---------|
| 🚗 **Land** | On the ground (roads, rails) | Car, train, bicycle |
| ✈️ **Air** | In the sky | Aeroplane, helicopter, balloon |
| 🚢 **Water** | On rivers, lakes and seas | Boat, ship, submarine |

---

## Land Transport

The most common type in everyday life!

### On the road
- 🚗 **Car** — the most common family vehicle
- 🚌 **Bus** — carries many people at once
- 🚛 **Lorry/Truck** — carries heavy goods
- 🏍️ **Motorbike** — fast but for few passengers
- 🚑 **Ambulance** — emergency medical transport
- 🚒 **Fire engine** — fights fires!

### On rails
- 🚂 **Train** — comfortable for long journeys
- 🚇 **Underground/Metro** — beneath city streets
- 🚊 **Tram** — on city streets (Lisbon has famous trams!)
- 🚟 **Cable car** — climbs mountains

### Without a motor
- 🚲 **Bicycle** — healthy and eco-friendly!
- 🛴 **Scooter**
- 🐴 **Horse and donkey** — traditional transport

> [!TIP]
> The bicycle is one of the most eco-friendly forms of transport! 🌿 It produces zero pollution and keeps us fit and healthy.

---

## Air Transport

Travels through the sky — the fastest for long distances!

- ✈️ **Aeroplane** — the fastest, for long journeys
- 🚁 **Helicopter** — can land anywhere (used in rescue!)
- 🎈 **Hot air balloon** — slow but beautiful
- ✈️ **Glider** — flies without an engine, using the wind
- 🪂 **Parachute** — for jumping from planes

> [!NOTE]
> Portugal has several airports! The main ones are in Lisbon (Humberto Delgado), Porto (Francisco Sá Carneiro), and Faro. ✈️

---

## Water Transport

Travels on rivers, lakes and oceans!

- 🚢 **Ship** — carries many people or huge cargoes
- ⛵ **Sailing boat** — uses wind to travel
- 🚤 **Speedboat** — small and fast
- ⛴️ **Ferry** — crosses rivers or sea channels
- 🛥️ **Fishing boat** — for fishermen
- 🤿 **Submarine** — travels underwater!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In Portugal, ferries cross the River Tagus (Tejo) between Lisbon and Almada or Seixal every day! 🌊

---

## Road Safety

Roads have very important rules for everyone:

### For pedestrians
- ✅ Always cross at a pedestrian crossing
- ✅ Look both ways before crossing
- ✅ Obey the traffic lights (red = stop 🔴)
- ✅ Wear a reflective vest at night
- ❌ Never use your phone while crossing the road

### For passengers
- ✅ Always wear your seatbelt
- ✅ Children use a car seat or booster seat
- ❌ Never put your head or arms out of the window
- ✅ Get in and out on the pavement side

### Road signs

| Shape | Colour | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Triangle | Red | Danger ⚠️ |
| Circle | Red | Prohibition 🚫 |
| Circle | Blue | Obligation |
| Rectangle | Blue | Information ℹ️ |

## Communication

As well as transporting people, we also transport **messages** and **information**!

| Method | How it works |
|--------|-------------|
| 📱 **Mobile phone** | Calls, messages, internet |
| 💻 **Computer** | Email, video calls, research |
| 📺 **Television** | News, entertainment |
| 📻 **Radio** | Music, news — no picture |
| 📰 **Newspaper** | Written news (paper or digital) |
| 📮 **Letter** | Written message sent by post |

> [!NOTE]
> In the past, people communicated by **letter** and it could take weeks to arrive! Today we send a message instantly to anywhere in the world. 🌍
