# The Future — Will and Going To

## Introduction

In English there are two main ways to talk about the future: **will** (for predictions and decisions made on the spot) and **going to** (for plans and intentions). Knowing when to use each one is an important skill.

---

## Will — Uses

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| **Predictions** (what we think will happen) | I think it **will** rain tomorrow. |
| **Decisions made at the moment of speaking** | "There's no milk." "I'**ll** go to the shop." |
| **Promises** | I **will** always be your friend. |
| **Offers** | I'**ll** help you with that. |
| **Threats / warnings** | If you don't study, you **will** fail. |
| **Facts about the future** | The sun **will** rise at 6:45 tomorrow. |

---

## Will — Forms

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Positive** | subject + **will** + base verb | She **will** come. / She'**ll** come. |
| **Negative** | subject + **won't** + base verb | He **won't** be late. |
| **Question** | **Will** + subject + base verb? | **Will** they win? |
| **Short answer** | Yes, they **will**. / No, they **won't**. | |

Contractions: **I'll, you'll, he'll, she'll, we'll, they'll, won't** (= will not)

---

## Going To — Uses

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| **Plans and intentions** (decided before speaking) | I**'m going to** visit Paris next summer. |
| **Predictions based on evidence** | Look at those clouds — it**'s going to** rain! |

---

## Going To — Forms

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Positive** | subject + **am/is/are going to** + base verb | She **is going to** study medicine. |
| **Negative** | subject + **am/is/are not going to** + base verb | I**'m not going to** watch that film. |
| **Question** | **Am/Is/Are** + subject + **going to** + base verb? | **Are** you **going to** travel? |
| **Short answer** | Yes, I **am**. / No, I**'m not**. | |

---

## Will vs Going To — Key Difference

| Situation | Use | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| You have a plan / intention | **going to** | I'm **going to** start a new course in September. |
| You decide at the moment of speaking | **will** | "Your bag is heavy." "I'**ll** carry it." |
| Prediction — no evidence | **will** | I think England **will** win. |
| Prediction — evidence present | **going to** | He isn't studying. He**'s going to** fail. |

> [!TIP]
> A useful test: if you can add **"I've already decided"**, use **going to**. If the decision happens as you speak, use **will**.

## Future Time Expressions

| Expression | Portuguese |
|------------|-----------|
| **tomorrow** | amanhã |
| **the day after tomorrow** | depois de amanhã |
| **next week / month / year** | na próxima semana / mês / ano |
| **in two days** | daqui a dois dias |
| **in 2030** | em 2030 |
| **soon** | em breve |
| **eventually** | eventualmente |
| **in the future** | no futuro |
| **one day** | um dia |

---

## Predictions About the Future

| Topic | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Technology | In 2050, most cars **will** be electric. |
| Climate | Temperatures **are going to** rise unless we act. |
| Work | Robots **will** do many jobs that humans do today. |
| Space | Humans **will** travel to Mars. |

## Vocabulary

```vocabulary
will | irá / vai (promessa / previsão)
going to | vai (plano / intenção)
won't | não irá / não vai
I'll | eu vou / eu irei
she'll | ela vai / ela irá
next week | na próxima semana
next year | no próximo ano
tomorrow | amanhã
soon | em breve
in the future | no futuro
prediction | previsão
plan | plano
intention | intenção
promise | promessa
offer | oferta / disponibilidade
```

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Will or going to?

Choose the correct form:

1. "The phone is ringing." "I ________ (answer) it."
2. She has booked a ticket. She ________ (fly) to London.
3. I think the team ________ (win) the championship.
4. Look! That tower ________ (fall)!
5. Don't worry — I ________ (help) you.

### Exercise 2 — Write the forms

Write positive, negative, and question forms:

1. she / go / to university (will) → ________
2. they / move / to a new house (going to) → ________

### Exercise 3 — Future predictions

Write 5 sentences predicting what the world will be like in 2050. Use both **will** and **going to**.

### Exercise 4 — My plans

Write a paragraph (6–8 sentences) about your plans for:
- Next weekend
- This summer
- After you finish school

Use **going to** for plans and **will** for spontaneous ideas or predictions.

> [!TIP]
> In spoken English, **will** and **going to** are often interchangeable for predictions. The rules above are guidelines — native speakers don't always follow them strictly!
