# Subtraction with Borrowing

When the ones digit of the top number is **smaller** than the ones digit of the bottom number, we need to **borrow** from the tens column.

---

## How borrowing works

### Example: $52 - 37$

Ones: $2 - 7$ doesn't work! 2 is smaller than 7.

**Borrow 1 ten** from the tens column. This turns the 2 into **12** and the 5 becomes **4**.

```
 4 12
 5  2   (borrow: 5→4, 2→12)
-  37
-----
   15
```

- Ones: $12 - 7 = 5$
- Tens: $4 - 3 = 1$

$$52 - 37 = 15$$

**Check:** $15 + 37 = 52$ ✓

---

## Another example: $81 - 46$

```
 7 11
 8  1   (borrow: 8→7, 1→11)
-  46
-----
   35
```

- Ones: $11 - 6 = 5$
- Tens: $7 - 4 = 3$

$$81 - 46 = 35$$

---

## Golden rule

> [!NOTE]
> **Always check your subtraction with addition:**
> $$\text{difference} + \text{subtrahend} = \text{minuend}$$
