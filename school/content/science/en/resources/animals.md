# Animal Classification

## The Animal Kingdom

There are over **8 million species** of animals on Earth! Scientists called **zoologists** study animals and organise them into groups based on their characteristics — this is called **classification**! It helps us understand how animals are related and how they live. 🔬

---

## Vertebrates vs Invertebrates

The first big division is simple: do they have a **backbone** (spine)?

| Group | Backbone? | Examples | Number of species |
|-------|----------|---------|------------------|
| 🦴 **Vertebrates** | ✅ Yes | Lion, dog, fish, eagle | ~65,000 species |
| 🐛 **Invertebrates** | ❌ No | Butterfly, octopus, worm | ~8,000,000 species! |

> [!NOTE]
> More than **97% of all animals** are invertebrates! Vertebrates are a minority — but they include all the animals we know best, including humans! 🧑

---

## Vertebrates — The 5 Classes

Vertebrates are divided into **5 main classes**:

### 1. Mammals

| Characteristic | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| 🌡️ Temperature | Warm-blooded (constant body temperature) |
| 👶 Reproduction | Viviparous (young born alive) |
| 🍼 Feeding young | Nurse with mother's milk |
| 🧥 Body covering | Fur or hair |
| 💨 Breathing | Lungs |

**Examples:** 🦁 Lion · 🐘 Elephant · 🐬 Dolphin · 🦇 Bat · 🐄 Cow · 🧑 Human

> [!TIP]
> **Cetaceans** (dolphins and whales) are mammals that live in the sea! They breathe with lungs and must come to the surface for air. 🐬

---

### 2. Birds

| Characteristic | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| 🌡️ Temperature | Warm-blooded (constant) |
| 🥚 Reproduction | Oviparous (hatch from eggs) |
| 🍼 Feeding young | Parents bring food |
| 🪶 Body covering | Feathers |
| 💨 Breathing | Lungs |

**Examples:** 🦅 Eagle · 🦉 Owl · 🐧 Penguin · 🦆 Duck · 🐦 Swallow · 🦜 Parrot

> [!NOTE]
> Not all birds can fly! **Penguins** 🐧 are birds that swim brilliantly but cannot fly. The **ostrich** is the world's largest bird — and it cannot fly either! 🏃

---

### 3. Reptiles

| Characteristic | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| 🌡️ Temperature | Cold-blooded (varies with environment) |
| 🥚 Reproduction | Oviparous (mostly) |
| 🍼 Feeding young | Independent from birth |
| 🟢 Body covering | Scales |
| 💨 Breathing | Lungs |

**Examples:** 🐍 Snake · 🦎 Lizard · 🐊 Crocodile · 🐢 Tortoise · 🦎 Chameleon

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Reptiles are **ectothermic** (cold-blooded) — they need the sun to warm up! That is why lizards bask in the sun in the morning to become active. ☀️

---

### 4. Amphibians

| Characteristic | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| 🌡️ Temperature | Cold-blooded |
| 🥚 Reproduction | Oviparous (eggs laid in water) |
| 🔄 Metamorphosis | Change form completely (tadpole → frog!) |
| 🟤 Body covering | Bare, moist skin |
| 💨 Breathing | Gills (young) and lungs (adults) |

**Examples:** 🐸 Frog · 🐸 Toad · 🦎 Salamander · Newt

**Life cycle of a frog:**
```
Egg 🥚 → Tadpole 🐟 → Tadpole with legs 🦎 → Frog 🐸
```

---

### 5. Fish

| Characteristic | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| 🌡️ Temperature | Cold-blooded |
| 🥚 Reproduction | Oviparous (mostly) |
| 🔵 Body covering | Scales |
| 💨 Breathing | Gills (breathe underwater) |
| 🌊 Habitat | Water (salt or fresh) |

**Examples:** 🐟 Sardine · 🐠 Clownfish · 🦈 Shark · 🐡 Pufferfish · 🐟 Salmon

## Vertebrate Comparison Table

| Class | Covering | Breathing | Reproduction | Blood Temp |
|-------|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| 🦁 Mammals | Fur/Hair | Lungs | Viviparous | Warm |
| 🐦 Birds | Feathers | Lungs | Oviparous | Warm |
| 🐍 Reptiles | Scales | Lungs | Oviparous | Cold |
| 🐸 Amphibians | Bare skin | Gills+Lungs | Oviparous | Cold |
| 🐟 Fish | Scales | Gills | Oviparous | Cold |

## Invertebrates — The Most Numerous!

Invertebrates have no backbone and are by far the most abundant animals on Earth!

| Group | Characteristics | Examples |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| 🦋 **Insects** | 6 legs, 3 body parts, antennae | Butterfly, bee, ant |
| 🕷️ **Arachnids** | 8 legs, 2 body parts | Spider, scorpion, mite |
| 🦀 **Crustaceans** | Hard exoskeleton, pincers | Crab, lobster, shrimp |
| 🐙 **Molluscs** | Soft body (with or without shell) | Octopus, snail, mussel |
| 🪱 **Annelids** | Segmented, cylindrical body | Earthworm, leech |
| ⭐ **Echinoderms** | 5-point symmetry, spiny skin | Starfish, sea urchin |

> [!TIP]
> **Bees** 🐝 are insects that are absolutely essential for life on Earth! Without them, flowers would not be pollinated and there would be no fruits or seeds. Bees need our protection! 🌸

## How Animals Feed

| Type | What they eat | Examples |
|------|-------------|---------|
| 🥩 **Carnivores** | Meat (other animals) | Lion, shark, eagle |
| 🥦 **Herbivores** | Plants | Cow, rabbit, elephant |
| 🍽️ **Omnivores** | Meat and plants | Human, bear, pig |
| 🍄 **Detritivores** | Dead organic matter | Earthworm, crab |

---

## How Animals Reproduce

| Type | How they are born | Examples |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| 🥚 **Oviparous** | From eggs | Birds, reptiles, fish, amphibians |
| 👶 **Viviparous** | Live young (gestation) | Mammals |
| 🥚 **Ovoviviparous** | Egg stays inside mother | Some sharks, some snakes |
