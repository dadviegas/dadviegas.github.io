# Daily Routines and the Present Simple

## Introduction

The **present simple** tense is used to describe habits, routines, and facts. In this lesson we learn vocabulary for daily activities and how to form correct present simple sentences.

## A Typical Day

**Morning:**
> I **wake up** at 7 o'clock. I **get up** and **have a shower**. Then I **brush my teeth** and **get dressed**. I **have breakfast** at 7:30. I **go to school** at 8 o'clock.

**Afternoon:**
> At school I **have lunch** at 1 pm. After school I **do my homework**.

**Evening:**
> I **have dinner** at 7 pm. I **watch TV** or **read a book**. I **go to bed** at 9 pm.

---

## Present Simple — Positive Form

| Subject | Rule | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I / You / We / They | base form | I **wake up** at 7. |
| He / She / It | add **-s** or **-es** | She **wakes** up at 8. |

Special cases:
- have → he/she/it **has**
- go → he/she/it **goes**
- do → he/she/it **does**
- watch → he/she/it **watches**

---

## Present Simple — Negative Form

Use **don't** (I/you/we/they) or **doesn't** (he/she/it) + base verb:

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|---------|
| I wake up early. | I **don't** wake up early. |
| She has breakfast. | She **doesn't** have breakfast. |
| They go to school. | They **don't** go to school. |

---

## Present Simple — Questions

Use **Do** (I/you/we/they) or **Does** (he/she/it) + base verb:

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Do** you wake up early? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| **Does** she have breakfast? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |
| **What time do** you go to school? | I go to school at 8. |
| **When does** he do homework? | He does homework at 4 pm. |

> [!TIP]
> Remember: in yes/no questions and negatives with he/she/it, the main verb stays in the **base form** — the -s goes on the auxiliary instead: *She doesn't **go*** (not *doesn't goes*).

## Time Expressions

| Expression | Portuguese |
|------------|-----------|
| **every day** | todos os dias |
| **always** | sempre |
| **usually** | normalmente |
| **often** | frequentemente |
| **sometimes** | às vezes |
| **rarely / seldom** | raramente |
| **never** | nunca |
| **in the morning** | de manhã |
| **in the afternoon** | à tarde |
| **in the evening** | à noite |

Frequency adverbs go **before the main verb** but **after "be"**:
- I **always** have breakfast.
- She is **never** late.

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Complete the sentence

Add **-s** or write the correct form where needed:

1. She ________ (wake) up at 6.
2. I ________ (have) lunch at school.
3. He ________ (go) to bed at 10.
4. They ________ (do) homework in the afternoon.

### Exercise 2 — Make it negative

1. I wake up early. → ________
2. She has breakfast at home. → ________
3. They go to school by bus. → ________

### Exercise 3 — Write questions

1. you / wake up / what time → ________
2. she / have / lunch / where → ________
3. he / do / homework / after school → ________

### Exercise 4 — My daily routine

Write a paragraph (8–10 sentences) describing your typical school day. Use frequency adverbs and time expressions.

> [!TIP]
> Notice the difference: *I wake up* (I do it myself) vs *I get up* (I physically get out of bed). You wake up first, then get up!
