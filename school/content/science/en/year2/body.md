# The Human Body

You already know the outside parts of your body. Now let's discover what is **inside**!

---

## The Skeleton

The **skeleton** is the framework of bones that supports our body.

- Adults have **206 bones**
- Bones protect important organs (the skull protects the brain)
- Bones grow with us until we are adults
- **Joints** are where bones meet — they let us move our arms, legs and neck

| Bone | Location |
|---|---|
| Skull | Head |
| Spine (vertebral column) | Back |
| Ribs | Chest |
| Femur | Thigh (the longest bone!) |
| Tibia | Lower leg |

> [!TIP]
> Milk and dairy products contain **calcium**, which makes bones strong. Eat yoghurt and drink milk for healthy bones!

---

## Muscles

**Muscles** are the tissues that allow us to move.

- We have more than **600 muscles** in our body
- Muscles work in pairs — some pull, others relax
- Exercise makes muscles stronger

> [!NOTE]
> The heart is a very special muscle — it works non-stop, day and night, pumping blood around the body!

---

## Sense Organs (Going Deeper)

| Sense | Organ | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Sight | Eyes | Light enters the eye and the brain interprets the image |
| Hearing | Ears | Vibrations in the air reach the ear and become sounds |
| Smell | Nose | Particles in the air reach the nose and are identified |
| Taste | Tongue | Taste buds recognise different flavours |
| Touch | Skin | Nerves in the skin send messages to the brain |

---

## Growth and Development

Human beings go through different stages in life.

1. **Baby** — depends on adults for everything
2. **Child** (that's you!) — learns, plays and grows fast
3. **Teenager** — the body changes a lot
4. **Adult** — growth stops
5. **Elderly person** — the body ages gradually

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To grow up healthy you need: **good sleep**, balanced meals, regular exercise and plenty of water!

## Try it!

1. **Feel your bones**: touch your wrist, your knee and your forehead — can you feel the bones under your skin?
2. **Muscles in action**: bend your arm and touch your bicep — can you feel it getting harder?
3. **One-eye test**: cover one eye and try to judge the distance to an object. Is it harder with just one eye?
4. **Growth timeline**: draw your timeline from baby to now, with important events along the way.
