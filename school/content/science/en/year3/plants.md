# Plants

Plants are fundamental to life on Earth — they produce the oxygen we breathe and form the base of almost every food chain!

---

## Photosynthesis

**Photosynthesis** is the process by which plants make their own food.

**What the plant needs:**
- Sunlight
- Water (absorbed by the roots)
- Carbon dioxide — CO₂ (from the air)

**What the plant produces:**
- Glucose (food)
- Oxygen — O₂ (released into the air)

$$\text{Light} + \text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Glucose} + \text{O}_2$$

> [!NOTE]
> Photosynthesis mainly takes place in **green leaves**, which contain a substance called **chlorophyll** — this is what gives plants their green colour!

---

## How Plants Reproduce

Plants can reproduce in several ways.

**Reproduction with seeds:**
1. The flower is pollinated (by wind or insects)
2. A fruit forms containing seeds
3. Seeds are dispersed (by wind, animals or water)
4. The seed germinates and a new plant grows

**Reproduction without seeds:**
- Cuttings (cut a stem and plant it)
- Bulbs (tulip, onion)
- Spores (ferns and mosses)

> [!TIP]
> Bees are essential pollinators! When they land on flowers to collect nectar, they carry pollen from flower to flower.

---

## The Usefulness of Plants

Plants are useful in so many ways!

| Use | Examples |
|---|---|
| Food | Fruits, vegetables, cereals, legumes |
| Medicine | Many medicines come from plants |
| Timber and paper | Eucalyptus, pine |
| Textiles | Cotton, linen |
| Oxygen | All plants release O₂ |
| Shade and cooling | Trees in cities provide shade |

---

## Endangered Plants

Many plants are at risk of disappearing!

**Causes:**
- Deforestation (cutting down forests)
- Forest fires
- Pollution of soil and water
- Climate change

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Portugal has several **endemic plants** — plants that exist nowhere else on Earth! We need to protect them. Never pick wild plants!

**Examples of protected plants in Portugal:**
- Viola de Sagres (found only at Cape St Vincent)
- Cork oak — the national tree of Portugal
- Holm oak

## Try it!

1. **Photosynthesis experiment**: place a green leaf in a glass of water in bright sunlight. With a magnifying glass, observe the tiny oxygen bubbles that appear.
2. **Germinate a seed**: place bean seeds on damp cotton wool. Observe the germination over several days.
3. **Plants in daily life**: look around your home and count how many products come from plants (clothing, food, furniture...).
4. **Grow a bulb**: place an onion bulb in a glass with a little water and watch the roots grow.
