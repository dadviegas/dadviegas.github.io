# Portugal — Our Country

Portugal is a country located in the south-west of the **Iberian Peninsula**, in Europe. It is one of the oldest countries in Europe, with borders that have remained almost unchanged for over 800 years.

---

## Geographical Location

- **Continent:** Europe
- **Peninsula:** Iberian (shared with Spain)
- **Borders:** To the north and east with **Spain**; to the south and west with the **Atlantic Ocean**
- **Capital:** Lisbon
- **Area:** approximately 92,000 km² (mainland + islands)

Portugal also includes the **Autonomous Regions of the Azores and Madeira**, archipelagos located in the Atlantic Ocean.

> [!NOTE]
> Portugal is the most **westerly** country in continental Europe, which made it a natural starting point for the great voyages of exploration during the Age of Discoveries.

---

## Regions of Mainland Portugal

| Region | Key Features |
|---|---|
| Minho & Douro Litoral | Green and rainy north, vinho verde and Douro wines |
| Trás-os-Montes & Alto Douro | Mountainous interior, continental climate |
| Beira Litoral | Atlantic coast, pine forests, Aveiro and Coimbra |
| Beira Interior | Plateau and mountain ranges, cold interior |
| Estremadura & Ribatejo | Fertile plains, Lisbon, Tagus valley |
| Alentejo | Vast plains, cork oak and holm oak, wheat fields |
| Algarve | South, warm Mediterranean climate, famous beaches |

---

## Relief — Mountain Ranges and Rivers

### Main Mountain Ranges (Serras)

- **Serra da Estrela** — highest point on the mainland (1,993 m), only ski resort
- **Serra de Sintra** — near Lisbon, UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Serra do Gerês** — National Park (the only one in mainland Portugal)
- **Serra de Monchique** — in the Algarve

### Main Rivers

| River | Source | Mouth | Major Cities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tagus (Tejo) | Spain | Atlantic Ocean (Lisbon) | Lisbon, Santarém |
| Douro | Spain | Atlantic Ocean (Porto) | Porto, Peso da Régua |
| Minho | Spain | Atlantic Ocean | Viana do Castelo |
| Guadiana | Spain | Atlantic Ocean (Algarve) | Évora, Beja |
| Mondego | Serra da Estrela | Atlantic Ocean | Coimbra |

> [!TIP]
> The **Tagus**, **Douro**, **Minho** and **Guadiana** are **international rivers** — they rise in Spain and flow through Portugal to the sea.

---

## Climate

Portugal mainly has a **Mediterranean climate** with Atlantic influence:

- **Hot, dry summers**
- **Mild, wet winters**
- The **north** is rainier and cooler; the **south (Algarve)** is hotter and drier
- **Altitude** affects climate in the mountain ranges (colder, with snow in winter)

---

## Population

- **Population:** approximately 10 million inhabitants
- **Capital and largest city:** Lisbon
- **Official language:** Portuguese
- **Currency:** Euro (€)
- Most of the population lives in **coastal cities** (Lisbon and Porto together account for a large share of the total)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Portuguese is spoken by around **260 million people** worldwide, in countries such as Brazil, Angola, Mozambique, Cape Verde and East Timor — a direct result of Portugal's maritime expansion.
