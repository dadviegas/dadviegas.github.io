# Reading & Writing — Year 3

## Reading Strategies

Good readers use strategies **before**, **during**, and **after** reading.

| When | Strategy |
|------|---------|
| **Before** | Look at the title and images; predict the topic |
| **Before** | Think about what you already know about the subject |
| **During** | Underline the most important ideas |
| **During** | Work out unknown words from context |
| **After** | Summarise the text in your own words |
| **After** | Give your opinion about what you read |

> [!TIP]
> If you find an unknown word, read the whole sentence first — the context usually helps you work out the meaning!

---

## How to Write a Summary (Resumo)

A **summary** (*resumo*) retells the most important ideas of a text in a short, clear form.

**Steps to summarise:**
1. Read the text carefully.
2. Identify the main characters and topic.
3. Select the key ideas (beginning, middle, end).
4. Write the summary **in your own words** — don't copy whole sentences.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A summary does **not** include every detail — only the essentials. It should be much shorter than the original text!

## Types of Reading

| Type | Portuguese | How | Purpose |
|------|------------|-----|---------|
| Silent reading | *Leitura silenciosa* | Read with your eyes only | Personal understanding |
| Reading aloud | *Leitura em voz alta* | Read clearly for others | Share the text |
| Expressive reading | *Leitura expressiva* | Read with tone and emotion | Drama, poetry |

---

## Writing Different Types of Text

This year you will write a variety of texts:

| Text type | Portuguese | What it does |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Narrative | *Texto narrativo* | tells a story |
| Descriptive | *Texto descritivo* | describes a person, place, or object |
| Instructional | *Texto instrucional* | explains how to do something |
| Poetic | *Texto poético* | uses creative language with rhythm/rhyme |

> [!NOTE]
> Before writing, **plan** your text: jot down your ideas, write a draft, then revise it. Good writing takes time!

---

## Revising Your Writing

When you finish writing, check:

- [ ] Does every sentence start with a capital letter and end with a full stop?
- [ ] Are all words spelled correctly?
- [ ] Does the text have a beginning, middle, and end?
- [ ] Are the ideas organised and clear?
