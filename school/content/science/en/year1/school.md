# My School

School is a place where we learn, play and make friends. Let's get to know it better!

---

## Spaces at School

A school has many different spaces, each with its own purpose.

| Space | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Classroom | Learning, reading, writing and maths |
| Library | Reading books and doing research |
| Playground | Playing and resting |
| Gym / Sports hall | Physical education |
| Canteen / Dining hall | Eating lunch |
| Toilets | Personal hygiene |
| School office | Administrative tasks |

> [!NOTE]
> Each space at school has its own rules. In the library we speak quietly; on the playground we can run and play!

---

## Classroom Rules

Rules help everyone learn better and respect each other.

- Arrive on time and be ready to learn
- Listen carefully when the teacher speaks
- Ask to speak before talking — raise your hand!
- Respect your classmates and do not interrupt
- Take care of classroom materials
- Tidy up your space at the end of the lesson

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Rules are not there to be difficult — they are so that everyone **feels comfortable and can learn** in the classroom!

---

## Jobs at School

Many people work at school to keep everything running smoothly.

| Job | What they do |
|---|---|
| Teacher | Teaches lessons and looks after pupils |
| Headteacher / Principal | Organises and leads the school |
| Teaching assistant | Helps pupils and supervises spaces |
| Cook | Prepares the meals |
| Cleaner | Keeps the school clean and tidy |

> [!TIP]
> Say "good morning" and "thank you" to everyone who works at your school. It makes everyone feel good!

## Try it! 🎯

1. **School map**: draw a map of your school with the main spaces labelled.
2. **My favourite rules**: choose two classroom rules and explain in your own words why they are important.
3. **Meet someone new**: find out the name of someone who works at your school that you do not know well yet.
4. **Memory game**: close your eyes and try to remember five spaces in your school. How many can you name?
