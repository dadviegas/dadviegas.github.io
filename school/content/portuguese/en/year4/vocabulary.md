# Vocabulary

Building a rich vocabulary helps us read, write, and communicate better. In Year 4 we study word relationships and word families.

---

## Synonyms (*Sinónimos*)

Words with **similar meanings**.

| Word | Synonyms |
|---|---|
| *bonito* | *belo*, *lindo*, *formoso* |
| *correr* | *caminhar depressa*, *apressar-se* |
| *feliz* | *contente*, *alegre*, *satisfeito* |
| *grande* | *enorme*, *vasto*, *imenso* |

> [!TIP]
> Synonyms are not always interchangeable — context matters! *Grande* and *enorme* both mean big, but *enorme* is stronger.

---

## Antonyms (*Antónimos*)

Words with **opposite meanings**.

| Word | Antonym |
|---|---|
| *quente* | *frio* |
| *alto* | *baixo* |
| *abrir* | *fechar* |
| *alegre* | *triste* |
| *claro* | *escuro* |

---

## Homonyms (*Homónimos*)

Words that are **spelled and/or sound the same** but have different meanings.

| Word | Meaning 1 | Meaning 2 |
|---|---|---|
| *manga* | fruit (mango) | sleeve (of a shirt) |
| *banco* | bench | bank (financial) |
| *são* | healthy | they are (*ser*) |

---

## Word Families (*Família de Palavras*)

Words that share the same **root** (*radical*).

Root: **mar** (sea)
- *mar*, *marinho*, *marinheiro*, *maré*, *submarino*

Root: **fogo** (fire)
- *fogo*, *fogão*, *fogueira*, *foguete*, *incêndio*

---

## Lexical Field (*Campo Lexical*)

Words that belong to the same **topic or theme**.

**Lexical field of school:** *caderno*, *lápis*, *professor*, *quadro*, *aula*, *recreio*, *mochila*

**Lexical field of food:** *fruta*, *legumes*, *cozinhar*, *refeição*, *receita*, *prato*

---

## Prefixes and Suffixes

| Prefix/Suffix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *in-* / *im-* | not | *infeliz*, *impossível* |
| *re-* | again | *refazer*, *reler* |
| *-eiro/-eira* | person/job | *padeiro*, *costureira* |
| *-mente* | makes an adverb | *rapidamente*, *felizmente* |
