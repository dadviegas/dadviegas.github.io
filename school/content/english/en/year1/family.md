# Family Members

## Introduction

Every family is different, but the words we use to describe family members are the same! Let's learn how to talk about our families in English.

---

## Immediate Family

| English | Portuguese |
|---------|-----------|
| **mother / mum** | mãe |
| **father / dad** | pai |
| **sister** | irmã |
| **brother** | irmão |
| **parents** | pais |
| **child / children** | filho/filha / filhos |
| **baby** | bebé |

> [!TIP]
> **Mum** (British English) and **Mom** (American English) both mean mother. **Dad** is informal for father.

## Talking About Your Family

| English | Portuguese |
|---------|-----------|
| This is my **mother**. | Esta é a minha mãe. |
| I have a **brother**. | Eu tenho um irmão. |
| I don't have a **sister**. | Eu não tenho irmã. |
| My **grandma** is kind. | A minha avó é carinhosa. |
| My **dad** is tall. | O meu pai é alto. |
| I have two **cousins**. | Eu tenho dois primos. |

---

## Family Tree Example

```
          Grandpa — Grandma
               |
         ┌─────┴─────┐
        Dad         Uncle
         |
   ┌─────┴─────┐
  Me        Sister
```

> [!TIP]
> The word **family** can also include pets! Some people say "my dog is part of the family."

---

## Possessive Adjectives with Family

| Pronoun | Possessive | Example |
|---------|------------|---------|
| I | **my** | **My** mum is kind. |
| you | **your** | Is **your** dad tall? |
| he | **his** | **His** sister is funny. |
| she | **her** | **Her** brother is young. |

---

## Describing Family Members

| English | Portuguese |
|---------|-----------|
| tall | alto/a |
| short | baixo/a |
| young | jovem / novo/a |
| old | velho/a / idoso/a |
| kind | carinhoso/a |
| funny | engraçado/a |
| clever | inteligente |

Example sentences:
- **My mum is kind and clever.**
- **My grandpa is old but funny.**
- **My brother is tall and young.**

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Who is it?

Read the description and write the family word:

1. Your mum's mother is your ________.
2. Your dad's brother is your ________.
3. Your aunt's child is your ________.
4. Your parents' parents are your ________.

### Exercise 2 — Write sentences

Use the words in brackets to write a sentence:

1. (my / is / dad / tall) → ________
2. (have / a / I / sister) → ________
3. (grandma / my / kind / is) → ________

### Exercise 3 — My family

Write a short paragraph (4–6 sentences) describing your family. Include:
- How many people are in your family
- Names and relationships
- One adjective for each person
