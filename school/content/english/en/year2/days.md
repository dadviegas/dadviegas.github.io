# Days, Months, and Seasons

## Introduction

Time expressions are fundamental in English. In this lesson we learn the days of the week, the months of the year, the four seasons, and how to talk about dates.

---

## Days of the Week

| Day | Portuguese | Abbreviation |
|-----|-----------|-------------|
| **Monday** | segunda-feira | Mon |
| **Tuesday** | terça-feira | Tue |
| **Wednesday** | quarta-feira | Wed |
| **Thursday** | quinta-feira | Thu |
| **Friday** | sexta-feira | Fri |
| **Saturday** | sábado | Sat |
| **Sunday** | domingo | Sun |

> [!TIP]
> In English, days of the week and months **always start with a capital letter**! ✓ Monday ✗ monday

---

| Month | Portuguese | Season (UK/Northern hemisphere) |

| **June** | junho | Summer |
| **July** | julho | Summer |
| **August** | agosto | Summer |

| **October** | outubro | Autumn |
| **November** | novembro | Autumn |

---

## The Four Seasons

| Season | Portuguese | Months (UK) |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Spring** | Primavera | March, April, May |
| **Summer** | Verão | June, July, August |
| **Autumn / Fall** | Outono | September, October, November |
| **Winter** | Inverno | December, January, February |

> [!TIP]
> British English says **Autumn**, American English says **Fall**. Both are correct!

---

## Talking About Days and Dates

| Expression | Portuguese | Example |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| **today** | hoje | Today is Monday. |
| **yesterday** | ontem | Yesterday was Sunday. |
| **tomorrow** | amanhã | Tomorrow is Tuesday. |
| **this week** | esta semana | This week is busy. |
| **next week** | na próxima semana | Next week is Christmas. |
| **last week** | na semana passada | Last week was great. |
| **on Monday** | na segunda-feira | I have PE on Monday. |
| **in January** | em janeiro | My birthday is in January. |
| **in summer** | no verão | We swim in summer. |

---

## Asking About Dates

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **What day is it today?** | It's Monday. |
| **What is the date today?** | It's the 15th of March. |
| **What month is it?** | It's April. |
| **What season is it?** | It's spring. |
| **When is your birthday?** | My birthday is in July. |

---

## Ordinal Numbers for Dates

| Number | Ordinal | Abbreviation |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | **first** | 1st |
| 2 | **second** | 2nd |
| 3 | **third** | 3rd |
| 4 | **fourth** | 4th |
| 5 | **fifth** | 5th |
| 10 | **tenth** | 10th |
| 15 | **fifteenth** | 15th |
| 20 | **twentieth** | 20th |
| 21 | **twenty-first** | 21st |

Example: **The 3rd of May** = May 3rd

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Order the days

Put the days in the correct order starting from Monday:

*Sunday / Wednesday / Monday / Friday / Tuesday / Saturday / Thursday*

### Exercise 2 — What season?

Write the season:

2. June, July, August → ________

### Exercise 3 — Fill in the blanks

1. ________ is the first day of the work week.
2. ________ and ________ are the weekend days.
3. My birthday is in ________.
4. Christmas is in ________.
5. Today is ________.

### Exercise 4 — Write the date

1. 1/1 → the ________ of ________
2. 14/2 → ________
3. 25/12 → ________

> [!TIP]
> Keep a small diary in English — write today's date and one sentence about your day each morning. This practises dates and past tense at the same time!
