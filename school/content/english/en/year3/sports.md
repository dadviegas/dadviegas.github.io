# Sports and Physical Activities

## Introduction

Sport is a fantastic topic for English conversation. Whether you play sport, watch it, or just enjoy talking about it, knowing the right vocabulary will help you express your interests confidently.

## Individual Sports

| Sport | Portuguese | Verb |
|-------|-----------|------|
| **swimming** | natação | go / do |
| **running / athletics** | corrida / atletismo | go / do |
| **cycling** | ciclismo | go / do |
| **gymnastics** | ginástica | do |
| **tennis** | ténis | play |
| **golf** | golfe | play |
| **martial arts** | artes marciais | do |
| **skiing** | esqui | go |
| **surfing** | surf | go |

> [!TIP]
> Use **play** with ball games, **go** with activities ending in -ing, and **do** with individual disciplines:
> - I **play** football.
> - I **go** swimming.
> - I **do** gymnastics.

## Expressing Likes and Dislikes About Sport

| Expression | Portuguese |
|------------|-----------|
| **I like swimming.** | Eu gosto de natação. |
| **I love playing football.** | Eu adoro jogar futebol. |
| **I enjoy cycling.** | Eu gosto de andar de bicicleta. |
| **I don't like rugby.** | Eu não gosto de râguebi. |
| **I hate running.** | Eu detesto correr. |
| **I'm good at tennis.** | Sou bom/boa a ténis. |
| **I'm not very good at golf.** | Não sou muito bom/boa a golfe. |
| **My favorite sport is …** | O meu desporto favorito é … |

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Play, go, or do?

Fill in the correct verb:

1. She ________ basketball every weekend.
2. He ________ swimming on Tuesdays.
3. They ________ martial arts at the sports centre.
4. We ________ tennis in the park.
5. I ________ cycling with my family.

### Exercise 2 — Likes and dislikes

Rewrite using the correct expression:

1. Football ✓✓ (love) → I ________
2. Running ✗ (don't like) → ________
3. Tennis ✓ (like) → ________
4. Gymnastics ✗✗ (hate) → ________

### Exercise 3 — Sports quiz

Answer in full sentences:

1. Which sport is played with an oval ball?
2. Which sport uses a racket and a net?
3. Where do football matches take place?

### Exercise 4 — My sports profile

Write a paragraph (6–8 sentences) about your sporting life:
- Which sports you play / do / go
- Which sports you like and dislike
- Your favourite sport and why
- A sport you would like to try

> [!TIP]
> When you watch a sport on TV, try to name the positions, actions, and equipment in English. You'll be surprised how much you already know!
