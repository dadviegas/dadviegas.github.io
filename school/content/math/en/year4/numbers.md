## Reading large numbers

In the number **347 852**:

| Digit | Position           | Value         |
|-------|--------------------|---------------|
| 3     | Hundred thousands  | $300\,000$   |
| 4     | Ten thousands      | $40\,000$    |
| 7     | Thousands          | $7\,000$     |
| 8     | Hundreds           | $800$         |
| 5     | Tens               | $50$          |
| 2     | Ones               | $2$           |

$$347\,852 = 300\,000 + 40\,000 + 7\,000 + 800 + 50 + 2$$

## Rounding

**Round to the nearest ten:**

$$47 \approx 50 \qquad 43 \approx 40$$

**Round to the nearest hundred:**

$$347 \approx 300 \qquad 382 \approx 400$$

> [!TIP]
> If the next digit is $\geq 5$, round up. If $< 5$, round down.
